## Placement decisions

The inherited method and structural inclusion/nesting arguments precede experiments. Matched head trajectories then demonstrate that a head can improve evolution despite being contained in the full span. Same-previous-state probes and physically normalized tangent diagnostics explain how the tested residual can favor physically inaccurate directions. Finite-budget nonlinear fit distances remain best-found bounds; nonstationary fits do not qualify an optimal distance.

The Burgers section first fixes representation and increases tests, then screens larger ordered prefixes and checks their trajectories. The wider-prefix result is a dense linear-span discrete-upwind study at fixed time step. It cannot be called nonlinear-head performance, sparse deployment, or continuum convergence. Common-test widening and joint width/test changes need separate labels. Transient regressions belong in the main argument rather than being hidden behind improving maximum-error summaries.

Quadrature belongs in a separate operator-labeled section. The current discrete upwind target, historical analytic derivatives and continuous/hybrid weak variants are not interchangeable. Frozen-rule primary space-and-time versus secondary space-only decisions must remain distinct. The main sources are `worktrees/2026-11-15-jcp-sealed-closeout/experiments/jcp-sealed-closeout/results/four-rule-decisions.json` and `reports/2026-11-05-quadrature-per-case/data.json`. Mesh-transfer and storage evidence distinguishes fixed learned weights from rebuilt normalization, tests and operators. Requested output work can grow even if reduced evolution is flat.

Complete-query cost closes the empirical argument, using the final same-allocation panel and all qualified target settings. The criterion is cohort median-and-worst accuracy, not per-case error equality. The cheapest head is near-tied with POD; every measured head loses every casewise comparison to its eligible coarse classical comparator. Same-grid advantages remain valid under that restriction. Cost accuracy is evaluated at requested evolved output times, head mechanism uses its trajectory history, and width accuracy includes initialization; headline percentages must not be directly equated.

## Glossary

**Placement** means the manuscript section where evidence supports an argument. **Scope** names the population, model, operator and metric for which that evidence is valid. **Dense** evaluates the selected discrete equations without the proposed sparse sampling approximation. **Frozen** means unchanged during the stated comparison. **Qualification** means passing the numerical or measurement conditions registered for that study.
