\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Single seed, single checkpoint.} Every number rests on one training
seed and one checkpoint per PDE. The three-seed repetition and the
sealed-cohort evaluation (Tables~\ref{tab:seeds}, \ref{tab:sealed}) are
running; that lane has recorded an amendment to its fidelity gate, from
$10^{-9}$ to $10^{-3}$ relative, made before any of its numbers were read.
\textbf{Development cohort.} Burgers numbers are on six opened cases (32
held-out for the tuning study), wave and Poisson on eight and twelve; the
L-shape cell shows the tail this understates (worst error on 461 validation
sources is two to three times the worst on its 32 development sources).
\textbf{2D only.}
\textbf{No cold-start SMA-NM-ROM comparison}, which reviewer 5mgh asked for;
the classical comparison is POD-LSPG through our own solver.
\textbf{Operator numbers are lower bounds}: $\nOpLaneImproving$
U-Net/Transolver arms and $\nOpFnoImproving$ FNO arms were still improving at
their wall budget, and none was tuned per family; a larger budget beats the
ROM by more.
\textbf{Quadrature certification is one draw}: a second draw at $q=64$ moved
$\rho_{\max}$ from $\nEqtopQsixtyFourArchivedRho$ to
$\nEqtopQsixtyFourRedrawRho$ and flipped the flag; until job
$\nEqtopPendingJob$ lands the EQ ladder is ``certified in this draw''.
\textbf{The headline ladder quotes a dearer baseline}: the fixed-$M$ $q=0$
rung costs $\nQxmFixedQzeroMs$\,ms where the scheduled $M=4(K+q)$ rung costs
$\nQxmScheduledQzeroMs$\,ms (different jobs, accuracy identical to $10^{-9}$).
\textbf{Pending cells}: the $1024^2$ panel, the L-shape solve layer, the
Navier--Stokes reduced model, and the operator resolution knob, which is
binding---if a trained operator exposes its own accuracy--cost family through
evaluation resolution, ``one point per trained operator'' is withdrawn.
\textbf{No theory}: no convergence guarantee for the cold-start iteration, no
bound on the quadrature error on unseen states, no continuum error bound.
