Question

What is the reduced model, in one line?

The solution is a short sum of learned shapes

u(x, t) ≈ c₁(t)·g₁(x) + c₂(t)·g₂(x) + … + c_r(t)·g_r(x)

The shapes g(x)

Come from one trained network. They are functions of position, so they can be evaluated anywhere, on any mesh.

The numbers c(t)

Are what the solver computes each time step. There are only r of them, against millions of mesh values.

The dial r

Picks how many shapes to use, from 32 up to 512 (2D) or 1024 (3D), with one trained network.

Method summary · 7

Everything else in the deck is about how the shapes are made and ordered, and how the numbers c are computed each step.
