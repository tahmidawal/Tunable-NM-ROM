- **No tuning, one seed, one size per family.** Configurations come from heat-compare-hires / ops-tune-grid; at 4096² the smallest existing size is used. The operator numbers are "good enough", not the best these families can do. Most networks were still improving when the 3000 s budget ran out. At 2048² and above an epoch over the 2611-draw square training set takes most of the budget: FNO, U-Net and DeepONet completed 1–4 epochs. Their high errors mainly show under-training within a fixed wall budget. They say little about what these architectures can reach.
- **Co-scheduling (DESIGN A1).** The account's QOS cap (~12 GPUs shared by four groups, later 2 for this group) forced several networks onto one GPU. L-shape 256² and 512² trained all four families on one GPU. At 1024² the pairs were FNO+Transolver and U-Net+DeepONet, and at 2048² Transolver+DeepONet. Each network kept the full 3000 s wall budget but got only a share of the GPU, so it completed fewer epochs. FNO at L-shape 512² and at 1024² completed only 2 epochs. Square 256² and every single-network job had a dedicated GPU. `co_scheduled_on_same_gpu` is recorded per network (marked ᶜ in the table).
- **No H200 was available (DESIGN A2).** Every H200 was allocated, with Slurm start estimates of 21:40 and 09-26. So the 4096² networks, FNO at 2048² and the 4096² panel ran on 80 GB A100/H100 cards, not the H200 the brief asked for. At 4096², FNO and DeepONet ran out of memory at micro-batch 1 on an 80 GB card. U-Net-small trained (1 epoch), but its query ran out of memory in the panel next to the 69 GB NM-ROM bank. Only Transolver-small has a 4096² number. These are out-of-memory results on 80 GB cards, not on a 141 GB H200.
- **FNO at 2048² (DESIGN A4)** trains with activation checkpointing of each FNO block, because it does not fit an 80 GB card otherwise (`s2048f` out of memory, kept). The arithmetic is identical (checked: gradients equal to 0.0); only the per-step time is higher.
- **Micro-batch probe leak (DESIGN A3).** Before the fix, a failed large-batch probe left memory allocated. Networks trained before the fix (256²–1024²) may therefore have used a smaller micro-batch than necessary. Gradient accumulation keeps the effective batch at 8, so the cost is fewer epochs, not a different objective.
- **L-shape training-set subset (DESIGN A5).** L-shape targets need one CG solve per draw, so the networks used the first 2611 (256², 512²), 1024 (1024²) or 256 (2048²) draws of the NM-ROM's 2611-draw fit split. Square networks see all 2611 draws (exact DST per batch).
- **Training GPUs differ** (A100 80GB PCIe or H100 PCIe, whichever the queue gave). Epochs within 3000 s depend on the card; the card is recorded per network.
- **Panel timing** is A–B–A with 3 repetitions per A phase (the source lanes used 5), 2 CG repetitions, and 1 at 4096², where the CG grid stops at rtol 1e-2. Absolute milliseconds are on this job's GPU and differ from Table 1's. Every ratio is same-job.
- **Gates.** In every panel the NM-ROM worst errors reproduce the Table 1 values (relative difference ≤ 3e-16), CG converged, NM-ROM/CG fields are deterministic and the device UUID did not change. Drift gate (A2/A1 median within 1.10): **failed at L-shape 256²** for the NM-ROM fast arm `R64_linear` (ratio 0.85, a 0.3 ms arm). That cell is `complete=false` in `cost_points.json`. Its numbers are reported but the fast-arm timing there is not gate-clean.
- **Operator precision.** U-Net, Transolver and DeepONet are float32 networks and FNO is float64, as in the heat/Burgers panels. Operator outputs may not be bit-reproducible across repetitions; the maximum relative deviation is recorded.
- **Cohorts.** These are the Table 1 development cohorts (12 square sources, 32 L-shape sources), and nothing was selected on them. Checkpoints were chosen on 16 draws of the NM-ROM validation split.
- **Superseded or cancelled attempts** (kept under `runs/`, never used for numbers):
  - l256f 4286516: cancelled while pending.
  - s256p 4290446: import error, rerun as s256q.
  - s4096f 4290709 and s4096u 4290832: waited for an H200, cancelled while pending.
  - s2048u 4292114, s4096ua 4292146 and s4096t 4292219: cancelled because of the probe leak.
  - s4096fa 4292145: leaky probe, superseded by s4096fb.
  - s2048f 4292408: out of memory without checkpointing.
- **Coordinator one-network-per-GPU rule (DESIGN A6).** It arrived after the co-scheduled jobs had run. Only the square 1024² cell could be rerun with one network per GPU (`s1024r`, panel 4306883). `cost_points.json` uses `s1024r`, and `results.json`/REPORT keep both rows. On the rerun every family is more accurate than its co-scheduled twin: U-Net 8.45 → 4.60 %, DeepONet 24.9 → 16.3 %, Transolver 45.3 → 35.6 %, FNO 11.7 → 8.7 %. So the ᶜ rows understate the operators.
- **L-shape 2048² panel (DESIGN A7–A9).** The NM-ROM state needs most of an 80 GB card, and the Table 1 job had used an H200. FNO (trained, 4 epochs) and DeepONet (trained, 9 epochs) therefore could not run their query beside it and are reported as query out-of-memory. The panel of record is `l2048r` (4303797). The earlier and later attempts (4301745, 4303032, 4305415) ran out of JAX memory during the NM-ROM build and have no numbers.
- **Timing differs from Table 1's absolute values** where the source job used another card (L-shape 2048²: Table 1 on an H200, here on an A100; accurate 68.6× vs Table 1's 84.2×). Every ratio in this report is same-job.
