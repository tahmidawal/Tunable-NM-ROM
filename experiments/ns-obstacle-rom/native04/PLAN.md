# Candidate fine P4 metric capacity probe

This is a single capacity-control allocation using exact retained P4/g4 h3 mesh, layout,
configuration and result bytes. They are candidate data, not admitted training truth.
The probe verifies the actual native layout and counts the complete original diagnostic
rule. It assembles precisely the dimensionless H1 and L2 forms used by the adapter in
V, Q and the matching scalar velocity space, retaining all six CSC matrices and free
index arrays. Actual nnz, bytes, hashes and per-phase high-water RSS are recorded. The
existing production factor estimates are evaluated on actual free submatrices, without
performing any LU factorization or eigenproblem. No field/training export, fitting,
source cap mutation, G2 admission or automatic retry occurs.

One fresh native-capacity04 directory, one GPU on gpu partition, sixteen CPUs, 64 GiB
allocation and twenty minutes. The probe itself guards sixteen GiB process RSS plus
conservative sparse conversion workspaces, thirty million entries per matrix, one GiB
per numerical array and three GiB total results. These are separately reviewed probe
bounds, not modifications of production limits. Full input/source/output hashes, sparse
matrices, failures and dependency evidence must be retained; maintain ten GiB local disk
reserve during collection and durable retention. Production capacity decisions follow
independent inspection of these measured matrices and remain separate from G2 gates.
