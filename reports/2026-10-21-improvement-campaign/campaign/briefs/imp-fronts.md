## Phase B (wave 2, after release): three single-change retrains vs a control retrain
Control = retrain of the incumbent recipe (same adaptive rule and steps). Arms, each a single change:
- (a) **data fidelity** — stop if the N0s floor is not ≥ 3× below the control's;
- (b) **steep / low-ν coverage** — stop if the C1 ROM median is not ≥ 2× lower;
- (c) **boundary-compatible bank** — stop if $m^*$ is not ≥ 2× smaller **and** the tail not ≥ 3× smaller.
Every arm also stops if the in-range worst floor is more than 2× the control's. Score on fresh in-range, N0s,
C1 and C2 cases (fresh = newly generated by you from new seeds, dev/val/test-once; never the sealed/OOD cohorts).
Stop rules verbatim in DESIGN.
