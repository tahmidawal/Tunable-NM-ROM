## Glossary

- **Span R′ / head K**: a solve in the first R′ columns of the importance-ordered bank (linear), or in K latent coordinates through the nonlinear head.
- **`fsc` / `fsh`**: the fixed-sweep solver (adaptive LM on the first 3 steps, then one LM sweep per step) for the span (with the cached predictor) and for the head.
- **dt**: the reduced model's backward-Euler step (the reference uses 0.005).
- **worst / median %**: over cases, the largest relative error at t = 0.05…0.25, normalised by the initial field.
- **ms**: median GPU time of one query (dense initial field on the GPU → six dense output fields).
- **FOM**: Newton–BiCGStab with the exact Helmholtz preconditioner, named by its step, Newton tolerance and linear tolerance.
- **Paper-rule speedup / own-matched speedup**: FOM time over the setting's time, with the FOM matched to the accurate setting / to that setting.
- **ρ**: the tensor rule's relative error in the tested advection on reached states; confirmed if ≤ 0.116 on every certification draw.
- **Validation / held-out**: 64 cases used to choose settings / 32 sealed cases opened once.
