# JCP literature notes

Research completed November 18, 2026 by the read-only `jcp_literature` researcher. Publication metadata was checked against primary records; detailed analyses were read in author manuscripts. Publisher access was incomplete, and manuscript section/figure numbering can differ from the journal version. This is a targeted methodological selection, not an exhaustive review or an independent numerical reproduction.

| Paper | Verified publication | Inspected evidence relevant to Tunable |
|---|---|---|
| Lee and Carlberg, *Model reduction of dynamical systems on nonlinear manifolds using deep convolutional autoencoders* | JCP 404, 108973 (2020), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2019.108973), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/1812.08373v3) | Section 4 derives manifold Galerkin/LSPG properties and discrete error bounds. | Kim, Choi, Widemann and Zohdi, *A fast and accurate physics-informed neural network reduced order model with shallow masked autoencoder* | JCP 451, 110841 (2022), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2021.110841), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2009.11990) | Section 4.4 counts sampled decoder/Jacobian and stencil dependencies; Section 5 treats hyper-reduced error; Section 6 varies model size, samples and data. | Barnett and Farhat, *Quadratic approximation manifold for mitigating the Kolmogorov barrier in nonlinear projection-based model order reduction* | JCP 464, 111348 (2022), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2022.111348), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2204.02462) | Section 5 compares affine/quadratic hyper-reduced Ahmed-body models; forces and velocity histories supplement state errors. | Chen, Chiaramonte, Grinspun and Carlberg, *Model reduction for the material point method via an implicit neural representation of the deformation map* | JCP 478, 111908 (2023), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2023.111908), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.12390) | Section 5.1 isolates gradient penalty, latent dimension and sampling. | Romor, Stabile and Rozza, *Explicable hyper-reduced order models on nonlinearly approximated solution manifolds of compressible and incompressible Navier–Stokes equations* | JCP 524, 113729 (2025), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2025.113729), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.03396) | Section 5 and Figures 9–11 compare reconstruction, evolved fields, sampling and interpolation/extrapolation. | Puri, Prakash, Kara and Zhang, *SNF-ROM: Projection-based nonlinear reduced order modeling with smooth neural fields* | JCP 532, 113957 (2025), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2025.113957), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14890v2) | Section 6 compares POD and autoencoder ROMs. Tables 3/4 vary time step and sampling together, reporting error/time/memory. Available author version precedes final journal publication. |

Mapping: distinguish approximation from dynamics, dense from sparse reduced operators, and latent evolution from complete field output. Continuous neural fields already have precedent. Same-bank controls isolate the head; independent POD/quadratic/neural-field comparators test competitiveness. Proposed plots and hypotheses are not new measured project results.
