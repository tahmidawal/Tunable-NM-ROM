# B1 recipe 1: normalized global parent

Prospective recipe: 16 global latent variables, training-only spectral POD span of 512 fields, centered physical coefficients, latent standard deviations normalized to one, free global mean/offset, three tanh decoder hidden layers of width 128, two encoder layers 256/128, and a linear skip. The spectral bank is an architecture-inspired feature control, not a native coordinate-MLP reproduction. It isolates head training from coordinate-feature regression error. Its projection floor is retained and scored; it may be useful as a parent without passing the whole-field representation gate by itself.

Same 64 training trajectories, 16 random development trajectories and 12 alignment cases; no final-test access or new truth. Fit physical current-time relative error. Adam starts at 0.001 with cosine decay to 0.00001, at most 20000 updates, batch 128. Encoder reconstruction initializes the first 3000 updates; afterwards jointly optimize decoder and per-snapshot codes while retaining the encoder term. Every 1000 updates, refit training codes with 25 LM iterations and development codes with 75 iterations, encoded/zero/temporal starts. Select worst projection error, report encoder versus optimized errors and stationarity. After 8000 updates, stop after five checks without 1% relative improvement. Preserve all checkpoints.

Reserve at most 120 GPU-minutes. Advance to local residual reconstruction if this parent materially improves on B0. No PDE performance inference. A second latent dimension or joint feature change requires its own prospective recipe.
