What the paper can say

One trained bank, one dial, the same answer on every mesh, at a fraction of the memory

And, stated plainly: on smooth Burgers it is not faster than a spectral solver or a tuned POD model, and it doesn’t extrapolate to steeper fronts. We know which of those reasons are measured and which are still hypotheses.

Recommended framing: a characterisation paper of a mesh-independent, tunable, mesh-free reduced model, with the speed and accuracy limits measured and explained.
