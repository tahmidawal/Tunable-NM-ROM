## Glossary

- **NM-ROM / nmrom_qN_…**: nonlinear-manifold reduced model: learned spatial bank (R functions), nonlinear head with K latent variables, plus N linear bank correction directions eliminated analytically. `q0` = fast setting, `q>0` = accurate settings.
- **cn**: reduced Crank–Nicolson weak steps (dt 0.025, 20 nonlinear least-squares solves). **direct**: each output time fitted independently to the exactly propagated sine moments of the supplied initial field (5 batched solves); valid only for a linear autonomous PDE with eigenfunction tests. **field / moments**: initial fit to the full field or to its M test moments. **tol1e-4**: LM stationarity tolerance (default 1e-6). **chol**: Cholesky normal equations.
- **err all-times / evolved**: worst over cases of the maximum over output times (including / excluding t=0) of relative L2 error against exact same-grid semidiscrete propagation.
- **named CN-CG**: full-order Crank–Nicolson with unpreconditioned warm-started conjugate gradients, dt 0.025, relative tolerance 1e-6. **fastest CN-CG with err <= method**: fastest tested (dt, tolerance) full-order setting whose all-times error does not exceed the method’s. **coarse-grid FOM matched**: same CN-CG on a coarse grid, interpolated, with physical evolved error <= the method’s.
- **x**: comparator GPU time divided by method GPU time, medians of 5 retained repetitions per case in one allocation; >1 means the method is faster.
- **dst_exact_CONTROL**: exact fast-sine-transform solve, a labelled direct/transform control. **linear_bank_…_BASELINE**: free linear coefficients in the learned bank — a labelled baseline, not the NM-ROM.
- **sealed_opened_once**: 16 draws (seed 791099) never seen by training, validation or any choice, evaluated once. **failed solves**: non-stationary LM exits or unconverged CG solves.
