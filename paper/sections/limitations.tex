\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Single seed, single checkpoint.} Every number rests on one training
seed and one checkpoint per PDE. The three-seed repetition and the
sealed-cohort evaluation are running; that lane has recorded an amendment to
its fidelity gate, from $10^{-9}$ to $10^{-3}$ relative, made before any of its
numbers were read.
\textbf{Development cohort}: six opened Burgers cases (32 held-out for the
tuning study), eight wave and twelve Poisson; on the L-shape the 461-source
validation worst is two to three times the 32-source development worst.
\textbf{2D only, and nothing harder than viscous Burgers is solved}: the
L-shape cell has no solve layer yet and Navier--Stokes no reduced model.
\textbf{No cold-start comparison against a shallow masked-autoencoder ROM}; the
classical comparison is POD-LSPG through our own solver, and the linear skip is
a design choice we do not ablate.
\textbf{Operator numbers are lower bounds}: \nOpLaneImproving{}
U-Net/Transolver arms and \nOpFnoImproving{} FNO arms were still improving at
their wall budget, and none was tuned per family; a larger budget beats the
ROM by more.
\textbf{Quadrature certification is one draw}: a second draw at $q=64$ moved
$\rho_{\max}$ from $\nEqtopQsixtyFourArchivedRho$ to
$\nEqtopQsixtyFourRedrawRho$ and flipped the flag; until job
$\nEqtopPendingJob$ lands the EQ ladder is ``certified in this draw''.
\textbf{The headline ladder was chosen after the fact and quotes a dearer
baseline}: $M=1088$ was selected after the crossed grid ran, the $M=256$
ladder fails the same bar, and the fixed-$M$ $q=0$ rung costs
$\nQxmFixedQzeroMs$\,ms against $\nQxmScheduledQzeroMs$\,ms for the scheduled
rung (different jobs). \textbf{Cross-job spread} of an identical cell is
$\nQxmAnchorSpreadPct\,\%$, the size of the tolerance saving.
\textbf{No theory}: no convergence guarantee, no quadrature-error bound on
unseen states, no continuum error bound.

(\nSeedsStatus); the L-shape solve layer (\nLshapeSolve); the Navier--Stokes
reduced model (\nNsRom); the operators' seed and precision controls
