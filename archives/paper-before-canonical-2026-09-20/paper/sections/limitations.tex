\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{One checkpoint per PDE; three seeds on one ladder only.} Every
headline rests on one training seed and one checkpoint per PDE. The Burgers
scheduled ladder was repeated on \nSeedsCount{} seeds
(Table~\ref{tab:seeds}): monotone on \nSeedsMonotoneEvolved{} of
\nSeedsCount, meeting the knob bar on \nSeedsKnobBar{} of \nSeedsCount{}
(\nSeedsUnconvergedCells{} did not converge), spans
\nSeedsErrSpanMin--\nSeedsErrSpanMax{} in error. That lane amended its
fidelity gate from $10^{-9}$ to $10^{-3}$ relative before reading any number,
and its sealed cohort is not yet opened.
\textbf{Development cohort}: six opened Burgers cases (32 held-out for the
tuning study), eight wave and twelve Poisson.
\textbf{2D only}; Navier--Stokes has a certified full-order solver but its
reduced model failed its pre-registered gate at $K=16$ (\S\ref{sec:exp:linear}).
\textbf{No cold-start comparison against a shallow masked-autoencoder ROM}; the
classical comparison is POD-LSPG through our own solver, and the linear skip is
a design choice, not ablated. \textbf{No theory}: no convergence guarantee, no
quadrature-error bound on unseen states, no continuum error bound.
\textbf{Operator numbers are lower bounds}: \nOpLaneImproving{}
U-Net/Transolver arms and \nOpFnoImproving{} FNO arms were still improving at
their wall budget, and none was tuned per family. The U-Net comparison survives
a float64 and a second-seed control, but that margin is
$\nOpCtrlMediumFSixtyFourMargin$\,pp and the float64 twins ran a quarter to half
the epochs at equal wall clock; the Transolver arm below the ROM has no twin.
\textbf{Quadrature rules above $q=32$ are marginal} (\S\ref{sec:exp:eq}).
\textbf{The headline ladder was chosen after the fact}: $M=1088$ was selected
after the crossed grid ran and the $M=256$ ladder fails the same bar.
\textbf{Cross-job spread} of an identical cell is $\nQxmAnchorSpreadPct\,\%$.

\textbf{The $1024^2$ frontier statement} rests on one job, on the evolved
metric only, and on rules transferred from $256^2$; a $512^2$ panel on the
$256^2$ GPU model is queued to bracket it (Table~\ref{tab:inflight}).
