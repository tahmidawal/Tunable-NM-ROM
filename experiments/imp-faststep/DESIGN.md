# imp-faststep (E5, ideation W1): a faster reduced time step for the off-mesh bank ROM, Burgers 2D

Pre-registration, written 2026-10-21 before any code or data of this lane. Binding sources: the campaign
`COMMON-BRIEF.md` and lane brief `briefs/imp-faststep.md` (main, `reports/2026-10-21-improvement-campaign/campaign/`),
`reports/2026-10-08-jcp-lane-agent-rules.md`, and the ideation report `reports/2026-10-20-improvement-ideation/report.md`
(§2.2, F1, F5, F6, §5 row W1). Amendments are appended at the end, dated; this text is never rewritten.

## 1. Question

Our deployed 2D query (LSPG, Crank–Nicolson with Rannacher start-up "CN-R", $\Delta t_0 = 0.005$, 50 steps) spends its
time on two dense $M\times r$ Jacobian assemblies per step inside a Levenberg–Marquardt loop (ideation §2: r = 384,
1.74 ms/step against 0.613 ms/step for the tuned POD-ECSW pick, H200, 1024²), and its error is dominated by the LSPG CN-R
time error. **Does a Galerkin step with offline precontraction (implicit CN, exponential ETDRK4, or one-pass
linearised CN) give an r = 384 evolution at least 3× faster than the incumbent, with no invalid or unstable run and no
larger worst-case error, at r ∈ {128, 384}, on 1024² and 4096²?** And, as a result rather than a gate: with the same
ETDRK4 step, how does tuned POD + ECSW compare (H-base, Problem 2)?

## 3. Arms

Notation (t2core/qcore): $A = \Phi^\top G' \in\mathbb R^{M\times r}$, $\Lambda = \operatorname{diag}(\lambda)$ the discrete
Laplacian eigenvalues of the M tests, tested advection $N(c) = \Psi^\top[(G_q c)\odot(G_s c)]$ (point form,
$\Psi = L\,w\,\psi\in\mathbb R^{m\times M}$, $G_q, G_s\in\mathbb R^{m\times r}$), semi-discrete
$A\dot c = -[N(c) + \nu\Lambda A c]$, thin QR $A = QR_m$.

**Diffusion coordinates (F1a + F1b), built offline per (mesh, r, rule):** $Q^\top\Lambda Q = VDV^\top$ (symmetric
positive definite, `eigh`, independent of $\nu$), $z = V^\top R_m c$, $c = R_m^{-1}Vz$, and
$$B_z = G_qR_m^{-1}V,\quad D_z = G_sR_m^{-1}V,\quad P_z = \Psi QV \quad(m\times r),\qquad
\dot z = -g(z),\quad g(z) = P_z^\top[(B_zz)\odot(D_zz)] + \nu Dz .$$
This is exactly the t2core GAL semi-discretisation $R_m\dot c = -Q^\top N(c) - \nu Q^\top\Lambda A c$ (multiply by
$V^\top$); $\|z\| = \|Ac\|$, so the GAL tolerances transfer unchanged. One advection evaluation costs $6mr$ flops
(three $m\times r$ products) instead of $2m(M + 2r)$; no $M$-sized operation is online. Initial fit and decode are
arm 1's (`p1core.make_split_query` init/decode), with the $r\times r$ maps $c_0\mapsto z_0$ and $z\mapsto c$ inside the
evolve call.

### 3.1 Arm 1 (incumbent)
`p1common.Setting` / `p1core.make_split_query`, form LSPG, scheme CNR, $\Delta t_0$, tolerances `tol_of('LSPG', .)`,
budget 600; exactly the p1-scaling `acc1024`/`acc4096` configurations R128 and R384 (frozen policy).

### 3.2 Arm 2 (G-CN)
Predictor: linear extrapolation $2z_n - z_{n-1}$ (first step: $z_0$). Newton iteration on the residual above with
$J = I + \tfrac{\Delta t}{2}(J_N(z) + \nu D)$, $J_N(z) = P_z^\top[\operatorname{diag}(D_zz)B_z + \operatorname{diag}(B_zz)D_z]$,
LU (`jnp.linalg.solve`); stop when $\|\rho\| \le \text{tolf}\,\|z_n\|$ (prod $10^{-10}$, tight $10^{-13}$, tighter
$10^{-15}$, the GAL levels of `p1common.tol_of`) or after 30 iterations (budget exit; a run with a budget exit at prod
level whose fresh residual exceeds the tolerance counts against the certificate below). Non-finite iterate → run
invalid.

### 3.3 Arm 3 (ETDRK4)
$\dot z = \mathcal L z + \mathcal N(z)$ with $\mathcal L = -\nu D$ (diagonal), $\mathcal N(z) = -P_z^\top[(B_zz)\odot(D_zz)]$;
the four-stage Cox–Matthews step exactly as `jcp-fom2d/psjax2d.py` (`E`, `E2`, `Q`, `f1`, `f2`, `f3` per query from the
traced $\nu$). Four advection evaluations per step; no Jacobian, factorisation or iteration.

### 3.4 Arm 4 (one-pass linearised CN, F5)
Linearly implicit trapezoidal (one-stage Rosenbrock, $\gamma = 1/2$) on the square ODE, first step the same way; second
order for this ODE (ideation F5). One $J_g$ ($2mr^2$ flops), one LU, two triangular solves per step.

### 3.5 Arm 5 (POD + ECSW with the same ETDRK4 step) and 5L

- **ECSW for the Galerkin force** (Farhat et al.): $\Phi^\top N_h(\Phi c) \approx \sum_{e\in E}\xi_e\,\Phi_{e,:}^\top

## 4. Numerical qualification per arm (Q1 + Q2; COMMON-BRIEF §3)

Every certificate is computed **in-job** from saved per-step states by code independent of the stepper's arithmetic
(original, un-precontracted t2core quantities: $A$, $Q$, $R_m$, $\Lambda$, the tested advection `Q.tested_value('point')`).

- **Arm 1 and 6 (LSPG)**: unchanged contract. Fresh autodiff Jacobian at every step (`p1core.make_certifier`);
  $\gamma = \|J^\top r\|/(\|J\|_F\|r\| + 10^{-300})$; non-stationary ($\gamma > 10^{-3}$ or non-finite) ≤ 1 % pooled over
  steps; distance to the tight solve ≤ 2.5e-4 every case; tight/tighter ≤ 2.5e-5 on dev6; validity (finite, no
  non-finite step, no damping saturation, reason 3).
- **Arm 2 (Galerkin, square system).** $\gamma$ is not a convergence measure for a square nonsingular system (at a root
  it is 0/0, near it bounded by $\|J\|_2/\|J\|_F$), so the analogous certificate is the **fresh residual**: at every step
  $k$, $c_k = R_m^{-1}Vz_k$ and $r_k = Q^\top[A(c_{k+1}-c_k) + \tfrac{\Delta t}{2}(F(c_{k+1}) + F(c_k))]$,
  $F(c) = N(c) + \nu\Lambda Ac$ (t2core GAL residual, CN coefficients); a step is *unconverged* if
  $\|r_k\| > (1+10^{-6})\,\text{tolf}\,\|Ac_k\| + 10^{-14}\|Ac_k\|$ or non-finite; unconverged ≤ 1 % pooled; plus the
  tight distance ≤ 2.5e-4 on every case and tight/tighter ≤ 2.5e-5 on dev6, validity (finite, no non-finite iterate).
  $\gamma$ of the fresh autodiff Jacobian of $r_k$ is recorded, not a bar.
- **Arm 3 (explicit ETDRK4).** No algebraic solve, so no tolerance distance exists. Analogous certificate:
  (a) validity and the stability monitor (§5); (b) **fresh-RHS certificate** at every step state: $g$ evaluated through
  the precontracted tables vs $V^\top R_m\cdot R_m^{-1}Q^\top[N(c_k) + \nu\Lambda Ac_k]$ through the original tables,
  relative difference ≤ 1e-10 at every step; (c) **un-precontracted twin**: the same ETDRK4 recurrence with the RHS
  evaluated only through the original tables (`tested_value`, $Q$, $R_m$), distance to the arm ≤ 2.5e-5 on dev6 (the
  tight/tighter analogue). The step-halving distance ($\Delta t/2$, dev6) is reported as the time-error estimate, not a
  Q1 bar.
- **Arm 4.** (a) validity and stability; (b) at every step the Jacobian used equals the fresh autodiff Jacobian of the
  original-table RHS to ≤ 1e-9 relative (Frobenius), and the linear-solve residual
  $\|(I + \tfrac{\Delta t}{2}J)\delta + \Delta t\,g\| \le 10^{-10}\|\Delta t\,g\|$; (c) un-precontracted twin ≤ 2.5e-5
  on dev6.
- **Arms 5/5L.** 5: validity, stability, twin through un-precontracted ECSW rows (dev6). 5L: hr-baselines `certify`
  (γ contract as arm 1, tight anchors on dev6 only — descriptive).
- **Q2 (arms 1–4, 6)**: on the Q2 population sub16 (dev6 + val32[0:10]) each arm is replayed with the successor rule
  (fib6765 at r = 128, gauss160 at r = 384; tables rebuilt with the same $A$, $Q$, $R_m$, $V$); bars: own-state
  $\rho = \|N_{\rm main}(c) - N_{\rm succ}(c)\|/\|N_{\rm succ}(c)\| \le 0.116$ at the six output states of every sub16
  case, and rollout distance main vs successor ≤ 2.5e-4 on every sub16 case. Distances are scoring-lattice relative
  (`p1common.fdist`, normalised by $\|u_0\|_{257}$). Q2 for arm 5 is not defined (POD has no rule ladder); its
  hyper-reduction error is the distance to `P-E4-none`, reported.
- Labels kept separate: **numerical** (Q1+Q2), **role** (the §6 stop-rule conditions), **frontier** (not dominated in
  (full-query time, worst error) among this job's arms and comparators).

## 5. Stability and validity (used by the stop rule)

A production run is **invalid** if any output, recorded state or iterate is non-finite, or (implicit arms) an LM
damping-saturation / reason-3 exit occurs, or (arm 2) the Newton budget is exhausted with a fresh residual above
tolerance at that step. It is **unstable** if, at any step, $\|Ac_k\| > 1.05\,\|Ac_0\|$ (for arms 2–4 $\|Ac\| = \|z\|$;
the continuum $L^2$ energy of viscous Burgers with zero walls is non-increasing, and $\|Ac\|$ is the norm of the
tested projection; the 5 % margin is for quadrature effects). The POD arm uses $\|c_k\| > 1.05\|c_0\|$ ($\Phi$
orthonormal). The monitor is evaluated on every step in-job.

## 6. Stop rule (verbatim from the brief) and its operational reading

> Stop if any run is unstable or invalid, its worst error is above the current one (arm 1), or the r = 384 evolution is
> < 3× faster. Report the outcome for each new arm separately; the E5 line is "fixed" only if at least one
> Galerkin/ETDRK4 arm passes all three conditions.

Applied to each new arm **2, 3a, 3b, 4** separately (5, 5L, 6 are comparators/controls and never "fix" E5):
1. **Stability/validity:** fires if any production run (main rule, prod level, 38 cases, both r, every mesh run) is
   invalid or unstable (§5).
2. **Worst error:** per (r, mesh), $W_X = \max_{\rm cases} e$ over the 38 cases. Fires if $W_X > W_{1}$ at any (r, mesh)
   (strict numerical reading; computed both over the E0-eligible cases and over all 38; fires if either is above). The
   tie-aware reading (is $W_X - W_1 > 2\varepsilon_{\rm ref}$ on the cases attaining the maxima) is reported beside it.
3. **Speed:** $S_{\rm evo}$ = median over the 6 dev6 timing cases of the per-case median (3 reps) of
   $\bar t_{A}/t_{B,\rm evolve}$ (A = arm 1 at the same r; $\bar t_A$ the mean of the two bracketing A evolutions). Fires
   if $S_{\rm evo} < 3$ at r = 384 on any mesh run.

An arm **passes** if none fires. **E5 = "fixed"** iff at least one of 2, 3a, 3b, 4 passes **and** is numerically
qualified (Q1 + Q2 at both r on every mesh run); an arm that passes the three conditions but is unqualified is reported
as "passes the stop rule, numerically unqualified" and does not fix E5.

**Secondary, from ideation F5 (arm 4 only, reported, not part of the E5 line):** stop if its observed order against a
converged reduced reference of the same ODE (three levels $\Delta t_0, \Delta t_0/2, \Delta t_0/4$; reference = arm 3's
ETDRK4 on the same ODE at $\Delta t_0/8$; dev6; per-case least-squares slope of $\log$ distance vs $\log\Delta t$; dev6
median) is below 1.8, or its E0 worst error exceeds arm 2's at the same Δt, or its end-to-end (fused query) gain over
arm 2 is below 1.3× (median over the dev6 timing cases). **H-base reading (result, not a gate):** arm 5 pick vs arm 3 at
the same Δt and r, and arms 2–4 vs 5L: lower fused-query time **and** lower worst error = dominance (developmental,
in-sample, unadjusted).

## 8. Metrics and outputs

Per (arm, r, mesh, case): E0 ST $e$, $e(t_k)$, $\varepsilon_{\rm ref}$, eligibility; Q1/Q2 evidence; stability maximum;
Newton iterations (arm 2); step-halving distance (dev6, arms 1–4); twin distances. Per (arm, r, mesh) on the 6 timing
cases × 3 reps: paired A–B–A **init, evolve, decode** times and a fused-query triplet; per-step evolution time =
evolve/steps. **Best stepper** (for E1/E2): among arms passing §6 and qualified, the lowest r = 384, 1024² median fused
query time on dev6 (selection on dev only); if none passes, "none" and the fastest valid arm is named descriptively.
EXPORT.json: every verdict, the per-step and per-query decompositions (evolution, initial projection, decode), source
paths. Plots: (time, worst error) front per r and mesh; per-step cost bars; Newton/time-error diagnostics.
