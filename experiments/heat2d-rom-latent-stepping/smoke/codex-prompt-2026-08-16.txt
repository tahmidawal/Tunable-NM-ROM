WHAT THIS IS
A reduced-order-model (ROM) experiment harness for the 2D heat equation, ported from a
validated Burgers-2D reference. Full-order model (FOM):
  du/dt = kappa * lap(u) on [0,1]^2, u = 0 on the walls, backward Euler dt = 0.005,
  50 steps, conjugate-gradient solve per implicit step.
The FOM lives in
  /home/tahmid/Dev/pod-ae-nmrom/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/worktrees/2026-08-13-heat2d-coord-decoder/experiments/heat2d-coord-decoder/heat2d_film.py
(functions boundary_mask, make_rollout, sample_params, build_trajectories, film_apply).

The ROM: a hard-BC FiLM coordinate "auto-decoder" u(x;z) = eps * b(x) * FiLM(x;z) with
b(x,y) = 16 x(1-x) y(1-y), one latent per (trajectory, time) snapshot. Online, at each
implicit step the latent is solved by damped Levenberg-Marquardt (LSPG) or damped Newton
on the Galerkin root, warm-started at the previous step's latent. Objectives:
 (a) STRONG form: the FOM's own discrete backward-Euler residual at m interior nodes,
     R = u - u_prev - dt*kappa*lap5(u), evaluated from decoder values at 5-point stencils;
 (b) WEAK form (the recipe under test): project R onto the M lowest DISCRETE sine modes
     phi_i (exact eigenvectors of the ghost-zero 5-point Laplacian, -L phi_i = lam_i phi_i),
     giving R_i = w_i [ phi_i^T (u - u_prev) + dt*kappa*lam_i*phi_i^T u ],
     w_i = (1 + dt*kappa*lam_i)^(-alpha).
 (c) hyper-reduction: replace the exact grid sums phi_i^T u by an m-point quadrature with
     non-negative weights from a capped Lawson-Hanson NNLS fit ("EQ"), on grid nodes
     ("eq<m>") or on a meshfree random pool ("eqoff<m>").
Controls: POD basis with the same solver (POD-LSPG at k in 6,8,16,32,64), and an
"oracle" floor (per-snapshot latents fitted to the HELD-OUT field).
