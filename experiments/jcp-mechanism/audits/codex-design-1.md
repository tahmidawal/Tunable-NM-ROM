**The design needs amendment before execution.** The normalization is correct; the main failures are the causal outcome labels, the negative-control guarantee, and the unchanged large-array implementation. No files changed and no jobs ran.

1. **Node-weight identity — CORRECT.** With \(L=n-1\) in 3D, \(L^{d/2}h^d\psi=L^{-d/2}2^{d/2}\prod_j\sin(a_j\pi x_j)=\Phi\). This matches `offmesh.test_block`, `common.dst_axis`/`phi_explicit`, and the 2D combination `qcore.offmesh_data`/`hops.phi_rows`. It is an exact mathematical identity, subject to floating-point differences between GEMM, sine evaluation and DST. **Fix:** explicitly require identical mode indices, ordering, coordinates and flattening; retain \(w=h^d\) without renormalizing the interior weights to sum to one.

2. **“Differs from dense FOM advection only by the derivative” — CORRECT, at a fixed decoded state.** Both expressions use the same samples, tests and projection; only the spatial derivative changes. This does **not** make the actual 3D incumbent sign-upwind: `tables.build_tables` uses `common.dminus`, a fixed backward difference. The [3D report](/home/tahmid/Dev/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/reports/2026-10-01-burgers3d-offmesh-quadrature.md) records tensor/sign-upwind discrepancies around \(10^{-5}\)–\(10^{-4}\). Also, this is the FOM’s operator evaluated on a ROM state, not its full evolution. **Fix:** distinguish backward, sign-upwind and analytic derivatives throughout; quantify the backward/sign-upwind discrepancy on the states used for the mechanism claim.

3. **A1 separation of gradient and placement — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** Dense-versus-nodes is a useful controlled derivative ablation, especially in 2D. Nodes-versus-selected-rule simultaneously changes **locations, weights and point count**; it cannot isolate placement itself. Successful nodes rollouts can demonstrate that off-grid locations are unnecessary for the observed accuracy, but failed nodes rollouts cannot establish that off-grid locations caused the gain. Exact differentiation also means differentiating the trained bank, not the true PDE solution; finer-training-data effects remain unresolved. **Fix:** state the narrow sufficiency claim, call the second contrast “quadrature-rule replacement,” and add matched-budget shifted/unshifted rules only if placement-specific causation is required.

4. **G/P/X outcomes — WRONG as mechanism conclusions.** For finite, defined metrics these predicates are mutually exclusive, exhaustive and non-tautological, but their labels do not follow. Equal incumbent and converged errors automatically trigger P—even if their fields coincide and there is no gain to explain. Equal error norms also allow very different fields, while cohort maxima may come from different cases. The \(0.116\) residual threshold and 1% field-distance threshold are not justified as mechanism-equivalence margins; the latter is substantially looser than the earlier 3D \(10^{-3}\) rollout criterion. **Fix:** require a resolvable incumbent–continuum field separation, then classify using paired per-case field distances and a preregistered fraction of that separation. Make ties, failed solves and insufficient separation X; replace P with “nodes did not recover the continuum rollout.”

5. **G1–G4 — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** G1–G3 are sound algebraic checks, but need specified norms, finite-difference steps, sample selection and absolute tolerances near zero. G3 does not independently validate spatial derivatives; G1 supplies that check. G4’s thresholds are **not inherently unattainable on GPUs**: the source reports demonstrate deterministic repeats and stringent parity. However, cross-device/compiler reductions can perturb LM stopping, predictor selection and acceptance branches; same-job parity does not guarantee historical replay at \(10^{-8}\). Conversely, reproducing only the 2D worst ST error can hide changed trajectories. Failure also does not destroy pairing between new arms run together. **Fix:** separate implementation parity, historical reproducibility and new within-job pairing; compare per-case fields and solver diagnostics, pin the numerical stack, and resolve “abort/void” versus “report as unpaired.”

6. **A2 fixed-state slopes — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** The relative slope is well-defined **if both sides use the current mesh’s normalization**: writing \(I(c)=\int\psi u\,\mathbf1\cdot\nabla u\), the common \(L^{d/2}\) cancels from \(\rho\). Reusing a target scaled at \(n=65\) without rescaling would manufacture a false trend. Freezing coefficients and actual mode tuples is correct; rerunning mesh-dependent eigenvalue ordering is not. Zero padding supplies the exact boundary values, so central differences at the first interior node retain second-order consistency. The remaining threats are unresolved bank frequencies, vanishing leading coefficients, ambiguous “median-state statistic,” and continuum-target error overwhelming small gaps. **Fix:** store normalized integral vectors and frozen mode tuples; define the fitted statistic precisely and preregister a target-accuracy/roundoff exclusion policy with “unresolved slope” outcomes.

7. **C-pos/C-neg — WRONG as guaranteed controls.** Both can fail, but failure does not necessarily identify broken code. C-pos has no fully specified manufactured function or demonstrated nonzero leading error: symmetry can cancel tested coefficients, and a coordinatewise quadratic state even has an exact central derivative. For C-neg, if \(N_h=N+h^2E+\cdots\), then \(1.01N_h-N=0.01N+1.01h^2E+\cdots\). A 1% multiplier guarantees a nonzero **asymptotic** floor when \(N\neq0\), not slope \(<0.5\) over either finite window; dominance and cancellation both matter. **Fix:** freeze an explicit manufactured state with independently computed integrals and nonzero error coefficients, and establish its finite-window expectations. Add a separate injected constant vector error with analytically known zero slope.

8. **Euler–Maclaurin prediction — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT; nominal fourth order is sound.** For \(f_a=\psi_a u\,\mathbf1\cdot\nabla u\), both \(\psi_a\) and \(u\) vanish linearly at each face, hence \(f_a=\partial_nf_a=0\). Tensor-product Euler–Maclaurin therefore removes the \(h^2\) face corrections and gives \(Q_hf_a-\int f_a=h^4C_a+O(h^6)\), with possible further cancellation. This holds in both dimensions for fixed smooth states/tests; it is fourth order in **spacing**, not point count. It does not guarantee a finite-window slope ≥2 or error “far below upwind at every mesh.” **Fix:** state the normalized asymptotic expansion, permit superconvergence, and condition numerical slope expectations on resolution and reference accuracy.

9. **Cohort/sealing — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** The design appropriately discloses historical reuse, freezes selected rules and excludes the 3D held-out cohort. Nothing written explicitly selects a new rule on test64. Nevertheless, the [2D test64 outcomes](/home/tahmid/Dev/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/reports/2026-10-01-burgers2d-offmesh-quadrature.md) were already public before this design, so this is replication on previously inspected data, not fresh sealed confirmation. “If the job fits” also leaves cohort completion dependent on runtime and potentially case difficulty. **Fix:** freeze execution order, all-or-none replication policy and fallback triggers before outcomes; report every incomplete case and all deviations. Reserve any generalization claim for a genuinely unopened, globally sealed cohort.

10. **Memory and GPU-hour feasibility — WRONG for unchanged code reuse; feasible only after redesign.** At \(128^3\), \(m=127^3=2{,}048{,}383\): \(P\) contains **4,197,136,767 doubles = 33.58 GB**, and \(B,D,P\) total **50.36 GB**, excluding the mesh bank, tensor, temporaries and compiler workspace. `offmesh.psi` materializes full arrays; this exceeds the GEMM element limit documented in `hops.py`. At \(1024^2\), \(M=1536,R'=384\), \(P\) is **12.86 GB** and \(B,D,P\) **19.29 GB**; `offmesh_data` chunks construction but concatenates the results. A2’s finest full test matrices would require **272.20 GB in 3D** and **206.06 GB in 2D**. **Fix:** use streamed contractions or DST/separable projections, never concatenate giant test blocks, and gate peak memory at the largest shape. The 6.5 GPU-hour estimate needs measured setup/compile/throughput evidence; array arithmetic alone cannot establish or refute that runtime.

11. **Other rejection risks — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** The reused 3D solver performs one sweep after its initial adaptive steps and can return nonstationary states; the design lacks an explicit rollout acceptance gate, so apparent mechanism effects may include solver error. Residual certification on incumbent-reached states also leaves nodes-reached states unchecked. “Converged” `lat32768` and the finite Gauss targets need accuracy adequate for the particular inference, especially A2. Finally, A2 measures rates but does **not** implement theory check C6’s leading-coefficient comparison, and these linear-span experiments do not establish nonlinear-head behavior. **Fix:** require finite/convergence diagnostics and a tighter-solve sensitivity check, check targets on nodes trajectories, compare the normalized vector gap with \(-\tfrac h2\int\psi|u|\Delta u\) if claiming C6, and restrict conclusions to the tested frozen spans.
