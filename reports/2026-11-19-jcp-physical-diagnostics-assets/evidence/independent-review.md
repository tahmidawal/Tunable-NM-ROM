## Glossary

**Scoped claim:** a conclusion limited to the tested configuration and checks. **Qualification:** satisfying predeclared numerical and measurement rules. **Admission:** permission to continue to later experiments after those checks. **Stencil:** neighboring points needed by a discrete operator. **Probe:** a specified diagnostic perturbation. **Code-only mirror:** a remote branch that omits designated large regenerable archive files. **Integrity:** consistency and traceability of the recorded evidence.
