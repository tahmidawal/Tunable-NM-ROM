## Caveats

- **32³: the Table-1 FOM changes between cohorts.** On the test cases `cnab2_s40` (0.185 %) is at least as accurate as the accurate setting (0.206 %), so it becomes the comparator; on the development cases it was not (0.152 % vs 0.151 %, ratio 1.0040), and `cnab2_s50` was used. Against `cnab2_s50` on the test cases the accurate setting would be 1.13×; the rule gives 0.926×.
- 32³: on the test cases the accurate setting is slower than the rule FOM (0.926×).
- The test cohort (seed 202609221) is the one already evaluated four times at 96³; its parameters are mesh-independent. It was never generated at 32³/64³ and no setting at these meshes was chosen on it (DESIGN.md §4). The fast A setting was picked on 2026-09-24 from development records, after the 96³ test results existed.
- Speedups are same-job ratios. The paper-value development rows use a different timing protocol (fast block, case 0) on a different GPU; the same-job development row is the like-for-like reference.
- CNAB2 is pseudo-spectral and does no linear solve; it is the Table-1 FOM for this problem, not an iterative solver.
- The audit is restricted: exact on saved full-field cases, sampled estimates (diagnostic) elsewhere; the truth solver is the same CNAB2 code and is not independently re-solved.

## Glossary

- **accurate setting** — the head: a small network mapping k=8 unknowns (plus 3 frame-translation unknowns) to the 64 bank coefficients, solved by 3 Gauss–Newton sweeps per step of Δt 0.02.
- **fast A / fast B** — the linear span of the first R'=16 importance-ordered bank columns, stepped with Δt 0.04 and 2 sweeps (A) or Δt 0.02 and 3 sweeps (B).
- **span R'** — the same linear solve with the first R' ordered bank columns.
- **bank** — the rank-64 POD basis of energy-centred training snapshots, used in a frame that moves with the flow structure.
- **CNAB2 / FOM** — the full-order pseudo-spectral solver; "steps" over T=0.2. The Table-1 FOM is its fastest stable setting at least as accurate as the accurate setting, in the same job.
- **worst %** — per case the largest relative L2 error (relative to the initial field's norm) over the five output times after t=0, then the largest over cases. **median-case %** — the median over cases of that per-case value. **cases > 5 %** — how many cases exceed 5 %.
- **×** — FOM median time divided by the arm's median time, both from the same job and cohort. **matched CNAB2** — the fastest CNAB2 setting at least as accurate as that arm.
- **test / development cohort** — 32 held-out cases never used for a choice at these meshes / 16 cases on which k=8 and the fast settings were chosen.
- **A–B–A timing** — interleaved rounds, then arm-major rounds, then interleaved rounds; **drift** = median(A2)/median(A1), **order** = median(B)/median(A1∪A2), both gated to within 10 %.
- **reproduction gap** — largest relative difference between this job's development error rows and those of the original development job (gate 1e-6). **bank gap** — rebuilt bank vs stored probe.
- **timed-output gap** — difference between outputs produced during timing and the accuracy pass.
- **perturbed control** — the audit must fail on a copy with one field changed by 1e-6.
