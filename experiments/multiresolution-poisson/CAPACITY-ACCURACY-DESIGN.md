# Proposed isolated Poisson bank-capacity comparison

This is a proposed follow-up protocol, pending acceptance of `staged_accuracy08` and coordinator review before submission. It changes the learned feature-bank size while preserving the nonlinear latent head, source-input contract and exact weak method. No new result is claimed here.

The fixed-rank study does not establish that rank 64 is insufficient for every possible trained bank. Its normalized snapshot POD diagnostic minimizes average training error; neither its training nor development worst-case projection is a minimax lower bound. A separate rank-128 POD projection diagnostic will quantify potential for a larger span without becoming a deployed decoder or an initializer.

The proposed primary arm widens the original learned coordinate bank from 64 to 128 features at latent dimension 16. Preserve all original Fourier frequencies, hidden layers, output columns and training codes. Construct 64 additional spatial output columns from a deterministic orthogonal complement of the original affine output weights in the existing 129-dimensional hidden-plus-bias space. Scale only those new columns to comparable training-grid feature norms. Append zero output columns to the nonlinear head and its linear skip. Thus the widened network initially represents precisely the original decoder function for every latent input; additional bank directions exist without perturbing that function. Persist both checkpoints, complement/scales, numerical bank singular values, and training-grid plus independent-coordinate function/Jacobian parity checks before training. A failed rank check is a retained diagnostic failure, never an accepted projection floor.

Use the identical 512 training sources, original 255-interval training mesh and per-snapshot relative objective. Do not add difficult-case weighting in this capacity arm. The proposed rank-128 stages retain the predeclared 40000 bank, 30000 head and 20000 joint update budgets, existing learning rates and sampling settings. The bank stage starts from full-field least-squares free coefficients; the head stage uses the exact QR field metric with perpendicular residual; the joint stage retains the existing relative reconstruction objective and regularizer. Save the widened initial, bank, head and joint endpoints without development-selected stopping.

The matched rank-64 arm starts from the same original checkpoint, follows the same three phases and matches each rank-128 phase's synchronized optimizer-block time. Use the established 250-update block rule, first complete block reaching the target, and retain every block, actual updates/exposures and overhead. Exclude compilation, data, QR, diagnostics, checkpoint writing and burn-in consistently. It matches measured training effort on the same GPU; it does not equate FLOPs or certify convergence. The staged head phase uses the bank produced by its own preceding rank-64 phase.

Retiming includes the frozen original network, both matched rank-64 head/joint endpoints and both rank-128 head/joint endpoints, alongside the same loose/tight CG and direct DST controls. Weights remain frozen at 64, 256 and 1024 intervals over all 30 original and 12 later development cases, all already opened before the capacity choice, with explicit separate aggregates. Keep 256 requested smooth weak modes (actual shell-preserving count recorded), one nearest-training prediction start, stationarity threshold 1e-6 and budget 300. The test count remains comfortably greater than latent dimension. Rebuild every bank, exact weak operator and code cache at every mesh. Full source input, initialization, solve and requested full-field output stay in the query timing; no empirical quadrature or parameter descriptors are introduced.

Retain three paired repetitions after burn-in, all full fields, actual solver exits and gradients, rank/linear-solve gates, refinement allowance and checkpoint/source/operator identity. Include full-bank projections at every mesh and two-start best-found stationary full-field head diagnostics at 256 intervals, separate from timed online inputs. All outcomes, including rank failures, iteration caps, missed physical targets and speed regressions, are retained. No final cohort is opened. The later 12 cases were first seeded for the preceding fixed-rank study; their outcomes now informed this capacity hypothesis, so they are not fresh independent validation here. The proposed bank objective remains 3% worst relative projection error, separate from 5% physical online eligibility.

The anticipated allocation is one private GPU attempt in the existing namespace. The preceding fixed-rank run provides a rough planning guide; timing claims will use only controls paired within the new job. Exact configuration, expected runtime and successful local sub-minute controls must be sent for coordinator review before submission.

Glossary: **rank** counts available spatial feature columns; **latent dimension** counts the nonlinear unknowns solved online; **bank** is the learned coordinate network's spatial output; **head** maps latent inputs to coefficients; **function-preserving widening** adds available features while leaving the initial decoded field unchanged; **POD** is a normalized training-snapshot projection diagnostic; **QR** constructs the physical field metric; **matched** means equal measured optimizer-block effort under the declared rounding rule; **stationary** means the normalized gradient meets its threshold; **development** data may guide experiments, while final data remain unopened; **CG/DST** denote iterative and direct Poisson controls.
