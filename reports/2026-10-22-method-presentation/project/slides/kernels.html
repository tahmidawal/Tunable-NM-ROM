How does each solver part scale with r?

Cost grows with r, but not at the textbook rates

  • Declared exponents: nonlinear term 1, Jacobian 1, normal equations 2, Cholesky 3
  • Measured in 2D: 1.17, 1.08, 1.92, 1.24
  • 3D: 1.01, 1.03, 1.33, 1.03
  • At r ≤ 1024 the GPU is latency-bound, so Cholesky is much cheaper than r³

Lane p1-scaling · 26

The registered hypothesis H-cost-r fails as stated, but the practical conclusion is fine: cost is dominated by the m·M·r products, not by the Cholesky.
