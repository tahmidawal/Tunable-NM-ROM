**Overall verdict: NOT-READY.** A2 closes most round-2 defects, but the operator-equivalence check and bank-stop predicate remain blocking. Several qualification, reference-population, and control specifications also need completion.

This audit was read-only. I read the cited briefs, pilot and reuse code, and ran one small NumPy arithmetic check. No files changed, jobs submitted, arrays copied, or log entries written.

1. **Problem, families, pairing and cohorts — CORRECT.** The distributions reproduce the pilot’s wide family, and pairing the five base uniforms across cells is statistically sound. Distinct split seeds, row-level disjointness, retained row identifiers, and freezing all retained cells before test opening address leakage. A1 correctly acknowledges that width changes both translation variability and wall interaction. A2’s intersection masks and joint row resampling support paired cross-cell comparisons. **Concrete safeguard:** keep within-cell eligible-population statistics distinct from statistics recomputed on cross-cell intersections; label their populations explicitly. No blocking change here.

2. **References — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT; the deviation is honestly stated.** A2.2 explicitly disclaims compliance with COMMON-BRIEF §2 and flags the spectral substitution to the coordinator; I do not reopen that contract decision. The amended estimator is actually $\varepsilon_{\rm ref}=d_{3/4}+d_t+d_p$, with coefficient-space and lattice gates, separate test allowances, and disclosed padding extrapolation. That is defensible as a **conditional estimator**, not a bound; $U=2\max\varepsilon_{\rm ref}$ does not remove its assumptions. A4 remains provisional, appropriately, and the high-resolution IC rule matches the pilot’s documented correction. Two details remain: the roundoff branch tests only $d_{1/2}$, allowing an anomalously larger $d_{3/4}$ to escape the decay test; and training/`train-c` coverage remains inconsistent between A1’s “every split,” A2’s dev/val/test populations, the original four-training-trajectory check, and A2.10’s resource list. **Fix:** require both spatial differences to be negligible before waiving decay; specify training and centred-training gates and failure actions explicitly. Any statistics after excluding reference failures must be labelled as conditional on the eligible subset, including worst-error and stop-rule claims.

3. **Arms 4.1–4.6 — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT overall; one mathematical defect is blocking.**

   - **4.1 F-SPEC — CORRECT within a finite-search scope.** A2 checks the selected optimum’s resolution boundary, extends it, and labels remaining truncation. This is a credible fixed-step competitor search, although it cannot establish global optimality. **Fix:** explicitly retain A1’s extension when *no* feasible finalist exists, since A2’s selected-finalist trigger cannot operate then. Publish invalid-rung and cap termination reasons.

   - **4.2 F-SPEC-AD — CORRECT algorithmically.** Integer ticks, aligned coarsening, the initial level, coefficients through $J+1$, accepted half-step states, rejected-trial costs, and immediate failure when further refinement is required beyond $J$ define an executable algorithm. The omitted $1/15$ factor is honestly described as a conservative indicator. **Fix:** retain executable assertions for output-time alignment and charge controller and rejected-trial work in the official query. No new blocking algorithm defect.

   - **4.3 R-BANK — CORRECT in scope; adapter details remain implementation obligations.** Per-cell retraining on the prescribed eight cells, both LSPG and Galerkin ETDRK4, rank skips, independent bank rules, and disclosure of the capped test prefix satisfy the principal round-2 concerns. Incumbent-only LSPG tolerances are explicitly a scope limitation. **Fix:** the box adapter must set `SIDE=1`, the unit-box IC/envelope/rules and output lattice consistently; report actual $M_t/r$, especially when the effective bank rank makes the test space nearly square. Do not interpret failure of one trained bank as an architectural impossibility.

   - **4.4 R-PODG — WRONG in A2.3’s G-PILOT premise.** The claim that the 3/2-padded pseudo-spectral product is “exact for band-limited modes” is false for this two-dimensional Dirichlet sine projection. For example, with $u=\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y)$, the continuous coefficient of $u\,u_x$ in mode $(2,1)$ is $4/3$. The pilot’s discrete sine-analysis quadrature at $N=8$, $M=12$ gives $1.333412924276817$, a relative discrepancy of $5.97\times10^{-5}$. The transverse $\sin^2(\pi y)$ factor does not have a finite sine expansion. Refining only the Gauss rule cannot eliminate a fixed pseudo-spectral quadrature error. **Blocking fix:** independently refine both integration procedures, or use analytic trigonometric integrals as the continuous reference; distinguish finite-discretization differences from implementation errors. Define what happens if the comparison still fails. The new POD rank cutoff is a substantial improvement, but retain stable reorthogonalization and the registered mass/stiffness checks.

   - **4.5 R-TRANS — CORRECT as a static translation approximation.** It is a legitimate parameter-dependent transport-aware basis, with an explicitly approximate treatment of walls, charged shifted synthesis, and the right boundary-defect diagnostic. It supports conclusions about this particular static-shift model only. **Fix:** explicitly apply the chosen training-data gates to `train-c`; preserve sign and zero-shift tests.

   - **4.6 R-ECSW — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** A2 resolves the $q=12$ versus $q=16$ naming conflict and preserves full-parent vector/rollout qualification, rather than the reused support-successor diagnostic. However, round 2’s requests for exact training-row/snapshot indices and valid comparison rollouts are not fully closed. **Fix:** pin the 12 trajectory indices, 11 snapshot indices, candidate policy and seed; require valid ECSW, parent and successor rollouts explicitly. The existing `run_cell.ecsw_q2` remains unsuitable without replacement.

4. **Metric, matching, selection, timing, speed-up, crossings and $r_\times$ — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** The lattice metric, three-way matching, paired timing arrays, compilation checks, fused-query authority, and $\max_b\min_a S(a,b)$ are coherent. A2 fixes the six-candidate hole by continuing in batches and classifying capped searches as missing. Crossings no longer impute censored speeds. Separating $r_\times^{\rm eval}$ from $r_\times^{\rm query}$ also resolves the principal round-2 ambiguity. However, $r_\times^{\rm eval}$ uses only the point rule $q=12$, although cheaper point rules are selectable; this can understate affordable bank rank and promote a bank stop. “The FOM finalist” is also ambiguous when multiple fixed/adaptive finalists survive. **Blocking fix for its use in stopping:** specify the comparator deterministically and minimize bank evaluation cost over the declared eligible forms/rules, or explicitly restrict the resulting stop’s scope. Pin representative dynamic inputs for these kernel timings and burn in immediately before each timed block. Handle one qualifying finalist at search exhaustion explicitly.

5. **Q1/Q2 for every selectable arm — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT, with the main round-2 Q1 defect resolved.** A2’s non-Newton substitution is defensible: independent coefficients, stages and updates certify the implemented discrete map, while reference error measures time-discretization accuracy. Applying it to both FOMs—including adaptive rejected trials—and all reduced explicit arms removes the previous asymmetry. Certified implicit anchors and test-stage Q2 also close major omissions. These certificates do not independently validate a shared nonlinear operator, which makes the operator-check repair above necessary. **Fix:** explicitly require valid parent/successor comparison rollouts for every Q2 path, including ECSW; define the test-stage LSPG tight/tighter population and normalization; retain certificate replay identity. The imported code must be adapted: `finalize.q1` currently lacks certified anchors, and the existing selector does not gate all competitors on Q1/Q2. Those are implementation requirements, not evidence that A2 has already been implemented.

6. **Must-fail controls — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT; A2 introduces a calibration contradiction.** Positive controls, direct stage-NaN injection, the seventh-candidate fixture, test-Q2 failure, and frozen-artifact checks are useful improvements. But requiring every injected effect to exceed its checker threshold by $10\times$ is incompatible with the specified MF-Q2 successor-weight scaling. If $N_{\rm succ}=\alpha N$ with $\alpha>1$, then $\rho=|1-\alpha|/\alpha<1$, so it can never exceed $10(0.116)=1.16$, however much the multiplier is increased. Likewise, changing AD tolerance from $10^{-30}$ to $10^{-20}$ does not guarantee cap exhaustion when the computed difference is zero. **Fix:** corrupt the production vector or choose a calibrated fault whose normalized effect can cross the required margin; force an unacceptable trial at $j=J$ for the cap-path test. Define control-specific acceptance criteria for Boolean controls. MF-SETUP’s builder assertion is valuable, but its two-viscosity output comparison alone does not prove setup placement inside timing.

7. **Stop rules — WRONG for BANK-STOP; SPEED-STOP is substantially repaired.** A2 freezes the scheduled arm–cell set, distinguishes missing evidence, and requires a match for every retained arm. Under the pilot’s “targets reached by both methods” interpretation, censoring no longer supplies speed evidence, while an entirely censored arm makes the main speed stop unresolved. This appropriately biases against firing without evidence. Deferred coverage still needs an explicit restricted-scope label. **The blocking bank defect remains:** A2 fires if every *defined* entry is above, even when required cells have undefined $r_\times$. One defined high-floor entry plus undefined entries elsewhere can therefore stop the bank, contrary to “in every cell.” **Fix:** require defined affordability and above-threshold floors for every required non-deferred bank entry; any undefined entry makes the universal stop UNRESOLVED unless another entry already establishes NOT FIRED. A separate operational stop for “no affordable tested rank” belongs to the coordinator.

8. **Budget and defer order — CORRECT as a release framework, not a validated estimate.** A2.10 substantially closes the resource-scope omissions: centred data, POD construction, ordering, derivatives, tensors, qualification searches, test work, memory, failed-job allowance and headroom are included. The cap, concurrency and defer order match the brief; the two-cell second-data-size reduction is disclosed. **Concrete safeguard:** the eventual certificate must also count reference retries, FOM extensions, possible Gauss-rule doubling and up to 18 qualification attempts. Two-rank power-law extrapolation is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed upper bound across compilation and memory transitions. The preliminary 54 GPU-hours remains unsubstantiated, but that alone does not block design approval.

9. **Other interpretability and fairness threats — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** A2 improves eligibility reporting, provenance, timing outliers and separate test timing. Two reporting distinctions remain important: reference exclusions change the population, and a frozen finalist that fails test accuracy matching cannot retain a “matched test speed-up” merely because its runtime is measurable. Likewise, maximizing over frozen ROM finalists using test measurements would be new selection. **Fix:** freeze the reported validation-selected configuration/pair policy; report test feasibility and qualification before attaching a matched-speed label, with failures retained and no reselection. Label all truncated-search, eligible-subset and deferred-coverage conclusions at the claim itself. Finer-grid diagnostics appropriately qualify broader field-accuracy claims.
