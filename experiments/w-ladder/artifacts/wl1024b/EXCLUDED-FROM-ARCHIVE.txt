Every one is deterministic and regenerable:
  mesh_<n>.npz      the mass-QR bank, its stiffness/damping and the transferred POD basis on the query
                    grid; rebuilt by ladder.py rebuild()/pod_banks() from the frozen bank parameters.
  reference_*.npz   the exact semidiscrete DST solution from the saved initial fields (u0, v0 are kept
                    in the chunked copy of the same file for the non-full-field cases).
  <mesh>_<case>_<fom arm>.npz   the same-job full-order solver output for the one designated
                    full-field case; every other case keeps its common-grid (64^2) fields in the chunks.

All ROM arms keep their full coefficient trajectories in the chunks, from which their fields are
exactly reconstructed as coefficients @ bank.T -- which is what audit_ladder.py does. The audit was run
against the COMPLETE archive before this reduction; its result is audit.json beside this file.
