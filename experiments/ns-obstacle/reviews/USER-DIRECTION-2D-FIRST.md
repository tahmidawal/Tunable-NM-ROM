On 2026-10-28 the user asked: “Lets do 2D first and lets ensure it works in 2D.
Make that the goal”. The durable conversation goal is active. This document records
the resulting campaign-scope change; it does not replace the goal state or frozen
scientific design.

The active path is qualified 2D references, reproducible training export, learned bank
and ordered prefixes, fair POD baselines, actual reduced solves, off-mesh quadrature
validation, and velocity/pressure/force/constraint and matched-cost evaluation. The
existing report, generated plots, presentation and handoff obligations remain. A source
test, smoke control or plausible field image is not an end-to-end working ROM.

All 3D execution and further implementation are deferred. Preserve its incomplete
source-only diagnostic checkpoint and failed evidence, then release that lane. No
3D work is silently marked passed or abandoned as evidence. The 2D and ROM owners
continue in their separate existing worktrees; independent reviewers remain read-only.

The resource envelope, scientific thresholds, staged training gates, declared stop
rules, sealed-cohort closure and merge restrictions are unchanged. No performance
advantage is promised or manufactured. Gated extensions follow their prerequisite
2D pilot; they cannot substitute for completing that pilot.
