How do we compute u·∇u without the mesh?

Evaluate the nonlinear term at m fixed points

N(c) = Pᵀ [ (B·c) ⊙ (D·c) ]
B = G(x_q), D = (∂x + ∂y) G(x_q), P_qm = w_q·ψ_m(x_q)

  • (x_q, w_q): a classical quadrature rule (Gauss or Fibonacci lattice), the same on every mesh
  • Derivatives D come from automatic differentiation of the network: exact, no finite differences
  • B and D are computed once offline; online it is two matrix–vector products and one multiply
  • This is Hari’s off-mesh quadrature; m is chosen per bank by a qualification test
