We would like to highlight the new evidence addressing the motivation and solver-comparison concerns. Our intended use is repeated PDE queries within a parameterised family, with an accuracy–cost choice from one trained reduced model.

In the revised manuscript, the fast width-192 Navier–Stokes head achieves 5.1–10.2× speedups over the fastest accuracy-matched configuration from a 450-entry GPU pseudo-spectral solver ladder (Table 1; Appendix E.1). Figure G.3 also shows that increasing the Burgers 2D resolution from 256² to 4096² leaves reduced-solve time essentially unchanged at fixed reduced settings on an H200 GPU.

These results demonstrate the value of the reduced model in the tested regimes. We distinguish reduced-solve cost from mesh-dependent input/output costs and report cases where classical solvers remain faster. The revised evidence supports our contribution of query-time accuracy–cost control for repeated PDE solves.

We are also running GPU cuDSS and AmgX single-solve benchmarks and will report those results as soon as they are available.
