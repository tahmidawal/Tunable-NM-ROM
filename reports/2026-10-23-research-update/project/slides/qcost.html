Question

What do the quadrature points cost, and does that depend on the mesh?

Cost grows with the number of points, never with the mesh

  • Accurate: r = 384, 1536 tests; fast: r = 128, 512 tests
  • Every rule is flat from 256² to 4096²: ratio 0.94–1.06 over all 40 measured arms
  • More points cost more: Gauss 64² (4,096) → 96² (9,216) → 128² (16,384) → Fibonacci 17,711
  • At 4096²: Gauss 96² 54.5 ms against the old 63² lattice 46.3 ms (accurate); Fibonacci 1597 13.3 ms against 18.7 ms (fast)

Off-mesh quadrature study 2D, one H200 job, dev6 × 3 repeats; solve = query − decode · 6

Solve time is the median query time minus the median decode time, on one GPU, so it is the reduced-space work only. The old 63-by-63 mesh lattice (dashed) is on the mesh, so it is not a matched equal-accuracy comparison; the off-mesh rules are measured against the continuum and the lattice against the mesh. In the accurate setting the off-mesh rule needed to pass the quadrature check (Gauss 96²) costs a little more than the old lattice; in the fast setting Fibonacci 1597 is cheaper.
