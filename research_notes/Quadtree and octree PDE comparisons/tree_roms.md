# Spatial trees already appear in reduced models

Research synthesis, October 27, 2026. The completed Astra review distinguishes compression of stored data, acceleration inside an actual reduced solver, and projection of an octree-discretized PDE. These are not interchangeable claims. No new project experiment was run.

## Direct examples

**Philipp Krah, Thomas Engels, Kai Schneider, Julius Reiss, “Wavelet adaptive proper orthogonal decomposition for large-scale flow data” (Advances in Computational Mathematics, 2022).** [Publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10444-021-09922-2); [full preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.05016). Section 2.1 describes nested rectangular blocks with tree codes and leaf blocks, halving spacing in every direction: a dyadic spatial block tree/forest, geometrically quadtree/octree organization, not a clustering tree. POD is computed using wavelet-compressed snapshots and its modes have sparse adaptive representations. Section 4.2 includes a planar cylinder wake and three-dimensional flapping insect. **This is decomposition/reconstruction; an adaptive online POD-Galerkin solver is future work in section 5.** Tree overhead matters, and randomized alternatives can be faster when memory is not limiting.

**Dan Gerszewski, Ladislav Kavan, Peter-Pike Sloan, Adam W. Bargteil, “Basis Enrichment and Solid-fluid Coupling for Model-reduced Fluid Simulation” (Computer Animation and Virtual Worlds, 2014).** [Full paper](https://users.cs.utah.edu/~ladislav/gerszewski14basis/gerszewski14basis.pdf). Section 3.3.1, Feedback, explicitly constructs a quadtree over the background grid: the root covers the domain and leaves represent disjoint subgrids. A cell-center comparison decides whether obstacle-panel velocity feedback needs descent. This occurs inside **online reduced fluid simulation**, with analytic Laplacian eigenmodes enriched by data/authored modes. The tree accelerates obstacle feedback before projection into reduced coefficients; it does **not** define local reduced trial spaces. Demonstrations are two-dimensional; three dimensions are future work. Projection can lose panel detail or violate obstacle boundary conditions even if the unprojected combined velocity satisfied them.

**Janis Geise, Sebastian Spinner, Richard Semaan, Andre Weiner, “An octree-based sampling algorithm for analyzing big simulation data” (2025 preprint; Computers & Fluids, 2026).** [Full preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.21352v1); [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compfluid.2026.107261). Section 2 explicitly specifies quadtree/octree branching. Algorithm 1 constructs a time-invariant sampling tree from an existing-data metric, optionally refined by geometry. Sparse Spatial Sampling interpolates existing simulation fields, then permits POD/SVD analysis. **It is postprocessing/modal analysis, not a reduced governing-PDE solver.** Examples include a plane through a transonic tandem airfoil, a three-dimensional cylinder, and aircraft data. A metric computed from existing time histories is not an online PDE-error certificate. Interpolation at boundaries and thin geometry constrain accuracy.

**Jianmei Zhou, Yihao Wen, Wentao Liu, “Source decoupling and model order reduction for 3D full-time TEM modeling” (26th EM Induction Workshop, 2024).** [Complete one-page abstract](https://www.emiw.org/fileadmin/emiw2024/abstracts/2.0_source_decoupling_and_mod_zhou_01.pdf). Explicit finite-volume octree discretization, separated temporal current/spatial source vector, and operator/source-dependent shift-and-invert Krylov reduction. The governing equations are projected and the reduced systems solved. This is actual reduced physics on an octree, with a global classical Krylov basis, not local learned features. The complete available source is short: it supports classification, not a detailed performance audit.

**Kailiang Lu, Jianhua Yue, Jianmei Zhou, Ya’nan Fan, Xiu Li, Qinrun Yang, “Accelerated full-waveform time-domain electromagnetic forward modeling in underground full-space using an octree mesh and model order reduction” (Journal of Geophysics and Engineering, 2026).** [Publisher article](https://academic.oup.com/jge/article/23/3/1078/8566305). The retrieved abstract and introduction describe octree refinement, mimetic finite volumes for magnetic flux, source decoupling, and shift-and-invert Krylov reduction. This is an actual reduced governing-equation solve, independently consistent with Zhou et al. above. The introduction discusses grading/hanging nodes, staircasing, and layout complexity; a tensor mesh can be preferable for simple layered geometry. **Retrieval of later sections failed.** Do not imply complete inspection of algorithm, validation, or performance sections, or transfer its advertised acceleration to this project.

## Adjacent evidence and exclusions

**Rody A. van Tuijl, Joris J. C. Remmers, Marc G. D. Geers, “Multi-dimensional wavelet reduction for the homogenisation of microstructures” (CMAME, 2020).** [Full paper](https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/145741774/1_s2.0_S0045782519305377_main.pdf). Actual reduced mechanical-equilibrium solves with adaptive multidimensional wavelet representation/integration, stress and history transfer, and Newton iteration on localized two-dimensional microstructures. A literal quadtree/octree data structure was **not established**; classify as adjacent wavelet ROM rather than direct tree evidence. Parameter-space quadtrees, clustering trees, generic domain decomposition, geometry-only neural trees, and polynomial local tiles do not establish the requested spatial reduced-trial architecture. PolyStokes is a near-miss involving local tiles, with multilevel development left to future work.

## What our method would have to add

Trees, POD, and online reduced coefficients are established. The candidate extra mechanism is a reusable family-trained local dictionary that represents new instances with fewer active coefficients or cheaper accepted solves than classical local spaces. Coordinate evaluation is not unique to neural models: continuous POD and finite elements can do it too. Against the electromagnetic methods, the distinctive hypothesis would be cross-instance reusable learned structure; operator/source-dependent Krylov reduction remains a serious control.

A proposed field has a global component plus selected smooth local corrections,

$$
u=u_{\mathrm{bc}}+B_0c_0+\sum_{K\in\mathcal A}\chi_K\widehat B_\theta(T_K^{-1}x)_{1:r_K}c_K.
$$

Fixed-support rank prefixes are nested; spatial splitting is not automatically nested. Retaining existing windows and parent terms, adding children at zero coefficients, preserves the represented field. Renormalizing old windows can destroy that property. All transient histories require compatible transfer. Count active rank, entire stored dictionary, support evaluation, selection, and coupling costs.

First compare projection using equal physical weights, training-derived dictionaries, held-out locations/scales, and the same candidate trees. Include global/local POD and polynomial/spline controls. A selector allowed to inspect the target is a **truth-assisted best-case diagnostic**, not a deployable algorithm. Only an actual optimum establishes the associated oracle error bound; feasible greedy selection is not certified optimal, so its failure does not prove that all selections fail. For a fixed total linear dimension and training inner product, global weighted POD minimizes training mean-square projection error. Frozen neural features with solved linear coefficients do not evade that fact. State-dependent local selection instead accesses a larger union of spaces and incurs larger dictionary and selection costs.

Only after local approximation headroom is established should shared neural detail be trained. Hold windows, tree, tests, quadrature, and acceptance fixed when changing the basis. Moving features can activate many stationary patches; global complexity, dominant temporal error, or cheap conventional solves undermine the premise. The completed bounded October 27 Burgers campaign supplies no broad bank speed advantage; unsampled POD was admitted, while qualified hyper-reduced POD remains unestablished within tested policies. The latter is not evidence of a bank win. This search is not exhaustive and does not support a universal absence or first-method claim.

## Glossary

POD/SVD are linear data-compression constructions. A trial space contains allowable solutions. Krylov reduction builds a space from an operator and sources. TEM means transient electromagnetics. A dictionary stores candidate functions; active rank counts independent coefficients used for one solve. A prefix takes the first entries in an ordering. A window restricts spatial support smoothly. A union of spaces permits different subsets for different instances. Truth-assisted selection uses unavailable target information. Offline preparation precedes queries; online work occurs for each query. The equation labels boundary lift, global bank, active patches, windows, mapped local features, local ranks, and solved coefficients.
