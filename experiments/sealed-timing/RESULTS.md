## Glossary

- **Lattice ROM query**: the reduced-order model's full query (projection of the initial field, reduced solve, decode onto the 257² or 63³ scoring lattice).
- **Matched spectral solver**: the full-order pseudospectral configuration ($N$ modes per direction) that the closeout selected as the fastest one matching the lattice ROM's accuracy on the sealed cohort. Not re-selected.
- **Prepared input / preparation**: the solver's starting data on the GPU (initial field on the mesh for the ROM; sine coefficients for the spectral solver), and the work of producing it from the case description.
- **Prepared-input ratio**: ROM query time / spectral query time, both from prepared inputs.
- **End-to-end ratio**: (ROM preparation + query) / (spectral preparation + query).
- **Fused**: preparation and query timed as one call; should equal the component sum within 10%.
- **Block drift**: largest deviation of one repetition block's median from the median over all blocks.
- **Fold**: main-block median vs confirmation-block median, larger over smaller.
- **Paired per-case range**: minimum to maximum over the six cases of the per-case ratio.
- **Sealed / validation**: sealed cases were opened once in the closeout; validation cases were used for historical selection and are already exposed.
- **ST median error**: median relative trajectory error against the space-time reference, shown only to confirm the frozen pipeline reproduces on these inputs.
- **(a)**: the three 2D meshes whose closeout timing checks failed.
