Question

How do we get a dial r from one network?

Reorder the 512 shapes so the first r are the best r

  • QR of the shapes on a grid makes them orthonormal
  • SVD of all training coefficients sorts directions by how much they are used
  • Result: one rotation T; the first r columns are the best r-shape model
  • Median error (CN-R, 16 cases): r 128 → 0.69 %, 256 → 0.27 %, 384 → 0.25 %, 512 → 0.24 %; flat after 256 (the time step takes over)
This is what makes the bank tunable: smaller r is cheaper, larger r is more accurate, and no retraining is needed. 3D captures 99.97 % of coefficient energy with 128 shapes.
