## Plain-language glossary

Latent coordinates: numbers the solver adjusts to describe the field. Prefix: the first group of those numbers. Frozen bank: the fixed spatial functions used to draw the field. Head: the neural map from latent numbers to bank coefficients. Fine: the final64-coordinate problem. Temporal warm start: a prediction from earlier accepted fine states. Affine slice: adjust a small group while holding the remaining current values fixed. Replay: solve time steps again in order using corrected fine history. Stationarity: the objective cannot be reduced significantly by a small latent change under the stated certificate. Quadrature: points/weights used to evaluate spatial integrals. Same-job timing: both methods measured on the same allocated GPU. Conditional accuracy: stronger checks only after the cost screen passes. This is nested/restricted initialization, not genuine multigrid cycles.
