\section{Architecture Choices: Evidence from Sweeps}
\label{app:arch}

This appendix collects the evidence behind the architectural choices in
\S\ref{sec:decoder}: the matched-dimension head ablation on Burgers and
Poisson (Tables~\ref{tab:head-burgers}, \ref{tab:head-poisson}), the three
error layers per cell (Table~\ref{tab:layers}), the head-capacity sweep on the
frozen Poisson bank (Table~\ref{tab:head-capacity}), the head-retraining arms
(Table~\ref{tab:training}), and the solver variants for the corrections on
Burgers (Table~\ref{tab:solver-variants}). The linear skip is a design choice
adopted for cold-start convergence and is not ablated in this paper.
\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\resizebox{0.9\linewidth}{!}{\input{figures/architecture}}
\caption{NM-ROM pipeline. Colour says when each quantity is fixed:
purple, trained once and frozen for every result; blue, assembled
offline once per mesh; orange, computed inside the timed query; green,
chosen at run time among artefacts that already exist; grey, supplied,
or a comparator. This is a schematic, not a measurement. The only things
that change between operating points are the three green controls (the
rank $q$, the stored quadrature rule or dense evaluation, and the
tolerance), so every point of the family comes from one training run.}
\label{fig:arch}
\end{figure}

Figure~\ref{fig:arch} draws the pipeline;
Table~\ref{tab:arch-blocks} lists its blocks, their sizes in the symbols of
\S\ref{sec:method:manifold}, and when each is fixed.

\section{Glossary}
\label{app:glossary}

Written for a reader who knows none of this project's vocabulary.

\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=0pt]
\item[Bank, head, latent code ($\bank$, $\head$, $\latent$; $R$, $k$)] The fixed spatial functions the decoder combines (width $R$); the small network that maps $k$ latent numbers to their coefficients; the $k$ numbers solved for per query.
\item[Correction rank $q$, rung, ladder, $\corr$] The number of fixed extra bank directions the solver may add to the head's output; one value of $q$; the sequence of rungs; the matrix of directions, a nested prefix chosen offline. $q=0$ is the head alone, $q=R$ the linear model on the bank.
\item[Test count $M$, weak tests, row scaling] How many sine test functions the residual is averaged against; the functions; the diagonal weights on those averaged equations. All three change which solution is selected.
\item[$m$, EQ, rule, NNLS, fit states, reachable states] Quadrature node count; empirical quadrature, evaluating a sum over all nodes by a weighted sum over $m$ of them; one fitted instance; the non-negative least-squares fit; the states used to fit a rule; states the solver actually visits, as opposed to stored snapshots.
\item[$\rho$, primary and tight bars, confirmed, marginal] The rule's relative error on the projected advection term on held-out reachable states; $\rho_{\max}\le\nEqtopBar$ and $\le\nEqtopTightBar$; a construction every one of whose re-draws passes; one only some of whose re-draws pass. Never the NNLS fit residual, and never a single draw.
\item[Dense] Evaluating the advection term at every interior node, exactly.
\item[Evolved vs all-times metric, $t{=}0$ compression] Worst error over output times $t>0$; over all output times including $t=0$; the error at $t=0$ alone, which is the decoder's reconstruction of the supplied initial field and bounds the all-times metric from below.
\item[Same-grid, reference, physical error] Error against the converged full-order solve on the same mesh (isolates the reduction); against a much finer solve (includes the mesh's own discretisation error); the cell's declared accuracy metric.
\item[Three layers: bank floor, best-found, solved] Error of the best linear projection onto the bank (what nothing at inference can beat); the smallest error any point of the augmented manifold attains, found by a multistart oracle (an upper bound on the true minimum); the error the deployed iteration returns.
\item[Stationary, budget exit, early-stopped, converged, strict] The solve met the normalised-gradient tolerance; it hit its iteration cap; any output produced under a cap or stall; the pre-registered completion rule that also accepts an attained initial fit whose residual is at round-off; the earlier rule that does not.
\item[Admissible] Eligible for a frontier or a ratio: converged under the completion rule as written, with stationarity $10^{-6}$ at every step or a residual-rule exit, and for a quadrature subject a rule that passes its bar. Arms run at the loose tolerance $10^{-3}$ are reported everywhere but are not admissible.
\item[Non-dominated, frontier, error span, cost span] A point nothing else beats on both axes; the set of such points; ratio of largest to smallest error among a ladder's non-dominated converged points; the same for cost. The tunability bar: monotone, at least three non-dominated points, at least $2\times$ on both spans, nothing early-stopped.
\item[Same-allocation, same-job, cross-job] Two measurements inside one cluster allocation on one GPU, which may be divided; two from different allocations, which may not.
\item[Development cohort, held-out, sealed cohort] Cases opened for diagnosis or selection; cases excluded from training, which may still be used for validation; cases opened once after choices are frozen. The Burgers sealed cohort has been opened and is reported in Table~\ref{tab:sealed}.
\item[Pre-registered, amended] Declared before the observations used to test a claim; changed later in a dated design amendment. Poisson's deciding falsification interpretation and the wave tie band were amended after earlier observations; their literal and amended readings are reported separately.
\item[Checkpoint recency] Whether the best validation checkpoint falls near the end of the training budget; a training-progress heuristic, not a convergence proof or a bound on attainable error.
\item[POD-LSPG, $k'$, DST, CG, Newton] The classical linear reduced model on a snapshot basis of rank $k'$ solved through the same weak objective; the direct discrete sine transform solve, exact for separable constant-coefficient operators on a rectangle; conjugate gradients; the full-order nonlinear iteration for Burgers.
\item[FNO, U-Net, DeepONet, Transolver] Fourier, convolutional, branch--trunk and physics-attention solution-map models. Each is fixed once trained; its evaluation grid is a cost--accuracy control of its own (Table~\ref{tab:resolution}), but one that does not change what the model can represent.
\item[GPU median, p95, NF/NS, timing outliers] Median and ninety-fifth percentile of retained device query times; numbers of cases with nonfinite output or a failure of the recorded stationarity rule; calls above one and a half times their method's median. These summaries are distinct from accuracy or convergence guarantees.
\item[Direct transfer, native plus interpolation, projected operator] Evaluation of fixed weights on a changed mesh; evaluation on the training mesh followed by charged interpolation; an operator prediction followed by a charged divergence-free projection.
\item[Checkpoint, frozen, incumbent] Saved network weights; unchanged for every result; the one Burgers checkpoint every cell shares.
\item[Provisional, pending] A number that is real but rests on one draw, one seed or an unfinished replication; a placeholder for a run that has not landed.
\end{description}
