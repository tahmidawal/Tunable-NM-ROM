The common thread

The shapes are good enough. What costs us is the work per time step, the number of steps, and decoding

Measured in four lanes: two Jacobians per step, 33–43 points per unknown, steps that won’t certify on steep states, and a decode that outweighs a spectral query. Fixing the shapes (wider banks, steep data, wall-compatible envelope) moved errors by 1.1–1.4×.

The one lever nobody has tested properly is the time integrator together with step certification: an adaptive embedded scheme. That's the most defensible next experiment if we continue on Burgers.
