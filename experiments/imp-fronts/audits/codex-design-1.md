**Overall verdict: WRONG — amend and re-audit before implementation.** Reusing the seed-1 control is valid, and the PDE compatibility argument is correct. The blocking problems are the failing A2 control, incomplete qualification logic, missing fresh dev/validation splits, unsafe freshness-check scope, and a smoke that cannot certify the proposed budget.

Findings below distinguish demonstrated failures from controls whose behavior remains unverified.

**A1 — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** The smearing statistic correctly measures a *relative preference*: \(D_B>1\) means the reference/upwind floor ratio exceeds the equal-size sine ratio. The LOO construction avoids fitting the held-out case, and the existing eligibility records support the stated 46-case population. However, neither upwind-trained POD nor reference-trained POD guarantees the prescribed \(D\) thresholds on held-out cases; they are empirical comparators, not effects present or absent “by construction.” Controls at rank 128 also do not establish sensitivity at the primary bank rank 384. Selecting T6 by large bank-reference floors is explicitly descriptive, but enriches the numerator being investigated. **Fix:** retain the frozen thresholds and descriptive interpretation, label the POD controls empirical, add a constructed detector check, define zero/near-zero denominator handling and SVD tolerance, and specify how E0 fields are evaluated on the 256-node sensitivity grid. Do not silently interpolate them or retune thresholds after seeing control results.

**A2, PDE compatibility — CORRECT.** For the unforced scalar equation \(u_t+u(u_x+u_y)=\nu\Delta u\), homogeneous stationary Dirichlet data imply \(u_t=0\) and vanishing advection on a smooth wall. Thus \(\Delta u=0\); the tangential second derivative of the identically zero trace vanishes, giving \(\partial_n^2u=0\). This applies to sufficiently smooth positive-time solutions away from corners. It does not assert initial compatibility for the Gaussian IC with wall values overwritten to zero. The restriction to \(t_1,\ldots,t_5\) is therefore important.

**A2, curvature, tail and controls — WRONG.** The stated one-sided stencil is second-order accurate, but its truncation error need not be small relative to the curvature being detected. I evaluated C-A2+ exactly as specified on all 38 dev6/val32 references: **only 14/38 satisfy the required tenfold curvature increase**. Ratios range from approximately **1.00004 to 513.11**; dev6|0 gives approximately **2.24**. Consequently, this diagnostic necessarily returns “control failed” on the current data. Exact compatibility of a sine projection likewise does not imply the proposed finite-difference curvature bound relative to another field. The fitted finite-band tail slope also mixes boundary regularity, interior content and lattice resolution. **Fix:** use analytic second derivatives for decoded banks and manufactured compatible/incompatible functions to calibrate the stencil’s error; retain reference FD estimates with a resolution check. Separate mathematical identity tests from empirical sensitivity tests. Freeze the tail time range to \(k=1,\ldots,5\), numerical floor handling, and any replacement thresholds in an amendment before evaluating bank outcomes.

**A2, arm-(c) decision — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** The OR predicate is implementable, but the parenthetical impossibility claim is false. A decoded field’s tail can be reduced below the true solution’s tail by smoothing or truncation, potentially at the expense of accuracy. Conversely, boundary compatibility might improve \(m^*\) without meeting either proposed diagnostic threshold; the Phase-B endpoint itself permits an \(m^*\)-only success. There is also a wording conflict between “controls failed, therefore no decision” and the later instruction that arm (c) runs only following “RUN.” **Fix:** describe this as a budget-prioritization heuristic, remove the impossibility claim, and define three explicit outcomes: RUN, DEFER, and INCONCLUSIVE, including the control-failure branch.

**A3 — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** The verbatim stop rule and conversion \(0.1\) percentage points \(=0.001\) are correct. The difference of worst errors implements “worst error rises”; the separately reported maximum per-case rise is a useful distinct quantity. Full DST-I reconstruction is a valid identity control. But \(K=16\) is not guaranteed to increase the worst error: orthogonal truncation can improve an inaccurate ROM field, particularly on the unqualified regime runs. The design also leaves unclear whether the decision applies separately to all nine configurations or only the primary configuration, and whether a secondary-cohort failure stops anything. **Fix:** freeze those decision populations explicitly; retain \(K=16\) as an empirical stress test and add a constructed real-reference fixture with known omitted sine energy that must exceed the bar. The GPU timing gate may remain deferred, but a CPU-screen pass must not be reported as an F7 success.

**A4 — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** The F15 stop predicate, equality conventions, equal-dimension sine comparator and containment inequality are correct. The high-frequency hybrid is not a guaranteed must-fail control. Even high-frequency sines can cancel unwanted high-frequency components of bank functions and improve their combined approximation; negligible reference energy in those sines does not make the augmented span ineffective. **Fix:** retain that hybrid as an empirical negative comparator. Add a guaranteed null enrichment—duplicate bank columns, with rank-revealing orthogonalization—whose span equals \(B_{512}\) and therefore cannot satisfy the strict improvement over \(B_{512}\). Freeze rank tolerances and numerical equality handling.

**B0, copied rules — CORRECT.** The four rules match the binding lane brief. In particular, boundary-arm stopping uses AND, so continuation uses OR. The factor comparisons and the strict “more than 2×” safety guard have the correct directions.

**B0, operational qualification — WRONG.** The endpoint currently permits a coverage-arm success from finite but Q1/Q2-unqualified trajectories, whereas F11 explicitly requires re-qualified ROM error. Counting invalid errors as infinity is necessary but insufficient: an infinite control median can make a finite arm appear automatically successful, and naïve comparisons can even accept \(\infty\le\infty/2\). Missing \(m^*\), insufficient eligible references, and failed tail trajectories have no complete decision semantics. The common brief’s \(2\varepsilon_{\rm ref}\) ranking rule is also absent. **Fix:** define qualification and minimum-population prerequisites, explicit unavailable/inconclusive outcomes, and finite-control requirements before factor comparisons. Preserve diagnostic errors without allowing them to establish an arm success; implement the reference-uncertainty tie rule separately.

**B1, reuse of K1 — CORRECT.** The [seed-1 bank record](/home/tahmid/Dev/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/worktrees/2026-10-19-jcp-robust/experiments/jcp-robust/inputs/banks/s1/BANK.json) documents a production incumbent-recipe retrain: Stage A completed 300,000 steps, the emitted Stage-B head completed 200,000 steps without hitting its time cap, and the deployment rotation is recorded. **B1, “seed-robust” claim — WRONG.** Comparing one seed-1 intervention against three control seeds tests sensitivity to the comparator, not robustness of the intervention to retraining seed. **Fix:** call this “passes against all three incumbent retrains.” Reserve “seed-robust intervention” for replicated intervention retrains; these need not be added to this budget.

**B1(a), fidelity-only arm — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** Changing only the generator is a valid intervention, and the cited PS accuracy matches the existing report. The adapter is underspecified: [psjax2d.py](/home/tahmid/Dev/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/worktrees/2026-10-18-jcp-fom2d/experiments/jcp-fom2d/psjax2d.py) returns six outputs at 0.05 intervals, whereas training needs 51 outputs at 0.005 intervals; its \(N=128\) convention retains modes \(1,\ldots,127\), not 128 modes. Apply the change to every Stage-A and Stage-B data stream while preserving draws and state indices.

**B1(b), coverage-only arm — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** The viscosity transformation preserves the geometric/amplitude draws and contains the old parameter box; it is a valid single intervention when applied throughout the recipe. The statement “same 576 trajectories” is incomplete: [run_seed.sh](/home/tahmid/Dev/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/worktrees/2026-10-19-jcp-robust/experiments/jcp-robust/train2d/run_seed.sh) supplies **4,032 additional seed-1000 trajectories in Stage B**. Applying the change only to Stage A would not implement the stated coverage-only retrain.

**B1(c), boundary architecture — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** The antisymmetrized, integer-frequency construction is indeed odd and 2-periodic in each coordinate, hence boundary-compatible. It is reasonably described as one *architecture intervention*, but not as isolating boundary compatibility alone: the incumbent learns its frequencies, whereas this arm rounds and freezes them, changing capacity and optimization as well. JAX/NumPy agreement cannot detect a shared violation of periodicity. **Fix:** state that causal limitation and add independent oddness, 2-periodicity, wall-value and wall-second-derivative tests. Freeze initialization, normalization, optimizer masking and rotation behavior. A frequency-matched auxiliary control would be needed for a stronger causal attribution, not necessarily for this screening experiment.

**B2, bank identity and stop-evaluator controls — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** C-B2’s synthetic boundary/tie tests are appropriate; extend them to invalid, infinite, missing and inconclusive inputs. C-B1’s hash rejection is a genuine must-fail guard, but “wrong-bank decode ≥10× matched error on every smoke case” is not guaranteed, especially when the matched smoke bank is poorly trained. **Fix:** make deliberate checkpoint/rotation/hash mismatch rejection mandatory; treat decode degradation as supplementary empirical evidence, or use a fixed well-fitted coefficient fixture with a pre-established separation.

**B2, fidelity and coverage controls — NEEDS-RESTATEMENT.** C-B3 is a reasonable empirical generator check, but requires all dev6 and a stated aggregate-worst interpretation; two arbitrary trajectories do not execute it.
