## Glossary

- **Development cohort**: the cases on which settings were chosen and which the paper's Table 1 currently reports.
- **Test cohort**: held-out cases never used to train, select or develop anything. It is evaluated once, with settings frozen beforehand.
- **Span $R'$ / linear rung**: the query solves for coefficients of the first $R'$ columns of the ordered bank by one least-squares solve (no neural head, no iterations). `R128_linear` is $R'=128$.
- **$R'$, $q$ arms**: `R{R'}_q{q}` uses the neural head plus $q$ correction directions on an $R'$-column bank; `orig_q{q}` is the same model before rotation (parity reference).
- **Accurate / fast**: the two NM-ROM settings of a Table-1 row.
- **FOM**: the full-order model, matrix-free GPU conjugate gradients (CG) on the same mesh at a relative-residual tolerance. The row's FOM is the fastest tested tolerance whose worst error is no larger than the accurate arm's.
- **Speedup (×)**: FOM median time ÷ arm median time, from the same job.
- **Own FOM / own ×**: the fastest CG at least as accurate as that particular arm, and the speedup against it.
- **Worst % / median %**: the largest and the median, over cases, of the same-grid relative $L^2$ error against the exact discrete solution (SuperLU on the L-shape, a sine transform on the cube).
- **Bank floor($R'$)**: the best error any coefficients of the first $R'$ columns could reach (the projection error); no solver can go below it.
- **GPU query / complete query**: device work only, or device work plus the host→device and device→host copies.
- **Order gate**: a check that an arm is not slowed down when it runs right after a long CG solve (paired after-CG vs control timings; pooled ratio ≤ 1.10 and each arm ≤ 1.25).
- **Parity**: the rotated full bank reproduces the unrotated model to $10^{-10}$.
- **Audit**: an independent NumPy/SciPy recomputation of every error against its own truth. It also runs controls that must fail (a swapped case and a perturbed error).
- **Stationary**: the Levenberg–Marquardt (LM) solve ended at a stationary point (head arms only).
