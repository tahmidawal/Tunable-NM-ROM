## Glossary

- **Bank/head:** learned spatial functions / nonlinear latent-to-coefficient map.
- **Correction rank:** the number of additional online linear correction coordinates, denoted $q$.
- **POD/FOM:** linear snapshot basis / full numerical PDE solver.
- **Development/final:** opened selection cases / untouched evaluation cases generated after freezing choices.
- **Same-grid error:** prediction discrepancy against the converged numerical solver on the identical mesh and time step.
- **Trunk projection:** best linear fit in the learned DeepONet spatial span, distinct from its predicted coefficients.
- **Transversality:** a local check that head and correction coordinates do not become redundant.
- **Empirical refinement:** change under a finer numerical solve; it is not a rigorous continuum-error certificate.
- **Frozen offline arrays:** precomputed basis and correction data whose bytes are reused during final evaluation.
