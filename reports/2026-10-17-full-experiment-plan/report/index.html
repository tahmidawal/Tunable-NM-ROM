Full experiment plan for the JCP paper

Tunable bank + off-mesh quadrature · plan v4 (v2 + v3 + v4 amendments) · 17 October 2026

Status: draft, not pre-registered. This plan was written to answer the advisor's request to show that cost depends on the latent dimension, not on the mesh, and to test the method from every angle. Codex has audited it three times. Each audit said needs restatement, with fewer and narrower problems each round. v4 fixes the third audit's priority-1 blockers and sets concrete rules for the rest. A fourth audit has not been run yet. Each lane built from this plan still needs its own pre-registered design, a Codex audit and a real-size smoke test before it runs.

v4 (amendments)Not yet auditedFixes all four priority-1 blockers and gives concrete replacements for the two later ones: frozen mode lists in both dimensions plus a recheck of earlier results; the full Q1 contract certified in-job; a corrected inventory; a cumulative 20 GPU-h ledger; a corrected IC contract and scaling model; valid statistical tests.
A real finding from audit 3, not just a plan fix. In 2D, the test functions are picked by sorting eigenvalues that depend on the mesh. For up to 1,024 tests the chosen set is identical at every mesh, but at 1,536 tests (the accurate setting) it differs by 4 functions between 256² and 1024², and by 8 at 2,048. So earlier cross-mesh comparisons in the accurate setting used slightly different test spaces at different meshes. The effect is probably small, but it hasn't been measured. v4 freezes one mode list per setting and adds a cheap recheck (P1g) of the earlier "flat accuracy" results.

5 · Scoring and integrity rules (apply everywhere)

  • Selection only on development and validation cases (2D dev6 and val32; 3D 923801). The older test cohorts are for replication only. The sealed cohorts (2D: 128 physical cases; 3D: 64) and the reserved out-of-distribution cohorts stay closed until the final freeze.
  • References. The order-verified space+time reference (ST) is primary. The space-only reference (S) applies only to backward Euler at Δt = 0.005: that is 2D only, since 3D steps at 0.01, so 3D S is never used. Eligibility is looked up per case-kind (historical: 13 non-rankable, 12 S + 1 ST). Two arms rank on a case only if they differ by more than 2ε_ref. ε_ref is a conditional estimate and is labelled so.
  • Qualification is per exact configuration: bank hash, r, mode list, rule, mesh, scheme, Δt, solver, initialisation, weighting and metric. It is certified fresh in each job, at every step. The full contract:
    • every run valid (finite, no damping saturation);
    • stationarity γ ≤ 1e-3, with at most 1 % of steps failing, pooled;
    • within 2.5e-4 of a careful adaptive solve on every case;
    • careful and extra-careful solves agree to 2.5e-5 on a subset.
    Old qualification labels count as prior evidence only. Three labels are kept separate: numerical qualification, role qualification (for example "equal cost") and frontier usefulness.
  • Post-hoc labels carry forward: E0's changed cross-check rule; combined's post-hoc 3D rule selection; the retracted A15 selection.
  • Statistics: the case is the unit, and time snapshots are never treated as independent. Failures are reported, and seeds and draws get their own intervals.
  • Timing: paired A–B–A in one job on one GPU, warm and burned in, with timed outputs equal to scored outputs. Hardware is compared only through dimensionless ratios.
  • Checks come in three kinds: positive gates that must pass; negative controls that must fail (injected timing fault, doubled work, wrong-mesh tables, under-resolved rule, wrong-gradient solver, case or time swaps, bad lattice vector); and sensitivity probes that are reported only. Each says which claim it blocks.

7 · All experiment groups

Q · Qualification (runs first; gates everything on a headline curve)

  • Q1 solver: stationarity, distance to a careful solve (≤ 2.5e-4 on every case) and iteration counts, per configuration.
  • Q2 quadrature: agreement between successive rules for that bank, solver, Δt and own reached states, on both the integral-error and rollout-distance bars, with a memory pilot for large rules.
  • Q3 conditioning: conditioning of A, the weighted J and JᵀJ against r; fixes chosen on validation and labelled when they change the problem.

A · Cost

  • A1 cost against mesh: fixed-work, fixed-configuration and deployed-policy panels. In 3D, one fixed mode list is reused across meshes.
  • A2 per-component scaling against r, with FLOP and memory-traffic counts.
  • A3 factorial r × M × m: separates representation size from test-space and quadrature growth.
  • A4 points needed, m*(r, M): discrete candidates, both bars, own states; "none" is a valid outcome.
  • A5 peak memory against r, with table storage and the tensor line for comparison.
  • A6 offline cost (shared, per prefix, per mesh) and break-even, computed only against a qualified competitor.
  • A7 batching and A8 mesh-free path: owned by the 3D speed lane. A 2D version uses the same experimental contract.

B · Accuracy

  • B1 error against r × mesh, qualified configurations only.
  • B2 error budget: separate ablations plus interactions (not an additive split).
  • B3 every rule family as full solves, staged: existing families first, then Monte Carlo and 3D Halton after their code is audited.
  • B4 time order: observed, never assumed from the method's name.
  • B5 qualified accuracy–cost front: it need not be monotone, and failed configurations are shown separately.
  • B6 test space: M/r ∈ {2, 3, 4, 8}, M = r as a weak control, Galerkin as a different equation, and a low mixed-frequency test set.
  • B7 longer run times: needs new references beyond T = 0.25.
  • B8 physical diagnostics: boundary error, energy, overshoots, and whether the scoring grid misses fine features (needs a finer observation route).

C · Competition

  • C1 full-simulation ladder: upwind/BE, central-CN and spectral, across Δt × tolerance × FOM mesh. The comparator is chosen on validation, and every match is labelled as a finite-ladder upper bound.
  • C2 crossover: both the same-mesh crossover and the best accuracy-matched one (FOM on any mesh).
  • C3 standard ROMs (moved up to priority 2): first a minimal POD-LSPG with ECSW and Q-DEIM; later GNAT, POD + exact tensor, EQ on our bank, and a CROM-style sampler. Equal tuning budgets.
  • C4 decoder ablation: matched data, budget, rank and norms.
  • C5 linear span vs nonlinear head: repair the head or drop the "nonlinear manifold" claim. The head-size K sweep goes here if the advisor means K.

D · Generality (each is its own development project)

  • D1 parameters: pre-declared ν and IC ranges, using newly drawn development cases. Reserved out-of-distribution cohorts stay closed.
  • D2 Allen–Cahn at full scale. Predicted: no advection-stencil gap.
  • D3 exponential nonlinearity: the time-dependent Frank–Kamenetskii problem is new work; a steady Bratu study is an optional smaller step.
  • D4 NS 3D off-mesh: not implemented; needs divergence and pressure checks.
  • D5 geometry: a domain that demonstrably lacks a fast separable transform (for example L-shaped or curved). Mixed boundary conditions alone don't qualify.

E · Robustness

  • E1 randomised rules: lattice shifts and Sobol scrambles. Coverage is checked for the deployed single draw, and a bad lattice vector must be caught by an independent family. The 2D code must first expose its seed.
  • E2 seeds: repeat the full recipe (training, ordering, selection) 3–5 times.
  • E5 hardware: paired within one GPU; dimensionless results compared across A100 and H200.

F · Final confirmation

  • F1 global freeze manifest covering every lane on the roster: E0, mechanism, time2, wide-bank, smooth-bank, combined, speed3d, this plan's lanes and the C3 baseline. It freezes:
    • banks, mode lists, metrics and cohort membership;
    • code versions;
    • every threshold, fallback and comparator.
    It is hashed and Codex-audited. D2–D5 and C4 are excluded unless they freeze first.
  • F2 open the sealed cohorts once: 2D 128 physical cases (256 case-kinds) and 3D 64 (128). This requires the freeze, a decision on key custody, and building the opening tool.
