The per-job verdicts are consistent with §5+A1. **A2 supports an additional empirical result, but cannot turn the original failed primary test into a pre-registered success.** No files changed. Local reads failed because of a sandbox error; this audit uses the supplied excerpts.

1. **High — A2 changes the decision procedure after failure.** Requiring an arm to pass both jobs strengthens its observed coverage and prevents rescuing `scaled`. However, A2 promotes previously unselected alternatives after A1.3 explicitly prohibited promotion following confirmation failure. It is a **post hoc follow-up with fresh replication**, not simply a stricter original rule. **Fix:** retain “original $256^2$ primary verdict: no certified rule”; report A2 separately as an amended follow-up, registered before its new data.

2. **High — “Confirmation used only after selection” is false for the combined result.** A2 filters candidates using both confirmation draws before choosing the cheapest survivor. Those draws therefore participate in selection. Likewise, the original 56 trajectories informed A2 and are no longer untouched validation data for that amended procedure. **Fix:** distinguish selection data, fresh replication data, and genuinely untouched confirmation; remove the universal glossary claim.

3. **High — The statistical confidence statement needs selection qualifications.** The approximate $3/n$ bound applies to a fixed method tested on independent trajectories from the stated distribution. Selecting among passing arms does not automatically preserve that bound for the winner. Neither 56 nor 112 may simply be substituted as an independently validated sample size. **Fix:** describe observed coverage counts; use independent post-selection testing or a justified simultaneous inference procedure for confidence claims. Reused trajectories across meshes and the two query populations do not add independent samples.

4. **Medium — No numerical transcription discrepancy found, but “every number verified” is unsupported.** The headline rows match `reports/summary.json` at displayed precision; arm statuses and reported $\rho$ values match the supplied arm summaries. However, the excerpts omit the underlying complete FOM and per-arm timing/error summaries, so every “Every arm” entry cannot be independently checked. **Fix:** distinguish verified transcription from source-level verification; “generated” alone does not establish correctness.

5. **Medium — $\rho$ columns mix population scopes without saying so.** For bc256 `scaled`, the report shows 0.0994 under $k\ge j$ and 0.1653 under “all $k$,” although $j=0$. The summaries contain both values; this is consistent with including confirmation trajectories in the latter, rather than changing the step range. At $1024^2$, the analogous values are 0.0958 and 0.1157—materially different margins below 0.116. **Fix:** label trajectory scope and step scope separately, show confirmation maxima, and include the required $k\ge1$ diagnostics for $j=0$ arms.

6. **Medium — The lane failure and required companion metrics are omitted.** Selected accurate rows achieve development errors below 1%, but GPU ratios are only **0.085×, 0.091×, 0.319×**: every mesh fails $S\ge5$ and is slower than its comparator. Host-inclusive ratios and current-relative errors are available but absent despite §5/A1.6. **Fix:** state the speed verdict explicitly and add those columns. The displayed FOM choices are consistent with the eligibility rule.

7. **Medium — Scope qualifications should accompany headline claims.** The report correctly acknowledges dev6 and the different hybrid method, but “CERTIFIED” remains stronger than a standalone row supports. The $256^2$/$512^2$ winners use an exact first step; coverage concerns sampled per-step states, excluding LM trials and predictors. **Fix:** label headline rows “empirical per-step coverage; exact-first-step hybrid; dev6 accuracy,” and specify the five scored output times.

8. **Medium — Retraction language should target claims, not valid measurements.** Explicitly retain the withdrawal of §3’s “every state at which the rule is evaluated.” Retract any characterization of A2 as the original pre-registered success, or of `scaled` as finally certified. **Do not retract** bc256’s negative verdict or its measurements. Its marginal control satisfies A1.5’s **not confirmed** gate; update “control must fail” to that precise meaning.
