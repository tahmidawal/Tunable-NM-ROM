This strong baseline is retained. The ordered 512-column bank admits matching prefixes 128 and 256; ordering is not a frequency hierarchy. Separate: denser output evaluation adds no solved unknowns; nested iteration changes bank width r; physical mesh continuation changes N, moments and residual weights; true coarse correction corrects fine-solve error. Here N, M, m, quadrature, timestep and final tolerance remain fixed. No mesh continuation or geometric-multigrid claim.

The inherited off-mesh target uses analytic advection, mesh discrete diffusion, and weighted weak least squares. This deviates from AGENTS' FOM-exact upwind preference; preserve the actual archived target for an explicitly algebraic acceleration diagnostic, not a PDE consistency or physical accuracy claim. Fine anchor is the tight solution of precisely this target, not truth. No speed claim against FOM/POD.

## Derivation
At each time step, with full fine histories fixed, minimise $f(c)=\frac12\|S R(c)\|_2^2$, $J=\partial(SR)/\partial c$. $S_{ii}=(a_0+\Delta t b_0\nu\lambda_i)^{-1}$. Injection $P=[I;0]$ and restriction $R_c=P^\top$ match the same rotated bank. $B_fP=B_c$ exactly. A different bank would require weighted field projection $c_f=(B_f^TW B_f)^\dagger B_f^TW B_c c_c$, checking rank, field loss and timing; not used here.

Nested iteration solves $\min_z f(Pz)$ from the predictor prefix, then zero-pads and finishes the identical fine target. Safeguard compares with the ordinary predictor and chooses lower fine objective. Full history is retained; this is a reduced subproblem for each fine time step, not a separate coarse trajectory.

Genuine algebraic two-level nonlinear correction: one fine LM pre-step, then solve $\min_e f(x+Pe)$ starting at zero, then fine post-solve. Coarse gradient is $P^TJ(x+Pe)^TSR(x+Pe)$; at zero it is exactly the restricted fine gradient. No tau is necessary because the coarse objective is the exact pullback (tau zero); no Galerkin replacement or square PDE root is used. Damped coarse normal matrix is $P^TJ^TJP+\lambda\operatorname{diag}(P^TJ^TJP)$. Conditioning of J and JP and squared-normal conditioning are reported. Acceptance requires finite descent in the fine objective, otherwise exact fallback to x. This is subspace correction; no smoothing property is assumed. It may cost more than direct fine solves.

## Design-gate corrections (before data)
The same-width control checks transfer only, not optimization; zero correction alone is the no-op. One intervention per step. A full fine convergence check bypasses all coarse/pre work if the predictor already meets the common criterion. The two-level fine pre-step has at most one update, followed by up to 600 post-updates (601 total cap, reported explicitly); nested has no pre-step. Coarse starts with fine damping, but its final damping never overwrites fine damping. Rejection restores the pre-intervention state and damping. After a converged pre-step the coarse stage is skipped. All incurred checks/evaluations remain charged. Direct target is unchanged; extra budget is an explicit intervention allowance, not an iteration-count speed claim.

Every norm is Euclidean on the identical native mesh interior divided by the initial field Euclidean norm (uniform physical-volume factors cancel). Agreement gates use all 50 evolved internal states, all dev6 and all repetitions. Tight and tighter must pass fresh autodiff stationarity at their own requested gamma (allow 1e-12 absolute numerical slack), max field distance 1e-6. Each production step must pass its own gamma plus 1e-12; agreement with tighter anchor 2.5e-4 / 2.5e-6 for loose/strict, respectively. Objective comparisons are diagnostic: own-history and common-anchor-history residual norms are separately saved; relative squared-objective disagreement is reported with denominator max(anchor objective,1e-30), not used to invent uniqueness. Jacobian and coarse-gradient relative discrepancies <=1e-9 (denominator max(reference norm,1e-30)); transfer/no-op <=1e-12. Negative zero-budget must leave at least one first-step gamma above 1e-3. Outliers: nonfinite output, failed certificate/agreement, and per-case intervention/direct time ratio >1.1; every case retained.

Before submission source/input SHA256 manifest pins checkpoint, rotation and code. All deterministic constructed modes/rules/cold arrays and physical descriptors are saved with exact hashes and numeric trust before first solve; construction is prescribed by frozen code. CNR retains its two full BE startup steps. No independent quadrature/PDE qualification is inferred.
