Which quadrature rules work?

Gauss and Fibonacci lattices; random points never qualify

  • Gauss reaches qualification at 9216 (r 256) and 16,384 points (r 384)
  • Sobol, Halton and Monte Carlo never qualify at any tested m
  • Fewer tests (smaller M) do not reduce the points needed
  • The rule must be re-selected for each retrained bank

Lane dims; FR:270–291; robust E2 · 27

Random-like rules converge as one over root m; Gauss and lattice rules converge much faster for smooth integrands, which ours are.
