# Sample-free Burgers residual via a precomputed quadratic tensor — 1D scaling test (2026-08-29)

Does a precomputed quadratic tensor make the 1D Burgers ROM residual sample-free at oracle
accuracy?  This note records the experiment that answers it: a `tensor` arm added beside the
committed `oracle` / `base_tight` (NNLS $m=32$) / `nodes_tight` (learned $m=32$) arms of the 1D
scaling ladder at $N \in \{128, 256, 512, 1024\}$, from the **committed** checkpoints and node
sets (no retraining, no NNLS refit, no node training).  Numbers are final for this single seed
(seed 0, test seed 1, 8 test trajectories); every table below is generated by
`runs/b1dtensor/gen_tables.py` from the run JSONs (`runs/b1dtensor/tables.md` is its raw
output).  Nothing here is hand-typed.

**Branch** `exp/2026-08-29-b1d-tensor`, worktree `worktrees/2026-08-29-b1d-tensor`, code in
`experiments/separable-decoder/`.  **Cluster namespace** `/cluster/tufts/paralab/tawal01/b1dtensor/`
(job dirs deleted after the pull; checksums verified).  **Jobs** 3032356 (N=128), 3032357 (N=256),
3032359 (N=512), 3032362 (N=1024), all `COMPLETED 0:0`, `gpu` partition, `--gres=gpu:a100:1`,
`jax_backend=gpu`, `JAX_DEFAULT_MATMUL_PRECISION=highest`, f64, commit `7ce4d6c`, manifest
checked in-job.

## Answer in one paragraph

**Yes, to $\le 1.2\times10^{-6}$ absolute in rollout error, with identical solver behaviour, at no
cost premium.**  At every $N$ the tensor arm's per-trajectory rollout error equals the full-grid
oracle's to $2\times10^{-7}$–$1.2\times10^{-6}$ absolute (pass bar $10^{-5}$), its stop-reason
histogram is identical to the oracle's in total and per trajectory, and its latent path deviates
from the oracle's by at most $2\times10^{-4}$ (N=128) down to $5\times10^{-6}$ (N=512, 1024).  It is
**not** bit-exact ($10^{-9}$ would have required every LM candidate to be a positive field) because
60–68 % of the LM candidate states — accepted and rejected — have at least one decoded grid point
with $u \le 0$ (min $u \approx -8\times10^{-3}$), where the tensor's fixed backward difference and
the oracle's sign-upwind stencil differ.  On the ~36 % of decoded training states that are positive
everywhere the agreement is $8\times10^{-15}$ (exact).  Against the sampled rules the tensor is
1–9 % more accurate than NNLS-32 and at parity ($\pm 2$ %) with learned-32, with no NNLS, no node
training, no $m$ to choose.  End-to-end it runs 10–20 % faster than the oracle and **at parity with
NNLS-32** (ratio 0.975–1.054): in 1D this is an exactness-and-simplicity result, not a speedup —
as expected.

## What was run

### E1 — local CPU audit (`b1d_tensor_audit.py`, `JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu`, jaxrun, ~8 s per N)

From each committed checkpoint: build $T$ in two chunk orders (TB), check the algebraic identity
$h^\top T h = \Phi^\top(u \odot D^- u)$ on all 8192 decoded training states (TA), the oracle
identity on the all-positive states (T0), mismatch statistics on all states (TS), the Jacobian
mismatch and gradient cosine at 32 unperturbed + 32 perturbed ($0.05\,\mathcal N(0,I)$) states (TJ),
the contraction condition $\sum_{jk}|T_{ijk}h_jh_k|/|q_i|$ (TC), and a host-loop LM rollout of the
8 regenerated test trajectories with **every LM candidate** (init, accepted, rejected) probed for
sign pattern and tensor-vs-oracle mismatch of $q$, $r$, $J$ (TL).  The host loop was cross-gated
against the device reference rollout (latent deviation $\le 8\times10^{-13}$).  JSONs and the
built $Q$ tables are in `runs/b1dtensor/audit/`.

### E2 — four A100 jobs (`sep_b1d_tensor.py`, one job per N, four in parallel)

One invocation per $N$: load the committed checkpoint + node npz, regenerate the 8 test
trajectories from `TEST_SEED=1` with the tridiagonal truth generator, run gates J / T2 / E / F / C /
G / V plus TB / TX / TA / T0 / TS / TQ, then the arms `oracle`, `base_tight`, `nodes_tight`,
`tensor` (lean fold, the production fast configuration) and `tensor_nolean` (the oracle's fast
configuration without the lean fold — the like-for-like timing vs oracle).  For every arm BOTH the
verbatim reference rollout (`jacfwd`, `lax.cond`, LU) and the optimized rollout were run,
interleaved, `BURN=2` + `TIME_REPS=5`, every repetition persisted, accuracy read from the last timed
invocation.  Gate D (node-training FD-vs-AD) does not apply — there is no node training — and was
skipped.

### Definition of the tensor arm

Interior bank $G \in \mathbb R^{n_i \times R}$ (bc applied), $(D^-G)[x] = (G[x]-G[x-1])/\Delta x$ with
$G[-1]=0$ (exactly `upwind_adv_field_1d`'s backward branch),
$T_{ijk} = \sum_x \Phi_{xi}\,G_{xj}\,(D^-G)_{xk}$ built in f64 blocked over $x$ (chunk 256; never an
$n\times R^2$ temporary), stored as $Q = T + T^{(j\leftrightarrow k)}$ ($32^3$ f64 = 256 KiB).
Residual advection term $q(h) = \tfrac12\,h^\top Q h$ replacing $\Phi^\top N_{\text{upwind}}(Gh)$;
everything else (exact linear terms $A=\Phi^\top G$, weights, $\Delta t$, $\nu$, $\lambda$)
verbatim the oracle's.  Jacobian by AD (`jacfwd` in the reference path, `jax.linearize` in the
optimized path); $\partial q/\partial z = (Qh)\,\partial h/\partial z$ is what AD computes.

## What failed, what is provisional, what to watch

- **Nothing failed.**  All four jobs completed on the first submission; every gate passed; all
  checksums verified; cluster dirs deleted.
- **Exactness is conditional, as Codex-2/3 predicted.**  The tensor is exact only on the positivity
  cone.  60–68 % of LM candidates (and 60–63 % of extrapolated inits) touch a $u\le0$ point, so the
  "exact" label formally fails; the *practical* label is "oracle to 6–7 digits in rollout error".
  The residual mismatch at those candidates is median $4\times10^{-7}$–$7\times10^{-6}$, max
  $1\times10^{-4}$–$4\times10^{-4}$ relative, and the Jacobian mismatch max $5\times10^{-5}$ — all
  far below the decoder floor and below the NNLS-32 held-out mismatch (6 %).
- **Gradient cosine needs care.**  The raw min cosine of $J^\top r$ over LM candidates is $-0.4$ to
  $-0.8$, but only at LM *stall* points where $J^\top r \approx 0$ while $|r| \gg$ tol (the 50-step
  histograms are ~99 % reason 2 = stall); the direction of a vanishing gradient carries no
  information.  Restricted to non-stationary candidates ($|J^\top r|/(\|J\|\,\|r\|) > 10^{-2}$) the
  min cosine is $\ge 0.999996$ and the scaled gradient mismatch $\|g_T-g_{or}\|/(\|J\|\|r\|)$ is
  $\le 4\times10^{-5}$.
- **Contraction condition** $\sum_{jk}|T_{ijk}h_jh_k|/|q_i|$ has a max of $2$–$6\times10^{8}$ (median
  $\sim 600$) — but that max is driven by individual high-mode entries $q_i$ that are themselves
  tiny; normalised by $\max_i|q_i|$ of the state the ratio is $\le 120$.  With f64 this costs at most
  ~3 digits of the 16 available; no evidence of cancellation harm appears anywhere in the rollouts
  (TA at $10^{-14}$ is the direct test).
- **Timing is single-seed, 8 trajectories, median of 40 samples per arm.**  Ratios within a job are
  reliable to a few %; the N=256 job ran on an **A100-PCIE-40GB** (pax051), the others on A100 80GB
  PCIe, so its absolute ms should not be compared across N (the committed baseline column shows
  the same-arm A100 numbers from the scale jobs for reference: within 1–3 %).
- **Off-manifold states (TQ perturbed half, $z + 0.05\,\mathcal N(0,I)$, beyond the LM trust radius
  $\approx 0.017$)** show larger mismatch (max $5.6\times10^{-4}$, min $u \approx -0.3$).  Rollouts
  never visit them (trust region), but a solver with a larger step could.
- **The reference-path (jacfwd) and optimized-path (linearize, lean fold) tensor rollouts agree to
  $\le 4.6\times10^{-8}$ relative / $9\times10^{-8}$ latent** — the same envelope as the committed
  fast-path parity.  The lean tensor fold saves 2–4 ms of rollout vs the non-lean tensor path.
- Not done (out of scope by instruction): other N, multi-seed, 2D port, analytic (hand-coded)
  Jacobian timing path, the fixed-sine-bank training job (E3), the FOM-choice study.

## Glossary

- **Bank $G$ / head $h(z)$** — the two halves of the frozen separable decoder: $R=32$ spatial
  shapes tabulated on the interior grid, and the small network mapping the latent $z\in\mathbb R^8$
  to $R$ mixing amounts; $u = G\,h(z)$.
- **Test modes $\Phi$, $M=32$** — the lowest discrete sine modes the residual is projected onto
  (exact eigenvectors of the Dirichlet Laplacian, so linear terms are exact through $A=\Phi^\top G$).
- **Oracle** — the arm whose advection term $\Phi^\top N(Gh)$ is summed over the full grid with the
  FOM's sign-upwind stencil; the best any sampled rule can do.
- **base_tight / NNLS-32** — advection term sampled at $m=32$ grid nodes with non-negative weights
  from a capped Lawson–Hanson fit; **nodes_tight / learned-32** — the same after gradient-matching
  node training (both from the committed npz).
- **tensor / tensor_nolean** — this experiment's arm, with / without the "lean" kernel fold of the
  optimized rollout (the fold merges the head's last layer with the $A$ projection).
- **ref / fast** — the verbatim reference rollout (jacfwd, `lax.cond`, LU) and the optimized rollout
  (linearize, Gauss–Jordan, no cond, unrolled scan); identical algorithm, both timed.
- **$T$, $Q$** — the $M\times R\times R$ advection table and its $(j,k)$-symmetrised form.
- **$D^-$ / sign-upwind** — the fixed backward difference; the FOM's stencil that looks left where
  $u>0$ and right otherwise.
- **Undershoot** — a decoded value $u \le 0$ where the truth is positive; the only place the tensor
  and the oracle differ.
- **LM candidate** — a latent state tried by the Levenberg–Marquardt solver (accepted or rejected).
- **Stop reasons** — why an LM solve ended: 0 budget (30 attempts), 1 tolerance reached, 2 stall
  (relative decrease $< 10^{-3}$), 3 $\lambda$ hit $10^{12}$, 4 already converged, 5 non-finite.
- **Rollout error** — mean over the 51 states of a trajectory of the relative $L^2$ error of the
  decoded field vs the FOM truth; the "err" columns average this over the 8 test trajectories.
- **Latent dev** — max absolute difference of the latent path $Z\in\mathbb R^{50\times 8}$ between
  two arms or two implementations.
- **e2e** — IC fit + latent rollout + full decode, ms per trajectory, median over 8 traj × 5 reps.
- **Gates** — J: analytic tridiagonal Jacobian == jacfwd; T2: tridiagonal truth == dense truth;
  E: sine modes are exact eigenvectors; F: weak operators == $\Phi^\top$(FOM residual);
  C: continuous node machinery == grid stencil at the grid init; G: Gram-space IC fit == banked IC
  fit; V: device rollout == host-loop rollout; TB: build order; TX: in-job $Q$ == E1's CPU $Q$;
  TA: $h^\top Th == \Phi^\top(u D^-u)$; T0: tensor == oracle on all-positive states; TS: mismatch
  stats on all states; TQ: tensor vs oracle residual/Jacobian at 32 states (recorded, not asserted).
- **Contraction condition** — $\sum_{jk}|T_{ijk}h_jh_k| / |q_i|$, the cancellation a floating-point
  sum of the quadratic form suffers.
- **Trust radius** — the LM step cap, $0.01\times$ the training-code radius ($\approx 0.017$).
