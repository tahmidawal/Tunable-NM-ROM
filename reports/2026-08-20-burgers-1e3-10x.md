# Burgers 1e-3 / 10x pure NM-ROM search — Phase 1

This report covers the preregistered transported Hermite-Gaussian Phase-1 search and calibrated Burgers FOM. All accepted numbers are **final**; the outcome is a rigorous negative at the preregistered representation hard stop. The untouched model-validation and confirmation draws were never opened.

## Outcome

**[final negative]** Neither transported analytic decoder can represent the locked 64-case selection trajectories closely enough to justify training. The best oracle is HG5 at mean 2.103e-02 and worst 5.267e-02, respectively 105.1x and 75.2x above the fixed representation gates. Its authoritative nearest-wall squared-error fraction is 0.380216, below the 0.5 license for the conditional wall chart. Therefore HG4 training, HG5 training, HG5S/D1, weak/EQ tuning, ROM scaling, and confirmation were all stopped rather than run on a failed representation.

The inherited seed-0 H160x4/g2 artifact copied into D0 has full-weak mean 1.039e-02 and worst 2.175e-02; this specific row is still far outside the new 1e-3 target and has no eligible paired 10x point. No claim is made that it supersedes the broader earlier three-seed architecture summary. The new HG5 number is an oracle, not a deployable Pareto point.
