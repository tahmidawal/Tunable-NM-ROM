WHAT THE EXPERIMENT IS (short): heat 2D, u_t = kappa lap u, u=0 walls, backward Euler
dt=0.005 x 50, CG per implicit step. A hard-BC FiLM coordinate auto-decoder u(x;z) is the
nonlinear manifold; per implicit step the latent z is solved by warm-started damped
Levenberg-Marquardt (LSPG) or damped Newton on the Galerkin root, on the FOM's own discrete
backward-Euler residual. The "recipe" under test projects that residual onto the M lowest
DISCRETE sine modes (exact eigenvectors of the FOM Laplacian, so the weak form is exact for
this linear PDE) with weight (1+dt*kappa*lam_i)^-alpha, and hyper-reduces the mode projections
to m quadrature points with non-negative capped-NNLS ("EQ") weights on grid nodes (eq<m>) or a
meshfree random pool (eqoff<m>). Controls: strong-form full-grid residual (fd), POD with the
SAME solver, a direct reduced POD-Galerkin (k x k solve per step), and an "oracle" floor from
latents fitted to the held-out fields.
