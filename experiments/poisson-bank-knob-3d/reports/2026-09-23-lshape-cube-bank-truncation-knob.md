# Nested bank truncation $R'$ as the tunability knob — L-shaped and 3D Poisson

This report covers the frozen L-shaped Poisson model ($K=16$, $R=512$) and the frozen 3D Poisson model ($K=16$, $R=128$). It measures the nested bank truncation $R'$ on each Table-1 mesh, with no retraining. Only runs that passed their NumPy audit and every gate appear below. The $32^3$ and $64^3$ rows are the reserved final cohort, evaluated once with settings frozen on development data. All other rows are development cohorts. Generated by `make_report.py` from `reports/summary.json` (sha256 `06dd3d2005e869fa`).

**Coverage.** Measured: cube 32, cube 64, cube 256, L-shape 256, L-shape 512, L-shape 1024. **Not yet available (provisional report):** cube 128, L-shape 2048.

## Method in one paragraph

The frozen bank $G\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times R}$ is rotated once, offline and from training states only. Write $G=Q_GR_G$ at the training mesh and stack $a_i=[Q_G^\top u_i;\,R_Gh(z_i)]/\lVert u_i\rVert$. The SVD $A=USV_s^\top$ gives $T=R_G^{-1}V_s$ and $L=V_s^\top R_G=T^{-1}$. The columns of $GT$ are then ordered by training energy. A query at truncation $R'$ decodes $u=(GT)_{:,1:R'}\,(L_{1:R'}c)$, and its weak residual uses $BP_{R'}$ with $P_{R'}=T_{:,1:R'}L_{1:R'}$ ($P=I$ exactly at $R'=R$). The *linear rung* drops the head and solves the least-squares problem $\min_a\lVert BT_{:,1:R'}a-f\rVert$ directly ($q=R'$). It needs more weak tests than unknowns, $M>R'$. With the L-shape's $M=257$ eigenmode tests it therefore does not exist at $R'\in\{384,512\}$.

## Where the speed comes from

The selected arms change two things at once relative to the paper's settings: the truncation $R'$, and the family (NM-ROM with the head, or the linear rung with the head dropped). The table separates the two. *Truncation gain* = the time of the same-family arm at $R'=R$ divided by the selected arm's time; it is the part that $R'$ alone buys. *vs paper setting* = the time of the paper's arm at the same $q$ (for the cube at $n\le64$ this is the dense-projection `poisson.engine`, elsewhere the unrotated parent) divided by the selected arm's time. At $32^3$ and $64^3$ the accurate arm is the full-rank ($R'=R=128$) linear rung: its gain over the paper setting comes from dropping the head (no LM) and from the DST query route, not from truncation. The L-shape linear rung has no full-rank counterpart ($M=257<R$), so its truncation gain is not defined.

## Glossary

- **$R$ / $R'$**: $R$ is the number of columns of the frozen spatial bank. $R'\le R$ is the number of rotated columns a query keeps.
- **$q$**: number of nested correction directions whose weights are solved in closed form.
- **$K$**: latent dimension of the nonlinear head.
- **NM-ROM arm**: the head plus $q$ corrections, solved through the truncated operator $BP_{R'}$.
- **linear rung**: the head is dropped and the $R'$ rotated columns are fitted directly by least squares ($q=R'$).
- **parent / parent engine**: the unrotated model at full $R$ (`orig`), and the unchanged paper-p3d query with dense test projection (`engine`, cube $n\le64$ only). Both exist for the parity gate.
- **n/c**: not constructible. The linear rung needs more weak tests $M$ than unknowns $R'$.
- **floor %**: worst relative $L^2$ distance from the truth to the span of the first $R'$ rotated columns, on the same cohort. No arm restricted to those columns can go below it.
- **err %**: worst (over the cohort) relative $L^2$ error against the converged same-grid discrete solution.
- **ms**: median query time over all retained repetitions and cases, in the series' Table-1 scope. Complete query means host array in to host array out. GPU query means the device work only.
- **FOM**: the matrix-free conjugate-gradient solver at the stated relative-residual tolerance (rtol). The row's FOM is the fastest tested tolerance whose worst error is at most the accurate arm's.
- **own-FOM speedup**: the arm is compared with the fastest CG that is at least as accurate as the arm itself.
- **paper fast bar**: worst error of the current paper fast setting ($q=0$, $R'=R$) in the same job. The fast arm must not exceed it.
- **development / final**: development cases may be used to choose settings. The final (held-out) cohort was evaluated once, with settings frozen beforehand.
- **order gate**: every arm is re-timed right after a long CG solve and in an interleaved control without it. The gate requires the pooled paired ratio and the median ratio to the main phase to be $\le1.10$, and every arm's paired ratio to be $\le1.25$ (DESIGN amendment A1).
- **parity**: the rotated arm at $R'=R$ must reproduce the unrotated parent field to $10^{-10}$ relative.
