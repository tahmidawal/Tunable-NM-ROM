# Independent review: experiments

Structured summary of the subagent review of the frozen manuscript; not a verbatim transcript.

**Assessment:** Weak reject / substantial revision, approximately 4/10. **Confidence:** 4/5.

## 1. Fixed-test causal evidence is one development checkpoint

**High; Missing evidence.** PDF pp.7–8,18; Table5 and D.1.

The sealed multi-seed schedule changes test count with rank. Repeat fixed-M on independent frozen cohorts or narrowly distinguish the two claims; define the preregistered criterion.

## 2. FOM-only comparisons cannot isolate nonlinear restriction

**High; Missing evidence.** PDF pp.1–2,5–8; main.tex:117–128,377–384,606–615.

Use a small same-bank internal ablation or state nonlinear necessity is unproven. FOM-only external comparisons need not change.

## 3. CG gains are not gains against the strongest applicable FOM

**High; Scope limitation.** PDF pp.5–7; Tables1,6; appendix C.1.

Identify the tested preconditioner/tolerance set, explicitly limit claims to named CG controls and avoid unsupported cross-allocation scaling attribution.

## 4. Earlier Burgers speed panel needs its own full configuration

**High/medium; Configuration omission; arithmetic verified.** PDF p.6 Table2; gen_rewrite_tables.py:63–80.

Twelve-call raw-source recomputation confirms both tight and relaxed ratios. Add q/M/m and checkpoint/stopping distinctions; do not use the historical permissive-stall result as corrected stationary-ladder evidence.

## 5. Essential 3D experimental definitions absent

**High; Verified omission.** PDF pp.6–7,16–17; Tables3–4,C.1–C.2.

## 6. Announced problem coverage exceeds PDF evidence

**Medium; Verified scope mismatch.** PDF p.5; main.tex:377–385.

NS2D and low-viscosity Burgers are named without displayed outcomes; wave3D is absent and Heat3D lacks FOM. Narrow setup promises or give precise supporting records.

## 7. Near-break-even timings have no dispersion

**Medium; Missing uncertainty/generalization evidence.** PDF pp.6,8,18; Table3.

Report timing IQR/paired interval, cases and repeats; timing repeats are not training seeds. Keep development labels and source-only seed evidence distinctions.

## 8. Nonlinear cases do not establish useful physical speed advantage

**High; Limitation of practical evidence.** PDF pp.7–8; Table4 and limitations.

Burgers3D fails physical refinement, NS and wave miss targets, and efficient FOMs dominate current nonlinear panels. Position as a tunability study unless a prospective physically resolved nonlinear win is obtained.
