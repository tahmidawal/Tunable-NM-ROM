\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

One trained decoder can expose a monotone accuracy--cost family at run time,
and the control that does it is the number of fixed bank directions the solver
is allowed to add to the neural head's output. With the test count held fixed,
that rank alone spans $\nQxmErrSpan\times$ in error for $\nQxmCostSpan\times$
in cost on 2D Burgers, every rung converged, inside one allocation; certified
quadrature rules and the stopping tolerance then move cost at fixed accuracy.
The trade exists because the residual is nonlinear in the coefficients; where
it is linear the corrections are free, the top rung is a linear model and the
cheapest point, and on waves the learned bank loses to POD at matched rank.
None of this beats a tuned full-order solver or a well-trained neural operator
on these 2D problems, and the reduced model's case rests on the mechanism, the
structural condition and residual-based consistency rather than on winning.
Two practices carried the work: reporting every result as bank floor,
best-found and solved error, and certifying quadrature rules on reachable
states rather than by their fit.
