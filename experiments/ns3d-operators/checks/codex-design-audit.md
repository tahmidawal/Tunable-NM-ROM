Static review only; no files changed, GPU jobs run, or network commands used. Paths below are relative to `experiments/ns3d-operators/` unless stated otherwise.

1. **Major — The memory probe does not establish that training fits.** `train_op.py:168–186` probes only forward/backward; AdamW states and optimizer-step temporaries are absent. Model construction also occurs outside the OOM handler. At `train_op.py:263`, checkpoint reload allocates another model while the training model, gradients, and optimizer remain resident. **Fix:** probe complete accumulated optimizer steps, including a second step with optimizer states already allocated; cover construction with OOM handling; release training objects before reload.

2. **Major — Directory creation has a duplicate-submit race.** `cluster/stage.sh:7–10` separates existence checking from `mkdir -p`. Concurrent submissions can both pass, then `rsync --delete` into the same directory and submit two writers. `train_op.py:111` also accepts existing output directories. **Fix:** atomically claim the remote job directory with plain `mkdir`, abort on failure, validate arguments and source cleanliness before staging, and refuse training when output artifacts already exist.

3. **Major — Interrupted runs can be labelled successfully trained.** `train_op.py:44–46,240–265` handles signals only through a flag, performs full validation afterward, and ultimately writes `complete=True` even for `stop_reason='signal'`. `cluster/train.sbatch:6,22` provides no advance signal or explicit forwarding arrangement. This conflicts with `DESIGN.md:123–124`, which excludes time-limited arms. **Fix:** configure an advance signal with sufficient checkpoint grace, ensure Python receives it, check cancellation during generation/probing/validation, and record interrupted arms as ineligible for selection.

4. **Major — The comparison inherits evaluation-set selection asymmetry.** `DESIGN.md:27–41` correctly discloses unequal frame counts and the bank’s inclusion of 16 validation trajectories. However, the frozen NM-ROM’s **latent dimension was selected on the very development cohort used for this comparison**: `../ns3d-shift-head/head_ladder.py:569–580`. Operators select sizes on separate validation cases. **Fix:** explicitly label the 32³/64³ results as development comparisons favouring an already selected NM-ROM; do not describe them as an unbiased, identical-data-and-selection comparison. Generalisation claims require a cohort unused for either method’s selection.

5. **Major — “Disk-capped” has no enforceable byte cap, and the audit is restricted.** `DESIGN.md:158–168` specifies saved subsets and free-space checks, but no numerical per-job/aggregate cap or reservation against concurrent writers. At larger meshes, most full-grid errors receive only a sampled check with a 20% tolerance; independently regenerating \(u_0\) does not validate evolved truth. **Fix:** precompute worst-case storage, enforce explicit caps and reserve space, deduplicate truth, and stream full-field audits where possible. Otherwise label the audit restricted and add an independent evolved-truth check on selected cases.

6. **Major — Failed timing gates do not explicitly invalidate speedup claims.** `DESIGN.md:145–155,174–175` says failures are printed, but does not prohibit headline ratios from failed panels. Two initial burn-in calls also do not ensure warmed clocks after later host work or arm changes. The referenced panel/audit implementations are not yet present. **Fix:** make failed or missing gates force diagnostic-only status; define burn-in before timed blocks, persist sample/case/order metadata and physical GPU identity, and enforce finite outputs, positive timings, coverage, and timed-output parity in the eventual auditor. The shared FOM rule is coherent, but its ratios must be labelled **against the NM-ROM-accuracy comparator**, not equal-accuracy speedups for every operator.

7. **Minor — The pinned-host fallback loses its intended transfer behaviour.** `train_op.py:124,142` pins the dataset, then advanced-indexes it into a new CPU allocation; that gathered tensor is ordinarily pageable, so `non_blocking=True` does not provide the intended pinned asynchronous transfer. Pinning also temporarily duplicates the dataset—about 61 GiB combined at 96³—while `submit_train.sh:4` accepts any host-memory request. **Fix:** gather into a reusable pinned batch buffer and enforce a mesh-specific host-memory minimum.

8. **Minor — Budget and checkpoint bookkeeping need clearer boundaries.** `train_op.py:240–264` may exceed 3000 seconds by one optimizer update plus validation/checkpointing; final reload/evaluation is excluded from `training_seconds`. Final evaluation also closes over `norm`, ignoring loaded `norm2`. **Fix:** define and record training versus finalisation time, reserve validation time if the cap is strict, pass checkpoint normalisation explicitly, and verify reproduced validation equals the saved best value. The wall-time cosine and 160-step warm-up otherwise match the cited 2D implementation.

9. **Minor — The U-Net equivariance claim is too strong.** `DESIGN.md:88–90` and `ops3d.py:16–18` claim translation equivariance, but stride-two pooling and upsampling (`ops3d.py:98–110`) break equivariance to arbitrary one-cell shifts. **Fix:** qualify the statement. Circular padding, omitted coordinate channels, the periodic DeepONet trunk, declared FNO mode counts, and the roughly 4096-token Transolver are otherwise defensible adaptations; static inspection does not establish an unfair architectural handicap.

**Verdict:** Hold production launches until the memory-probe, directory-race, and interruption-status issues are fixed. I found no operator evaluation-data leakage: the split, rounded-row exclusions, training-only scales, evolved initial-relative loss/validation criterion, output scaling, and exact operator \(t=0\) pass-through are consistent. The Leray projector matches the FOM algebra, and the float64 spectral-convolution file is byte-identical to the 2D reference; the declared precision policy is implemented. Training can proceed after those operational fixes and the comparison-status clarification, but same-allocation speedup claims additionally require implemented, enforced panel gates and the audit/storage amendments above.
