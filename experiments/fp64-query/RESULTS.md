### Replacement sentences for the rebuttal (numbers generated from the job JSON)

> With every array operation in double precision (checked by a dtype audit of the traced program), our frozen online solver answers a Burgers 2D $256^2$ query 6.9-7.5x faster than our original solver at widths 96-384, with median error unchanged (within 0.3 %), all timed in one A100-80GB job on the 64 test cases (6.8-7.5x on 64 fresh cases). The gain comes from reusing one Jacobian factorisation per query (a lagged-test-space Petrov-Galerkin solve), not from reduced precision: the single-precision stencil behind our earlier 5.5-10x figure changes no per-case error by more than 2e-05 relative and accounts for only a 1.03-1.40x time difference. At width 176, the double-precision query reaches 0.121 % median error in 3.47 ms, against FNO 0.22 % in 4.06 ms and U-Net 0.21 % in 7.38 ms measured in the same job (1.17x and 2.1x faster at about half their median error). The operators retain a lower worst-case error (0.48-0.50 % against our 1.70 %).

## Glossary

- **width (R')**: number of bank columns (reduced unknowns) of the linear rung.
- **baseline**: the unchanged query of the query-speed lane (about one Levenberg-Marquardt step per time step, Jacobian and Cholesky every step); float64 throughout.
- **accurate / fast**: the frozen lagged-operator settings of the query-speed lane (one Jacobian and one factorisation per query, fixed corrections per step; lagged-test-space Petrov-Galerkin). "fast" is a separate, less accurate point. Without suffix they run the stencil in float32 (mixed precision).
- **_fp64**: the same setting with the stencil contraction and advection in float64; every array op float64 (verified by the precision audit).
- **median % / worst %**: median / maximum over cases of the per-case error, the maximum over the five evolved output times of ||u - u_ref|| / ||u_ref(0)|| against the tight full-order reference, in percent.
- **ms median**: median single-query GPU latency over all timed invocations (warm-up excluded); **ms 5-95 % of cases**: range of per-case median latencies; **min-max**: over invocations.
- **x vs baseline**: baseline ms / arm ms, same job. **fp64 / mixed time**: float64 arm ms / mixed arm ms.
- **median / worst vs baseline**: arm error / baseline error of the same width, same job.
- **drift gate**: ratio of the two A phases of the A-B-A timing (must be within 1.1); **neighbour gate**: the query-speed timing sanity gate on adjacent invocations (limit 1.1).
- **Q6 precision audit**: every variable of the traced JAX program of the query, counted by dtype.
- **test64 / fresh64**: 64 test cases (params_draw(20260916,64)) and 64 fresh cases (params_draw(2026111402,64)).
