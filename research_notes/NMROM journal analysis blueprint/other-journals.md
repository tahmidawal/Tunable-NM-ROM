# Other journals and conference comparators

Research completed November 18, 2026 by the read-only `related_journals` researcher. Journal HTML was inspected for the JSC papers; author or publisher-version manuscripts were inspected for the others. These notes record literature observations, not reproductions.

| Paper | Publication and primary access | Relevant analysis |
|---|---|---|
| Fresca, Dedè and Manzoni, *A Comprehensive Deep Learning-Based Approach to Reduced Order Modeling of Nonlinear Time-Dependent Parametrized PDEs* | JSC 87, 61 (2021), [DOI and journal HTML](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10915-021-01462-7) | Reduced-dimension, data-size, architecture and loss-weight studies; comparisons with POD reconstruction/local POD-Galerkin. Its parameter/time predictor differs from an online physics solve. |
| Fresca and Manzoni, *POD-DL-ROM: Enhancing deep learning-based reduced order models for nonlinear parametrized PDEs by proper orthogonal decomposition* | CMAME 388, 114181 (2022), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2021.114181), [institutional full text](https://re.public.polimi.it/bitstream/11311/1190168/2/11311-1190168_Fresca.pdf) | Figure 1 shows a linear outer basis with nonlinear compression. Tables 4/5 compare pretraining; a flow-rate quantity supplements field errors. |
| Romor, Stabile and Rozza, *Non-linear Manifold Reduced-Order Models with Convolutional Autoencoders and Reduced Over-Collocation Method* | JSC 94, 74 (2023), [journal HTML](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10915-023-02128-2) | Figures 5/12 compare reconstruction with POD; Figures 8/15 vary samples and report mean/maximum time error. Tables distinguish decoder/offline costs. Section 5 discusses failures, optimizer limits and baseline implementation speed. |
| Geelen, Wright and Willcox, *Operator inference for non-intrusive model reduction with quadratic manifolds* | CMAME 403, 115717 (2023), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2022.115717), [author full text](https://kiwi.oden.utexas.edu/papers/Quadratic-manifold-dimension-reduction-Geelen-Wright-Willcox.pdf) | Sections 2/3 separate decoder fitting from inferred dynamics; Figures 7/12 compare equal-dimension dynamics and time traces. A non-neural nonlinear comparator, with a different dynamics identification procedure. |
| Diaz, Choi and Heinkenschloss, *A fast and accurate domain decomposition nonlinear manifold reduced order model* | CMAME 425, 116943 (2024), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2024.116943), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.15163) | Section 4.2 analyzes SQP; Section 6 gives conditional error results, including an inverse-Lipschitz assumption. Table 3 compares linear/nonlinear and full/hyper-reduced variants. |

The following are conferences, not journal papers: Chen et al., **CROM**, ICLR 2023 ([project/publication record](https://crom-pde.github.io/), [paper](https://peterchencyc.com/files/crom/2206.02607.pdf)); Berman and Peherstorfer, **CoLoRA**, ICML 2024 ([PMLR record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/berman24b.html), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14646v2)). Both are close comparisons for continuous neural representations; distinguish CoLoRA's equation-driven and data-driven variants.

Proposed Tunable analyses: representation/dynamics separation; frozen-bank ordering ablation; weak-test sensitivity and physically scaled Jacobian diagnostics; residual/Jacobian quadrature error at deployed states; same-weight mesh transfer with continuous POD; matched-accuracy complete-query costs; long-time physical behavior; training-size/seed sensitivity. All proposed outcomes are hypotheses. A nonlinear head inside a fixed bank cannot beat that unrestricted bank's globally optimal reconstruction in the same norm.
