\begin{abstract}
We study separable learned decoders whose nonlinear latent coordinates admit
exact preassembly of the linear and polynomial parts of a weak discrete PDE
residual, and we characterise the accuracy, cost and discretisation limits of
removing fitted empirical quadrature from the repeated reduced evaluation.
