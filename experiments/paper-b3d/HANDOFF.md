## Glossary

- **Same-grid error:** discrepancy against the converged solver on the identical numerical mesh/time step, normalized by the supplied initial-field norm.
- **Correction ladder:** increasing online correction rank while all trained decoder weights and other frozen choices stay fixed.
- **POD/FOM:** a linear snapshot basis / the full numerical PDE solver.
- **Frontier:** measured methods for which no compared method is both cheaper and at least as accurate, or more accurate and at least as cheap.
- **Stationarity:** the recorded normalized local-gradient stopping rule; sampled gradients are independently recomputed.
- **Empirical refinement:** observed change after refining space or time, not a rigorous continuum-error certificate.
- **Empirical quadrature:** a fitted sparse integration rule; not used in this final panel.
- **Hard-link duplicate:** a separately named file restored from an earlier archive entry with exactly identical bytes.
