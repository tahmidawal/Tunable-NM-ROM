# Independent review: prospective P4/g4 reference role

## Bottom line

The P4/g4 role is scientifically sound as a scope for source preparation. The common-denominator envelope is valid, with conditions listed below. Nothing shown so far supports admission. Every post-P4 control is still prospective.

Two things need fixing before results are seen:
1. The three-way comparison must be defined so the triangle inequality actually holds.
2. The remedy to apply if the P3 contrast fails should be declared in advance.

Lift is the most likely binding quantity, and the likely cause would be the P3 comparator, not P4.

## 1. The envelope argument

**What is valid.** Let a = |P4g4 − P3g4|, b = |P4g4 − P3g3|, g = |P3g4 − P3g3| and d_p = max(a, b). Then g ≤ a + b ≤ 2·d_p. This holds only if all three numerators use the same norm, the same evaluation operator and the same denominator.

**Conditions it needs (required):**
- **A common evaluation set inside both discrete domains.** The g3 and g4 discrete boundaries differ between nodes. Field-norm points near the cylinder must lie inside every mapped domain, or use one documented inverse-map/extrapolation rule applied identically to all three pairs. If each pair builds its own point set, the inequality is not guaranteed.
- **Pressure poles.** (.15,.2) and (.25,.2) lie exactly on the circle. Confirm they are geometry nodes in both g3 and g4 meshes, or that pole evaluation never extrapolates.
- **One denominator everywhere.** Use the adopted P4/h3 denominator for d_h as well, not "the finer result" pair by pair. The numerical effect is negligible, but the rule should be exact and stated in advance.
- **Matched provenance for P3/g3.** The retained P3/g3 h3 solve predates the current code (native output controls, quadrature changes). Either rerun P3/g3 in the same reviewed package as P3/g4, or show that a reproduction matches the retained arrays to noise level. Otherwise g mixes geometry with code-version differences.
- **Per-solve validity for P3/g3.** It may be used as a measurement despite its failed lift checks. It still needs passing residual, trace, mass and Jacobian checks, plus its own noise floor.

**Overstated inference.** The statement that g is "covered by the existing two-times-p envelope" is weaker than it sounds. The factor 2 is the one safety margin for unmeasured order and h error, and here it absorbs geometry as well. The true error decomposition is roughly order error plus geometry error, which could reach a + g ≤ 3·d_p.

This is acceptable under the conditional-empirical framing, mainly because g measures the g3 error and so probably overestimates the g4 error. The wording should change to "measured g ≤ 2·d_p; the factor 2 is shared, not an independent geometry margin." Also report g against budget/5 on its own. If g alone exceeds budget/5, treat the evidence as inconsistent and trigger the P4/g5 remedy.

**Geometry metrics.** Area (2.5e-14) and perimeter (2.6e-13) are integral quantities and can superconverge. For the "finite resolved fixed-order geometry diagnostics" the role requires, also retain:
- maximum pointwise radial deviation, and
- maximum normal-angle deviation

at the boundary force quadrature points for g3 and g4. These are the quantities that actually feed the forces. They are diagnostics, not new thresholds.

## 2. Concrete blockers and required fixes before execution

1. **Declare the remedy branches before results.**
   - If a (the order contrast at fixed g4) exceeds the budget, the failure is driven by solution order. P4/g5 cannot fix it. The scientifically appropriate remedy would be a higher-order contrast such as P5/g4 versus P4/g4 at h3. That would need a separately reviewed amendment and resource proposal, with the P3 results retained and disclosed.
   - If only b or g is large while a is small, the issue is geometry, and P4/g5 is the remedy.
   - Do not decide afterwards to drop the P3/g3 contrast from the max.
2. **Check that the solver-tightening control is feasible.** The DFG independent residual is already 2.1e-15. If the baseline nonlinear tolerance is near round-off, a 100x tighter tolerance may be unreachable (spurious failure) or met trivially (no-op). Before release, state the baseline tolerance, the stopping criterion, and the achievable floor. If 100x is infeasible, any replacement specification (for example, fixed extra Newton steps or iterative refinement) is a reviewed amendment, not a silent substitution.
3. **Define "resolved."** If the exact noise multiple used to call a difference resolved is not already frozen, fix it now, before any noise data exist.
4. **Noise floors on every constituent, not just the adopted state.** The current plan measures d_solver and d_quad on P4/h3 only. Resolving the earlier h-difference needs noise bounds at h1 and h2. Resolving the p-contrast needs noise bounds for both P3 solves. These are cheap and the margins are probably large, but they must be measured, not assumed.
5. **Raw FE trace value.** Both cases report exactly `1e-12`. That looks like a floor, clamp or formatting artifact, not a measurement. Retain the raw value.
6. **Comparing pressure across domains.** There is no pin, so the absolute pressure level shifts with domain length (Poiseuille gradient × added length) and with outlet form. Across domains, compare only shift-invariant quantities: velocity, drag, lift and the pole Δp. The frozen domain metrics already follow this; make sure no diagnostic compares raw pressure across domains.
7. **Discretization in the domain controls.** Extended meshes should reproduce the original [0,2.2] mesh exactly and append elements, or else measure and report the remeshing difference. Otherwise the domain difference is contaminated by discretization differences.

## 3. Evidence needed before admission, by role

**Noise:**
- solver 100x
- assembly +4 / volume diagnostic 12→16
- boundary rule 12 vs 16
- then d_solver and d_quad, with raw numerators and denominators retained

**Domain:**
- upstream xmin = −.2
- downstream xmax = 4.4
- separate symmetric-traction long outlet

Each is checked against .002 relative (velocity, drag) and 2e-4 absolute transverse. These are problem-definition adequacy checks for the fixed DFG domain. They are not part of delta_y, and the formula should not acquire a domain term.

**Population.** Re20 does not cover Re25: the wake is longer and the boundary layers thinner, so h and domain sensitivity are probably worst there. Upstream elliptic influence is probably strongest at Re15. A protocol to freeze before any population result:
- **All 15 states:** per-case gates (residual, mass, trace, divergence, force consistency, balance, backflow) plus the P4 h1/h2/h3 trend and the P3/g4 h3 contrast. This is likely affordable and avoids monotonicity arguments.
- **Re15 and Re25 (plus Re20):** full noise controls and the P3/g3 geometry contrast.
- **Domain extremes:** downstream and symmetric outlet at Re25, upstream at Re15, in addition to Re20.

Qualifying validation states as reference truth is not ROM tuning. Any reference remedy they trigger must be applied population-wide, not case by case. Check the 12 GPU-hour total before freezing this protocol.

## 4. Optional, not scientifically necessary now

- **P5 controls:** only under branch 1 of blocker 1.
- **P4/g5:** only under the geometry branch.
- **Graded downstream extension beyond x = 2.2** to relieve the near-guard memory projection. This needs its own resolution argument.
- **Observed-order reporting:** current ratios are about 9.8 (velocity), 6.6 (pressure), 8.0 (drag), 4.0 (lift) and 5.2 (divergence), below asymptotic P4 rates. Report these, but do not gate on them.

## 5. Recommended bounded sequence

0. Freeze, without running anything: the comparison operator and denominators, the resolution criterion, the remedy branches, the solver-tightening feasibility specification, and the population protocol.
1. Independent raw audit of the existing P4 arrays: raw trace value, pointwise geometry deviations, surface rule 12 vs 16, backflow, wall-shear sign, manufactured flux and force controls.
2. Re20 noise controls on P4/h3, plus cheap noise brackets on h1/h2 and on the P3 solves.
3. Re20 p/geometry matrix: P3/g4, plus P3/g3 rerun or reproduced. Compute a, b, g and delta_y.
4. Only if step 3 passes: Re20 domain controls, each as a separate allocation.
5. Population protocol from section 3, then the immutable-mesh/shared-lift reuse checks.

## 6. Where the risk lies

On current provisional Re20 numbers, 2·d_h is at least 10x below every budget/5. Delta_y will therefore be dominated by d_p, and the binding quantity is most likely lift against the 4e-4 absolute budget, given the P3 lift failures. The DFG deviations (drag 2.2e-6, lift 3.2e-6) are about 6x below the finest h-differences, which fits the refinement picture. They are an anchor gate, not a calibration of delta_y, and they are limited by the precision of the literature reference values.

## 7. Remaining uncertainty

- The P4 geometry-order interaction is unmeasured. Geometry sensitivity has been checked at P3 only; P4/g4→g5 has not been tested.
- delta_y is a conditional empirical estimate, not an error bound.
- The manufactured P4 case reproduces the analytic solution to about 1e-12. That suggests it lies in the mapped discrete space, so it tests exactness, not convergence. Confirm that a non-polynomial manufactured convergence study is part of the passed G1 evidence.
