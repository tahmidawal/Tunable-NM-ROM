Question

Does the cost depend on the mesh?

No: solve cost is flat in 2D and 3D

  • 2D: 256² → 4096² (256× more nodes): every setting within 1.01–1.02 of its 1024² time; band 0.8–1.25
  • 3D: 64³ → 384³ (216× more nodes): 4 of 4 ROMs within 1.01–1.09
  • 3D is timed mesh-free (inputs and outputs at the method’s own points)
