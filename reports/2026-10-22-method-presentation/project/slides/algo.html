u = G(x)c on any mesh

  • Steps 2–5 never touch the mesh: they work on r numbers, M = 4r tests and m quadrature points
  • The mesh only appears at the start (reading u₀) and the end (writing the output)
  • So solve cost is flat in the mesh, but reading and writing fields still grow with it
