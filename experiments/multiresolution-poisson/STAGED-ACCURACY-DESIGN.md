# Staged neural-bank training for Poisson accuracy

This protocol is predeclared before training or timing; all results are development evidence until independent confirmation. It continues the approved Poisson branch and namespace, using the current original-relative checkpoint as the common initialization.

The latent dimension and feature-bank size remain fixed. The existing training-source prefix and original training mesh are unchanged; no larger dataset or new architecture is conflated with staging. A new development seed/count is declared in `config-staged-accuracy.json` before training, alongside the complete previously opened cohort. No sealed final source receives a truth solve, fit or model selection.

The primary controls are the unchanged network and ordinary joint continuation. The staged method first learns the spatial neural bank using unrestricted per-training-source coefficients, then freezes that bank while fitting the same nonlinear head and latent training codes, then jointly fine-tunes. The free coefficients begin with an offline least-squares fit within the original bank. All staged endpoints and coefficients are saved. The learned bank remains a neural coordinate network; a separately computed normalized training-only SVD span is a diagnostic, never a primary decoder or a source of its initialization.

The bank and joint stages use the existing per-snapshot relative field reconstruction objective and feature-Gram regularizer. The head stage uses the exact full-field metric. With $G=QR$, training fields $u_i$, and $b_i=Q^T u_i$, its loss is

$$L=\frac1S\sum_i\frac{\|R h(z_i)-b_i\|_2^2+\|u_i-Qb_i\|_2^2}{\|u_i\|_2^2}.$$

The perpendicular contribution is computed directly. It is not a coefficient-space Euclidean loss. Frozen parameter subtrees are excluded from optimizer state, rather than simply assigned a small learning rate. Rank and conditioning are checked before any triangular coefficient solve. A deficient bank uses a truncated-SVD diagnostic, is labelled failed-rank, and is not accepted as a valid full-rank projection or query model.

The configuration fixes staged update counts, batch sizes, learning rates and seeds. The joint control receives the sum of the measured staged optimizer-block elapsed intervals on the same warmed GPU. Each interval begins immediately before a declared block of compiled optimizer calls and ends after all updates are synchronized. Compilation, data generation, burn-in, QR, diagnostics, checkpoint writes and progress logs are excluded from this match and reported separately. The control stops at the first complete block reaching the target; actual update count, exposure count and the at-most-one-block overshoot are retained. This matches measured optimizer effort; it does not establish convergence or equal FLOPs. No development metric chooses the checkpoint or stop time.

All declared model endpoints are frozen across the requested evaluation mesh ladder. Every checkpoint receives rebuilt full banks, exact weak operators and source-based training-code caches. Online input is the supplied full source field. All timed neural methods use one nearest-code start, a common stationary stopping threshold and the same iteration cap. Full projection, initialization, solving and requested decoding are charged. Same-job CG controls and direct DST are retained, with paired full outputs and repetitions after per-block GPU burn-in. Assembly and compilation are offline, separately recorded. No quadrature or source descriptors enter the online model.

Each checkpoint also receives a full same-grid bank projection diagnostic. On the declared diagnostic evaluation mesh, bounded reference-only nonlinear head fits use both the mean training code and a nearest full-field training prediction. Their objective includes the perpendicular field residual. Every candidate field, gradient, budget/stop reason and linear-solve status is retained; only stationary candidates enter the best-found head-fit diagnostic. These are not global-optimality proofs and never supply an online initial guess. Their purpose is to distinguish bank approximation, nonlinear compression and PDE-objective error.

All outputs require GPU backend, f64, highest precision, source/checkpoint/seed identity and complete cohort accounting. Auditing reconstructs neural bank/operator/decoded fields, references, projected and full head residuals, analytic head gradients, sampling choices and actual stopping status independently. Training phases are written to result JSON before failure assertions. Full checkpoint and field archives are checksum-collected, split into tracked parts, then the exact remote directory is removed. The current branch remains separate.

Glossary: **bank** is the learned spatial feature matrix; **head** maps latent coordinates into bank coefficients; **free coefficients** are unrestricted training-only bank weights; **QR** provides an orthogonal field metric; **SVD/POD** is a training-snapshot projection diagnostic; **stationary** means the normalized optimization gradient satisfies its declared threshold; **development** sources may guide later changes while sealed final sources remain untouched; **paired** means a recorded runtime and error belong to the same invocation; **CG/DST** are iterative and direct Poisson solvers; **rank** counts numerically independent spatial features; **staging** separates bank fitting, head fitting and joint fine-tuning.
