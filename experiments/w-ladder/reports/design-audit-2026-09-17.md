**§2 Mathematics of §3.1**

5. **OK** — with the bank mass-orthonormal (`pilot.rebuild`:144-148) and dirichlet damping identically zero (`fresh_fom.boundary_ratio`:54-59), `fresh_rom.weak_acceleration`/`acceleration.accelerated_rhs` with $J\to I$ gives `accel = -stiffness@a` = $-c^2Ka$ exactly. DESIGN.md:99 is correct.
6. **WRONG (as stated)** — DESIGN.md:86-88: "if it is nonsingular … the trajectory **must reproduce the linear bank evolution** up to the RK4 integration error". Two omissions. (a) The ODE is reproduced only *given the same initial state*; `nested_q32` starts from an 8-start LM fit (`pilot.cold_fit`:218-233), `linear_bank64` from the raw projection. They coincide only if the q=32 fit drives the residual to ~0 — plausible (locally onto) but contingent and ungated. (b) RK4 is **not** invariant under the nonlinear change of variables $a=h_{32}(z)+B_{32}y$; the two arms are two *different* 4th-order approximations, so the gap is O(RK4 truncation), not "integration roundoff". The curvature claim itself is right: $[J|B]$ square and invertible ⇒ $\ddot a = J\ddot z+B\ddot y+h''=-c^2Ka$ exactly, curvature cancelled.
7. **WRONG** — G5b's "expected $\lesssim10^{-6}$ (64×64 normal equations over 960 stages)" (DESIGN.md:218-219) misattributes the source and is likely off by orders of magnitude; the dominant term is the RK4-coordinate mismatch of (6). The sibling pair `linear_bank64_rk4` vs `linear_bank64` (ladder.py:504) measures the raw RK4 error and should be used to *set* the band, not a pre-guessed 1e-6.
8. **WRONG / high-confidence, quantified** — the hidden assumption is the **Cholesky guard**. `acceleration.cholesky_solve`:61 takes the fast path only when `bound = 1/(‖J‖_F‖L^{-1}‖_F) > 1e-3`. G0 records the q=0 head's `minimum_dynamic_rank_ratio = 1.324e-3` (checks/g0-wave-replay.json) — already within 30 % of the threshold, with 0 fallbacks. The retained $B_8$ columns have norms 8.7e-4…5.4e-4, i.e. the *same* scale as the head's smallest linear columns (9.6e-4); ladder.py:129 replaces them with **norm-1 orthonormal** columns, a ~10³ rescale of those directions. `bound` scales like $\sigma_\min/\|J\|_F$, so it drops to ~1e-6 ⇒ **every RK4 stage of `nested_q8/16/32` takes the QR + exact-SVD fallback**. Consequences: the rungs are materially slower than `head_q0`/`trained_nested40` for a coordinate reason, DESIGN.md:72 ("costs the same launch-bound stage as the q=0 solve") is probably false, `total_guard_fallbacks` will be ≈960·… , and G5a's "must agree to integration roundoff" (DESIGN.md:85-86) is optimistic because the two arms then run *different solvers* on a 10³-worse-conditioned system. Fix: use scaled (PCA-variance) directions for all rungs, or disclose and gate the fallback count.
9. **SUGGESTION** — `acceleration.accelerated_rhs`:88 computes `normal_backward_error`; DESIGN §6 gates rank (1e-8) but not this. Given (8), gate `maximum_normal_backward_error` and `total_guard_fallbacks` in G2.
10. **NEEDS-RESTATEMENT** — the rank check in `nested_model` (ladder.py:141) is taken on the **untransformed** Jacobian, while the runtime 1e-8 guard acts on `transform @ J`. G2 (DESIGN.md:205) says "sampled joint rank > 1e-8" without saying which. They are different numbers.

**§3 Energy certificate**

11. **OK** — $K\succ0$ ⇒ real $\omega$, modal propagation unconditionally stable and exactly energy-conserving in exact arithmetic. `eigen` is guaranteed positive here (audited $\lambda_{\min}\approx19.7$); note `modal_targets`:32 divides by $\omega$ unguarded — fine for this bank, fatal for any future near-null mode.
12. **OK** — $E_r$ *is* the discretisation's physical energy. `pilot.assemble`:129-132 gives $K=G^\top(M\,L\,G)=G^\top A G$ with $A=ML$; `pilot.energy_np`:63-72 uses trapezoid mass ($=h^2$) and raw edge differences, and $u^\top A u=\sum(\Delta u)^2$ exactly. With $u=Ga,\;v=Gb$ and $G^\top MG=I$: $\tfrac12(b^\top b+c^2a^\top Ka)=E_{\text{phys}}$. `ladder.reduced_energy_drift`:260-262 and `linear_stepping`:203-226 are consistent with this.
13. **SUGGESTION** — `linear_stepping` builds the CN propagator *inside* the timer (ladder.py:234-241) whereas `linear_bank64`'s eigendecomposition is precomputed offline (`modal.prepare`, ladder.py:424). Not verdict-bearing, but disclose the asymmetry.
14. **WRONG (unstated risk)** — RK4 on an oscillator is unstable for $\omega\Delta t>2\sqrt2$. The head arms only excite the head's 32 directions; `linear_bank64_rk4` excites all 64 bank modes at $\Delta t=0.01$. There is no bound on $\lambda_{\max}(K)$ anywhere in the design, and even at $\omega\Delta t\approx1$ RK4 loses ~0.8 % amplitude per step (≈ 84 % over 240 steps). DESIGN.md:107 calls RK4 only "slightly dissipative". Pre-register $\lambda_{\max}(K)$ per mesh and a declared outcome if `linear_bank64_rk4` fails to `complete`.

**§4 POD transfer (§3.3)**

15. **OK** — the DST-I scaling is correct. `pilot.dst1`:177-183 yields $\sqrt{2/n}\sum a_j\sin(\pi jk/n)$, i.e. the orthonormal, involutive DST-I. Re-evaluating the interpolant on $n'$ requires $\sqrt{n'/n}$ per axis, so $n'/n$ in 2D — exactly `* (target / n)` at ladder.py:116.
16. **OK** — coarse transfer by `pilot.restrict`:55-60 (`slice(ratio-1,None,ratio)`) hits $x=(j'+1)/n'$ exactly, so nodal subsampling *is* sine-interpolant evaluation, including the (correct) aliasing.
17. **NEEDS-RESTATEMENT** — fairness vs the neural bank is only approximate and undisclosed. The bank is re-evaluated analytically on the 1024² grid (`pilot.evaluate_bank`) and so carries fine-grid content; POD at 1024² is band-limited to the 255² sine band. That systematically favours the bank at the finest mesh (and mildly favours POD at 64², where the bank also aliases). Say so in §3.3; H-POD is non-verdict-bearing, so no verdict moves, but the sentence "a fair analogue" should not be written without this caveat.
18. **SUGGESTION** — `sine_transfer_check` (ladder.py:129-137) tests a single low mode (3,2) representable on every grid; the 1e-12 gate therefore exercises neither the zero-padding scaling at high $k$ nor aliasing. Add a mode near the 256-grid Nyquist and a round-trip prolong→restrict identity.
19. **SUGGESTION** — the frozen `campaign-config.json` records `pod_algorithm = "Fresh randomized SVD, rank 64+24 oversampling, four power iterations"`; ladder.py:76-93 uses an exact method-of-snapshots eigh. That is a *better* POD and matches DESIGN §3.3, but the report must not describe it as "the campaign's POD".
