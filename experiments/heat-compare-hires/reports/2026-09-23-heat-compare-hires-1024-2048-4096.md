## Glossary

- **worst %**: over the 16 cases, the largest current-relative L² error over all six output times (t = 0 included), against the exact same-grid semidiscrete solution (DST). **median %**: median over cases of each case's maximum over times.
- **GPU ms**: median over 16 cases × 5 repetitions of the time from a supplied initial field on the GPU to six full fields on the GPU; each repetition after a 0.15 s GPU burn-in; compilation and offline assembly excluded.
- **FOM chosen**: the fastest tested CN–CG setting (same job) whose worst error is ≤ the arm's worst error. **speedup** = its time / the arm's time. **× named** = time of the named FOM (Δt 0.025, rtol 1e-6) / the arm's time.
- **CN–CG**: Crank–Nicolson in time, unpreconditioned warm-started conjugate gradients per step, on the same grid; `dt`/`rtol` in the name.
- **NM-ROM**: the paper's frozen wide-bank model (bank rank R = 128, latent K = 8); `q` = number of linear corrections; `cn` = weak Crank–Nicolson stepping; `field_direct_tol1e-4_chol` = the batched fit (each output fitted to the exact flow of the supplied field's test moments; valid because heat is linear and autonomous and the tests are sine eigenfunctions).
- **linear bank**: free coefficients in the span of the same frozen bank (the top rung q = R); not the NM-ROM.
- **POD r**: uncentred POD of the 3072 training snapshots (512 draws × 6 times) computed at the target mesh; `galerkin_cn` / `lspg_cn` = Galerkin / least-squares Petrov–Galerkin projection of the FOM's Crank–Nicolson step (Δt 0.025); `galerkin_exact` = exact reduced exponential.
- **quadratic manifold r**: u = u_ref + V a + W vech(a aᵀ), ridge weight γ chosen by an 80/20 holdout that splits whole training trajectories; solved with the NM-ROM's own solver and contracts (`cn`, `field_direct_tol1e-4_chol`).
- **neural operators**: FNO / U-Net / Transolver / DeepONet trained at the target mesh on the 512 training draws with the Burgers panel's published configurations and a 3000 s wall budget per network; checkpoint = best validation.
- **controls**: exact DST propagation and a 64-interval coarse-grid CN–CG + interpolation; printed for honesty, never used as "FOM chosen".
- **sealed cohort**: the 16 evaluation draws of the paper's heat rows; **failed solves**: LM solves that did not reach stationarity, or CG solves that missed their tolerance.
- **order-effect gate** (DESIGN A2): (a) carry-over — a fixed sentinel query (exact DST propagation of case 0, 15 repetitions) is timed after every block; the median over all sentinel repetitions after full-order blocks divided by the median after all other blocks must lie within ±10 %; (b) re-time — three real arms are rebuilt and timed again on all 16 cases after the full-order phase and must match their own block medians within ±10 %; (c) positive control — the sentinel timed behind deliberately queued GPU work must fail the ±10 % test. The v1 per-block sentinel test is reported but is noise-dominated (A2).
- **operator training budget**: every operator stopped on its 3000 s wall budget (the Burgers panel protocol); `epochs` counts the partial last epoch. Convergence is not established, so the operator rows are a budget-limited comparison, not a statement about what these architectures can reach.
- **GPU**: 1024² ran on an A100 80GB (no H200 was free; DESIGN A2), 2048² and 4096² on an H200. Every ratio is inside one job.
