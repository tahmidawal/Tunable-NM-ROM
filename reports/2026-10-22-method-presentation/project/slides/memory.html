What does off-mesh quadrature cost in memory?

Twenty times less memory than the tensor method in 3D

  • At r = 1024 (3D): 1.61 GB for the off-mesh rule against 34.37 GB for the precomputed tensor
  • At r = 512: 0.34 GB against 4.30 GB
  • Off-mesh query also faster than the tensor in all six 3D cells

Lanes jcp-wide-bank and quadrature-burgers3d · 5

The tensor method precomputes a third-order tensor whose size grows like r cubed. Off-mesh quadrature only stores the network values at m points, which grows like m times r.
