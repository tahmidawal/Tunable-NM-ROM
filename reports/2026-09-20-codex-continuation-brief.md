## 4. How this campaign works, so you do not break it

- **Pre-register before you run.** A lane writes its question, arms, gates and falsification clauses into
  `DESIGN.md` and commits *before* the first job. Amendments are appended with a date and a reason; earlier
  text is never edited. A verdict that rests on a gate changed after the data landed must say so.
- **Never certify a quadrature rule by its NNLS fitting residual.** Only held-out error on the states the
  solver actually reaches, against a fixed bar, then re-drawn. Status is one of confirmed (n of n re-draws),
  single-draw, marginal (k of n), none.
- **Never form a cost ratio across jobs or GPUs.** Same-allocation only. Timing is three repetitions with
  burn-in and device sync, medians reported, all repetitions retained.
- **Never hand-type a number into a report.** Generate it from the run JSONs with a script in `reports/`.
  Every prose number that has gone wrong in this project went wrong by being typed.
- **Report what failed.** Retractions matter more than successes here and are the part that gets silently
  dropped. Several results in the lab log supersede earlier ones.
- **Never push and never merge.** The repository is about 200 GB and single pushes fail; the mirror decision
  is the user's. Do not touch `best-results/`.
- Errors: worst over cases and median, against the converged same-grid full-order solve timed in the same
  job. On Burgers report worst-over-evolved-times, worst-over-all-times and the t=0 compression separately.
