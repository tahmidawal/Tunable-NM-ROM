## Glossary

- **full / oracle**: residual evaluated on the whole grid, no quadrature.
- **ex / exact-linear**: linear residual terms computed exactly through $A = \Phi^{\mathsf T} G$;
  advection still sampled at NNLS nodes.
- **tensor**: advection term projected exactly, $\Phi^{\mathsf T}(u \odot D u) = \tfrac12 h^{\mathsf T} Q h$.
- **ex_learned / learned-32**: the sampled arm with learned node positions.
- **NNLS-32**: sampled arm with 32 nodes fitted by non-negative least squares.
- **eq / qf**: sampled empirical quadrature / quadrature-free (Poisson).
- **e2e**: end to end, including the initial-condition fit and the full-grid decode.
- **matched rung**: cheapest FOM tolerance setting at least as accurate as the ROM arm.
- **(b), (c1), cos**: residual error, gradient error, and gradient direction agreement against
  the full grid.
- **paired / AB/BA**: ROM and FOM timed alternately in one job on one GPU.
