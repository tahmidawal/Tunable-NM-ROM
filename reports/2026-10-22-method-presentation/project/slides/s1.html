How do we get c at time zero?

Fit the initial condition to the shapes

c₀ = argmin_c Σ_q w_q ( G(x_q)·c − u₀(x_q) )²

  • u₀ is sampled at 48 × 48 Gauss points, not on the whole mesh
  • Closed-form weighted least squares: one small linear solve
  • Cost about 0.2 ms at r = 384

The initial field can come from any mesh; only 2304 point values are needed.
