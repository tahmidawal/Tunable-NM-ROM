Why does a spectral solver beat us on speed?

Smooth Burgers needs very few sine modes

  • The matched spectral solver needs a median of only 11 modes per axis
  • Each ROM unknown costs 1.07×10⁸ flops per step; each spectral mode 2.3×10³: a factor 4.6×10⁴
  • The ROM has far fewer unknowns, but each is vastly more expensive

Spectral investigation, why-spectral report · 28

This is the core reason for the speed result. Our cost is set by m·M per unknown; a spectral method pays an FFT per mode. For smooth solutions in a box, the spectral method wins by construction.
