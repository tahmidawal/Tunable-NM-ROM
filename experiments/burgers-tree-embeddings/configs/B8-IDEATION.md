## Targeted primary-literature novelty check, 2026-11-20

This is a focused prior-art check, not an exhaustive novelty proof. Neural reduced coordinates, local nonlinear ROMs, multilevel windowed networks and residual-driven refinement each have precedents:

- [CROM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.02607) already learns continuous neural reduced manifolds and supports adaptive spatial resolution. [SNF-ROM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14890) already projects PDE dynamics onto smooth neural-field manifolds. Thus solving a PDE in neural embeddings or reconstructing at arbitrary resolution is not by itself new.
- [DD NM-ROM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15163) couples local nonlinear autoencoder ROMs with hyper-reduction and studies2D steady Burgers. [Scalable DD NM-ROM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.00507) composes local ROMs for time-dependent2D Burgers. Spatially local nonlinear reduced models and reusable local training therefore need explicit differentiation from these works.
- [Multilevel FBPINNs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05486) combine overlapping windowed subnetworks across levels. [AB-PINNs](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.08924v1), especially Section3.2, adds subnetworks in high-PDE-residual regions on top of a global/local ansatz. Hierarchy, global-plus-local corrections and residual-driven subdomain addition are already established ideas. Their reviewed formulations optimize network parameters for the PDE; our intended distinction is offline-learned shared decoder weights with small independent online child codes.
- [ADD-FBPINNs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05659) is a recent preprint on learned adaptive partitions, including viscous Burgers. [Block-sparse GN for FBPINNs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13231) exploits localized Jacobian/Gauss–Newton sparsity. Neither adaptivity nor efficient local Jacobians should be claimed as categorically new.
- [LiCROM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15907) uses neural fields as continuous linear displacement bases. Our direct-coefficient learned-feature control has conceptual overlap; a neural feature bank alone does not establish a nonlinear reduced manifold.

A defensible potential contribution is the specific combination of independently adjustable, zero-preserving nonlinear child embeddings; fixed-window retained-parent enrichment with useful zero-code tangents; deployable residual-driven activation during embedding-space PDE evolution; and verified improvement in COMPLETE solve cost at common physical tolerance versus strong matched global neural/CROM/SNF controls. This combination remains a research hypothesis, not demonstrated priority or achieved superiority. The online adaptation and full cost-to-tolerance evidence must distinguish it from the adjacent DD-ROM/FBPINN literature, not merely rename their local or hierarchical ingredients. The present smooth-case diagnostic and representation improvements alone do not establish the complete contribution.
