**Caveat on the batched columns, stated because it works in the ROM's favour.** Both sides are `vmap`ped over queries, and a `vmap` of a `lax.while_loop` runs until EVERY lane has finished, so each batched solve pays its batch's worst-case iteration count. That penalises the classical solver much more than the ROM, because a classical iteration is expensive and a latent LM iteration is not -- visible above as the classical per-query cost getting WORSE from B=1 to B=2 at N=1024. A production many-query classical solver would let queries terminate independently (separate streams, or a batched solve with per-lane exit), so the batched ratios here are an upper bound on the ROM's advantage, not a measurement of the best possible classical batching.
