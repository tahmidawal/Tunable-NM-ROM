control concurrently with the replacement ROM owner. Root identified it, confirmed
its interrupted state and absence of live shell processes, and restored sole-writer
ownership. These original sources and uncommitted packaging additions are preserved;
none were staged or executed. The selected reviewed fixture is
`native_interface_control.py`, with its final source inventory under `native01/`.
