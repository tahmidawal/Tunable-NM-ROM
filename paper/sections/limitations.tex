\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Single seed, single checkpoint, until the seed lane lands.} Every
  number in this paper rests on one training seed and one checkpoint per PDE.
  The three-seed repetition and the sealed-cohort evaluation
  (Tables~\ref{tab:seeds}, \ref{tab:sealed}) are running; the seed lane has
  also recorded an amendment to its fidelity gate, from $10^{-9}$ to $10^{-3}$
  relative, made before any of its numbers were read. Until those land, no
  spread is reported and every endpoint carries this label.
\item \textbf{Development cohort.} The Burgers numbers are on six opened
  development cases (and 32 held-out cases for the tuning study); the wave and
  Poisson numbers on eight and twelve. The L-shape cell shows the tail this
  understates: worst error on 461 validation sources is two to three times the
  worst on its 32 development sources.
\item \textbf{2D only.} No 3D cell is in this paper.
\item \textbf{No cold-start SMA-NM-ROM comparison.} Reviewer 5mgh asked for the
  shallow masked autoencoder ROM under the same cold-start protocol; it was not
  run, and the comparison we do make is against POD-LSPG through our own
  solver.
\item \textbf{Operator numbers are lower bounds.} Every neural-operator arm was
  still improving when its wall budget ended ($\nOpLaneImproving$ arms in the
  U-Net/Transolver screen, $\nOpFnoImproving$ FNO arms), and none was tuned
  per family. A reviewer with a larger budget would beat the ROM by more, not
  less.
\item \textbf{Quadrature certification is one draw.} Every ``certified'' flag
  in \S\ref{sec:exp:eq} is a single draw of its candidate pool and fit-state
  subset; a second draw at $q=64$ moved $\rho_{\max}$ from
  $\nEqtopQsixtyFourArchivedRho$ to $\nEqtopQsixtyFourRedrawRho$ and flipped
  the flag. The replication job $\nEqtopPendingJob$ is pending, and until it
  lands the EQ ladder is ``certified in this draw'' and nothing stronger.
\item \textbf{The headline ladder is dearer than the scheduled one.} Holding
  $M$ fixed isolates the rank effect but quotes a more expensive baseline: the
  fixed-$M$ $q=0$ rung costs $\nQxmFixedQzeroMs$\,ms where the scheduled
  $M=4(K+q)$ rung costs $\nQxmScheduledQzeroMs$\,ms (different jobs; accuracy
  identical to $10^{-9}$; costs not to be divided).
\item \textbf{Pending cells.} The $1024^2$ same-allocation panel, the Poisson
  $1024^2$ ladder, the L-shape solve layer, the Navier--Stokes reduced model
  and the operator resolution knob are marked as placeholders where they would
  appear. The last one is binding: if a trained operator exposes its own
  accuracy--cost family through evaluation resolution, the framing sentence
  ``one accuracy--cost point per trained operator'' is withdrawn.
\item \textbf{No theory.} No convergence guarantee for the cold-start
  iteration, no bound on the quadrature error on unseen states, no continuum
  error bound.
\end{itemize}
