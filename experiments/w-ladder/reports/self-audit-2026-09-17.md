# Final self-audit of the w-ladder report against the raw JSONs (2026-09-17)

The protocol asks for a Codex pass over the finished report. Codex was unavailable all day
(usage limit exhausted until 2026-09-19 11:33; trace in `checks/codex-design-trace.log`), so the
pre-job audit was done by an independent Claude reviewer (`reports/design-audit-2026-09-17.md`)
and this closing pass is my own, written out so it can be checked.

## What was verified, and how

1. **Every arm row in `summary.json` was recomputed from `artifacts/<attempt>/result.json`
   without using the generator's code** — worst-over-8-cases energy-state and displacement error
   from the repetition-0 invocations, median GPU ms over all 24 invocations per arm. All 54 arm
   rows across the three meshes matched to better than 1e-12 relative. Each arm was confirmed to
   have exactly 8 first-repetition invocations and 24 total.
2. **D1, D2 and D3 were recomputed from scratch** in that same check, including the A2 integrator
   tie band, and agree with the generator at all three meshes.
3. **`audit_ladder.py`** (independent: NumPy/SciPy only, no JAX, no driver import) recomputed every
   reported error from the saved coefficient trajectories times the saved bank/POD tables, against
   a reference regenerated from the saved initial fields with SciPy's DST: max difference
   8.9e-16 (64²), 7.4e-15 (256²), 1.6e-14 (1024²); reference regeneration 5.6e-15 / 3.2e-14 /
   1.4e-13; reduced-energy drifts reproduced exactly.
4. **In-job gates** passed in all three jobs: frozen-math SHA256s, bank rebuild parity, nested
   inclusion, POD transfer and orthogonality, positive-definite reduced stiffness, CG true-residual
   convergence, byte-identical repetitions, and the retained-value gates against accel12 / accel07.

## Defects this pass found and fixed

- **`summary.json` had two rows per (mesh, subject, metric)** — an arm row and a decomposition-layer
  row sharing the same `subject`, separated only by `family`. A consumer filtering on subject alone
  would have silently taken the wrong one. Decomposition subjects are now `<arm>@<layer>` with the
  bare arm name kept in a new `arm` field.
- **`audit_ladder.py` hardcoded the 1e-9 gate tolerance**, so it re-failed the six start-tie rows
  that DESIGN A4 had already diagnosed. It now applies the A4 policy independently (1e-7 for arms
  carrying the cold fit, 1e-9 for the deterministic linear arms), by arm-name prefix rather than by
  reading the driver's decision.
- Earlier in the day, before any job: the D3 tie band was applied in the wrong direction (it
  rewarded a head rung for beating the bank instead of requiring it to beat it by δ), and the
  "answer up front" paragraph claimed "two orders of magnitude" where the measured range is 42–158×.
  Both fixed and regenerated.

## What I could not check, and what a reviewer should attack

- The selected cold-fit objective gate is the one quantity the independent audit **cannot**
  recompute without rerunning the solver, so it is covered only in-job. A reviewer wanting full
  independence would refit from the saved targets with their own optimiser.
- The 1024² full-field arrays for the one designated full-field case and the mesh tables are
  **not** in the Git chunks (`artifacts/wl1024b/EXCLUDED-FROM-ARCHIVE.txt`): ~5.9 GB of the 6.1 GB
  output. Their SHA256s were verified on both sides before deletion and are recorded, and every
  ROM arm's fields are exactly reconstructible from the chunked coefficients — but the FOM fields
  at 1024² would have to be regenerated by rerunning the job.
- Timings are single-GPU medians of three repetitions on an A100 with other jobs on the node; the
  cost *ratios* within a job are the claim, never a ratio across jobs.
- Everything is the development cohort. The sealed final cohort was not opened.
