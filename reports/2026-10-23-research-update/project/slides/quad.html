How many quadrature points does the nonlinear term need?

Gauss and Fibonacci rules pass with a few thousand points; Sobol and Smolyak need far more

  • ρ: worst relative error of the quadrature nonlinear term against a converged reference (Gauss 640²), over every state the model reaches
  • Acceptance bar: ρ ≤ 0.116
  • At about 4,000 points: Gauss 0.019, Fibonacci 0.033
  • Sobol converges slowly; Smolyak (a control) never reaches the bar
  • The old 63² mesh lattice (triangle) is measured against its own mesh target, so it is not directly comparable

Off-mesh quadrature study 2D, accurate setting, 1024², dev+val · 5

This is the off-mesh quadrature of the nonlinear term u·∇u: the network and its exact derivative are evaluated at m fixed points in the unit square, the same on every mesh. Gauss and Fibonacci lattices converge fast because the integrand is smooth; random-like Sobol points converge roughly as one over root m. The number of points grows with the bank width: about 4,000 at r = 128 up to about 37,000 at r = 512, and that is what drives the cost per step.
