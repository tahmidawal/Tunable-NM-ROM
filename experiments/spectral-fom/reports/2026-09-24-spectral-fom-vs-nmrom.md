# Spectral / fast-transform FOMs vs NM-ROM (stored, not in the paper)

**ARCHIVE ONLY.** On 2026-09-23 the user decided that this lane is an archive. It is not used in the paper. The numbers below are final for the meshes listed.

This report measures the strongest fast-transform full-order solver for each problem in the paper and times it against our accurate and fast settings. Each comparison runs in the same allocation, on the same discrete system and against the same reference. The numbers are **stored for a later decision** and are not in `paper/`. Every number below is generated by `experiments/spectral-fom/make_report.py` from the collected JSON. Ratio = spectral FOM GPU ms / our GPU ms, so a ratio **below 1 means the spectral solver is faster**. Rows are final for the meshes listed; missing meshes are listed under open items.

## Findings (generated)

- **Poisson 2D square** (256, 1024, 2048, 4096): spectral/ours is 0.099–0.352 against the accurate setting and 0.332–0.569 against the fast setting. The DST solve is exact, with worst error ≤ 1.4e-15. The spectral solver is **faster and more accurate than both settings at every mesh**.
- **Poisson 3D cube** (32, 64, 128, 256): spectral/ours is 0.223–0.514 against the accurate setting and 0.359–0.586 against the fast setting. The DST solve is exact, with worst error ≤ 1.9e-15. The spectral solver is **faster and more accurate than both settings at every mesh**.
- **Burgers 2D** (256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096): the matched spectral/ours ratio is 0.072–1.733 for the accurate setting and 0.166–3.854 for the fast one. Tight Picard, at reference accuracy, gives 0.174–5.709 and 0.706–26.127. The ROM is faster than its matched spectral setting at: 2048 fast, 4096 accurate, 4096 fast.
- **Heat 2D** (1024, 2048, 4096): matched spectral/ours is 0.367–0.442 (accurate `lin_R128_bf`); 1.108–1.405 (fast `lin_R48_cn`); 0.590–0.699 (accurate_cn `lin_R128_cn`); 0.738–0.943 (fast_bf `lin_R48_bf`). The exact modal propagation is also the lane's truth.
- **Heat 3D** (32, 64, 128, 256): matched spectral/ours is 0.209–0.308 (accurate `lin_R320_bf`); 0.503–0.610 (fast `lin_R128_cn`); 0.217–0.345 (accurate_cn `lin_R320_cn`); 0.447–0.564 (fast_bf `lin_R128_bf`). The exact modal propagation is also the lane's truth.
- **NS 3D** (recorded): CNAB2/ours is 1.091–7.790 for the accurate setting and 0.952–13.142 for the fast one. The ROM is faster than its matched CNAB2 setting in 7 of 8 rows. It is not faster at: 32³ fast (0.952).

## L-shaped Poisson

No spectral arm. The Dirichlet Laplacian on the L-shaped domain is not diagonalised by any separable fast transform. The domain is not a tensor product, and its re-entrant corner gives a singular solution. The standard alternatives are domain decomposition with fast solvers on rectangles, a capacitance-matrix method, or multigrid. Each of these is an iterative or direct method of the kind the paper already excludes, so none was built.

## Audit scope and review

- **Poisson (2D, cube).** The audit recomputes every subject's error on every case at full resolution, against a NumPy dense-sine truth.
- **Burgers and heat.** The audit is **partial**.
  - Full-grid errors are recomputed only where full fields were kept: case 0, for the ROM arms and one or two spectral subjects.
  - Every other (subject, case) is checked on a subsampled grid against the driver's saved subsampled truth.
  - For Burgers, the truth is the paper's own FOM, and it is not recomputed independently.
- **`check_timing.py`.** It independently recomputes, from the raw invocation records:
  - every median, the drift gate, the raw neighbour gate and the case-normalised (T1) neighbour gate;
  - the subject × phase × case × repetition inventory, which must have no duplicates or gaps;
  - Burgers lane parity, on both arms and every case.
  It includes two controls that must be detected. It passes on every accepted and record run.
- **Codex review.** An independent read-only codex review (`checks/codex-review1.md`) found no blocker, and no numerical mismatch in two recomputed rows. Its disposition is in `checks/codex-review1-disposition.md`.
- **Burgers DST choice.** The DST implementation inside the Burgers $H^{-1}$ was chosen per mesh by a short, untimed, fixed-order micro-benchmark with no burn-in, so a sub-optimal choice there would only make the spectral side slower. The chosen variant is listed per mesh.

## Glossary

- **NM-ROM**: the paper's reduced model: a frozen spatial bank G, with a query solving for a few coefficients.
- **R' (bank width)**: the number of leading columns of the importance-ordered bank a query uses. It is the tunability knob.
- **linear rung (`R<R'>_linear`, `lin_R<R'>`)**: a solve in the span of the first R' bank columns. There is no head.
- **head, q**: u = G h(z), a small network h of a latent z. q is the number of correction directions (0 = head only).
- **accurate / fast setting**: the owning lane's pre-registered choices: the most accurate arm, and the cheapest arm no worse than the paper's old fast setting.
- **spectral FOM**: a full-order solver that diagonalises the discrete operator with a fast sine transform (DST-I). Here it solves exactly the same discrete system as the paper's iterative FOM.
- **`dst_fft` / `dst_mm`**: two implementations of the same DST-I: a real FFT of the odd extension, and GEMM with the dense orthonormal sine matrix.
- **Picard (`pic_dt<Δt>_nt<ntol>`)**: the Burgers step solved by u ← u − (I+ΔtνA)⁻¹ r(u) until ‖r‖ ≤ ntol‖u_prev‖, the paper's Newton stopping rule.
- **IMEX (`imex_dt<Δt>`)**: one Picard sweep per step: explicit upwind advection, implicit diffusion.
- **`modal_cn` / `modal_exp`**: heat solved in DST modal space: Crank–Nicolson at Δt = 0.025 applied exactly (= CN–CG with an exact linear solve), or exact exponential propagation (= the truth).
- **err**: the paper's per-problem error, worst over the cohort. Poisson: relative L2 against the same-grid discrete solution. Heat: relative L2 per output time, max over times. Burgers: max over t ≥ 0.05 of ‖u−u_ref‖/‖u0‖ against the paper's tight Newton–BiCGStab reference. NS: the lane's evolved worst.
- **ms**: GPU query time: the median of `fused_device_seconds`, from after the synchronised input copy to completion. For ROM rows, over phases A1 ∪ A2; for spectral rows, over phase B.
- **ratio**: spectral FOM ms / our ms. Below 1 means the spectral solver is faster.
- **matched spectral**: the fastest spectral setting whose worst error is ≤ the arm's worst error.
- **A–B–A**: ROM phase, spectral phase, ROM phase in one allocation. Each is randomised, with burn-in before every call, and with a sync, cooldown and fixed dummy kernel between phases.
- **drift gate**: the ROM median in A2 divided by the median in A1 must lie in [1/1.1, 1.1].
- **neighbour gate**: the median after a long predecessor divided by the median after a short one must be ≤ 1.10, within a phase. "Case-normalised (T1)" divides each time by that subject's median on that case first.
- **audit**: an independent NumPy recomputation of every recorded error from saved fields, using a different truth method, with two controls that must be detected.
- **CG certified**: the paper CG's own flag that its true relative residual reached rtol.
- **cohort**: Poisson 2D: 12 opened development sources. Cube: the final cohort, 64 cases. Burgers: dev6. Heat: the held-out sealed cohort. NS: the lane's development and held-out cohorts.
