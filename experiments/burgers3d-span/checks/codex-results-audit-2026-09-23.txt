   A2/A3 honestly disclose that they followed validation results. Their candidate additions are validation-driven development, not an entirely prospectively fixed experiment. They retain the accuracy bar, eligibility threshold and comparator rule.

   A4 is a justified correction: merging the earlier certificates restores genuinely eligible competitors, preserves fast picks/FOMs, and changes accurate picks according to the literal minimum-error rule. However, the corrected selection was frozen **after a first held-out attempt had already started and produced one quick-run line**. The report’s opening “opened once” and glossary’s “frozen before … opened” are therefore literally inaccurate, despite the later disclosure.

   I found **no evidence that held-out outcomes drove a ROM choice**. The claim that the aborted logs were unread remains the lane owner’s attestation, not something these files independently prove. Appropriate wording is: “one completed held-out evaluation following an aborted attempt with the superseded selection; preliminary results reportedly unread.”

**CANNOT claim**:

- Successful held-out ≤5% fast settings, an accuracy win, or simultaneous speed-and-accuracy dominance.
- Shared-FOM ratios as accuracy-matched fast-arm gains.
- A nonlinear-head advantage: every headline arm solves in a linear bank span.

- Exact sign-upwind equivalence or an error guarantee from rho.

**Verdict:** The corrected numerical selections are reproducible and the stopping rule was applied correctly; the substantive conclusion is a negative held-out result under the registered target. The report largely acknowledges that outcome honestly. Publication should correct the once-opened wording, qualify the unresolved cohort fingerprint mismatch, and retain the distinction between shared-FOM and own-matched speedups. The 129-node fast result supports a qualified speed/accuracy tradeoff, not a successful Table 1 accuracy-and-speed win.
