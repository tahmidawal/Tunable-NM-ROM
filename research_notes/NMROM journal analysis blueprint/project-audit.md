# Project evidence and analysis design

Read-only audit completed November 18, 2026 by `method_analysis_plan`, using the canonical lab log, current manuscript and current reports. No new jobs or numerical reproductions were run.

The [current method](/home/tahmid/Dev/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/worktrees/2026-11-13-manuscript-rebuttal/paper_revision/main.tex:341) has a fixed nonlinear head, $u=b+Gh(z)$, and a separate direct-coefficient ordered span, $u=b+\widehat G_{R'}c$. Runtime prefix choice belongs to the span. The [ordering appendix](/home/tahmid/Dev/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/worktrees/2026-11-13-manuscript-rebuttal/paper_revision/main.tex:1363) gives training-data PCA optimality; it does not establish nested nonlinear heads or monotone rollout accuracy.

The [harder Navier–Stokes manuscript study](/home/tahmid/Dev/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/worktrees/2026-11-13-manuscript-rebuttal/paper_revision/main.tex:640) uses mesh-specific POD banks plus nonlinear heads, so its head evidence is separate from evidence for a neural coordinate bank. Cylinder uses a changed Galerkin rollout, not a demonstration of the complete fixed-test least-squares procedure on that geometry.

The [baseline campaign](/home/tahmid/Dev/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/reports/2026-10-17-full-experiment-plan/final/report.md) includes dimensional sweeps, test-space controls, error-budget and long-time studies. Square-test configurations fail qualification; longer-time and three-dimensional conclusions retain reference/quadrature restrictions. The [improvement campaign](/home/tahmid/Dev/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/reports/2026-10-21-improvement-campaign/final/report.md) includes continuous-POD and stronger full-order controls. The [final closeout](/home/tahmid/Dev/Tunable-NM-ROM-Claude/reports/2026-11-15-experiment-closeout.md) distinguishes primary space-and-time versus secondary space-only quadrature decisions, with a secondary two-dimensional failure. Spectral full-order comparisons are faster on timing-qualified prepared-input panels; no end-to-end ratio follows because corresponding ROM preparation was unmeasured. Neither project-wide speed superiority nor generic neural-bank superiority is established.

Highest-priority new synthesis: pair approximation and dynamics for the same bank; measure frozen-bank tunability; explain weak-test visibility through scaled physical tangent Jacobians; qualify quadrature residual/Jacobian action on reached states; distinguish discrete/continuous operators; separate mesh transfer from input/output complexity; show complete-query matched-accuracy curves. Reuse qualified saved evidence first, and commission only missing controls. New tree embeddings remain separate future work.

A conditional local residual bound could use a common admissible trial-state set, Lipschitz projected residual on a domain including the reference solution, an inverse-Lipschitz constant on that trial set, a uniform quadrature defect, and residual-norm near-optimality of the returned state. A sampled Jacobian spectrum is a diagnostic, not proof of the uniform inverse estimate; LM stationarity is not residual near-optimality. Transient propagation needs a separate stability argument.

Existing quantitative values were deliberately not copied into the new report: publication tables containing project results must be generated from run records. Bibliographic metadata and proposed mathematical formulas do not claim new measurements.
