# Weak-test visibility on the frozen Burgers span

This is a prospective development-only mechanism experiment. No results exist yet. The first block isolates test-space effects for the original discrete sign-upwind operator; it does not establish continuum accuracy or a speed advantage.

## Question and primary hypotheses

For one fixed learned representation and one fixed discrete PDE, do larger smooth weak-test spaces detect reachable field changes missed by smaller spaces, and does that change the evolved field error? H1 concerns the magnitude of physically normalized singular values, near-null directions, and their relationship to subsequent field error. Nondecrease of the smallest singular value when unchanged weighted rows are appended at the **same** state is an algebraic consistency check, not an independent scientific discovery. H2: enlarged tests reduce error against a qualified same-grid full-order trajectory and reduce independently expanded-test residuals. These are local diagnostics, not a uniform inf-sup theorem. H2 may fail because additional tests can alter the least-squares compromise or because representation error dominates. All cases and negative outcomes are retained.

## Frozen first block

- Existing dn256b checkpoint, original 512-function bank and saved rotation; first 128 rotated columns, no neural training or new ordering.
- Burgers 2D, zero Dirichlet boundary, 256 intervals per axis; the physical mass norm is the interior Euclidean norm divided by 256.
- Backward Euler with $\Delta t=0.005$ to $T=0.25$. Exact original FOM sign-upwind advection and five-point diffusion. No analytic-derivative replacement.
- Test prefixes $M\in\{256,512,1024,2048\}$, ordered by the discrete sine-Laplacian eigenvalues, fixed before seeing results. Actual unknown count is 128; there are no free frame coordinates. The historical square control is not a success prerequisite.
- Exact all-node quadrature throughout, including initialization. No sparse quadrature or NNLS fit is used in this diagnostic. All-node quadrature is exact for the declared discrete target and is not claimed to be a mesh-independent online implementation.
- First two previously exposed `dev6` cases, generated from `params_draw(7090702,4)[:2]`. No validation or final-test cohort is opened. If the first block qualifies, a separate registered extension may include the remaining four dev6 cases.
- Shared physical initial field and shared exact linear mass-projection coefficients for all test counts. No reference-state reinitialization after the initial time.

## Numerical controls and eligibility

The primary error reference is the same-grid, same-step FOM. It is a conditional **discrete** reference, not an estimate of the continuum PDE solution. Its Newton relative residual must be below $10^{-10}$ at every step; rerun with tighter nonlinear/linear tolerances and require the maximum trajectory difference divided by the initial-field norm to be below $10^{-8}$. Nonfinite values, exhausted Newton budgets or tighter-reference failures exclude only the affected case from qualified error claims; raw values remain available.

ROM solve levels use normalized first-order stationarity thresholds $10^{-5}$, $10^{-7}$, $10^{-8}$, with a 200-iteration ceiling per step. The normalized stationarity ratio is $\lVert J^T r\rVert/(\lVert J\rVert_F\lVert r\rVert+10^{-300})$. A tiny step or damping limit does not count as convergence. Every reached state is reevaluated. The primary tight/tighter trajectory difference must be below $10^{-6}$ relative to the initial field and below 1% of the measured maximum error; where the measured error is below $10^{-4}$, the absolute $10^{-6}$ criterion controls. Failure leaves that test configuration diagnostic, not qualified.

Production/tight/tighter histories persist: returned coefficients, residuals, stationarity, iteration counts, accepted/rejected proposals, damping, termination reason and elapsed time. The production threshold is preliminary and may fail. Timing is not the primary outcome; warmup, burn-in and one repeated complete solve witness are retained if a timing is discussed. No ratios across jobs or comparisons to unrelated FOM timings are permitted.

An independent full-grid projection using explicit sine sums checks the DST implementation for the minimized and expanded residual and for three deterministic physical tangent directions. Relative residual/action agreement must be below $10^{-10}$, using an absolute $10^{-12}$ allowance for near-zero quantities. A centered finite-difference check at representative reached states must agree with the Jacobian action within $10^{-5}$ after an epsilon ladder; upwind switching is separately recorded. Failures invalidate visibility attribution until repaired and retained.

## Visibility, residual leakage and field diagnostics

Let $B=\partial u/\partial c$ be the full physical field tangent and $W=256^{-2}I$. Diagonalize $B^TWB=V\Lambda V^T$, keep eigenvalues larger than $10^{-12}\lambda_{\max}$, and form $Q=BV\Lambda^{-1/2}$. Save every eigenvalue, retained rank and excluded direction. If rank changes at relative thresholds $10^{-10}$ or $10^{-14}$, report that sensitivity. The tested step sensitivity is $A_M=J_M V\Lambda^{-1/2}$. Save full singular spectra of $J_M$ and $A_M$, smallest/largest singular values, condition numbers, and the singular values after deterministic nonsingular coefficient rescaling. At an unchanged retained tangent rank, physical spectra should remain invariant (relative difference below $10^{-8}$); raw-coordinate spectra need not. A rank change is reported as cutoff sensitivity, not an automatic Jacobian-action failure.

All local spectra are evaluated on the same reference-model states as well as each arm's reached states, so a change of state is not confused with a change of test space. Row scaling is the fixed inverse Helmholtz weight $(1+\Delta t\nu\lambda_j)^{-1}$; mass-normalized sine tests are nested without normalization by $M$. The independent weak-residual observer uses a disjoint shell of the next 1024 smooth sine modes beyond the largest solve space. Both unscaled and scaled residual norms are saved. This observer is independent of the minimized objective, not a statistical held-out cohort.

Per case and time save physical relative $L^2$ error against the discrete reference, best linear projection error, the norm of the actual evolution difference $u_{\rm ROM}-P_Bu_{\rm ref}$, centered-gradient relative error, sign-upwind advection error, full five-point diffusion error, energy, exact enforced zero boundary error and independent weak residual. Verify the corresponding Pythagorean identity for the fixed linear mass projection; do not subtract two error norms or generalize this check to a nonlinear manifold. Derivative/operator errors are diagnostics against the same discrete target; no continuum derivative conclusion follows.

## Decision and stopping rules

Primary observations are all per-case trajectories and worst/median summaries across the fixed cases, never selected favorable times. A useful expanded-test region requires numerical eligibility, no physical-error regression larger than 2% relative on either case, and a reduction in both the maximum physical error and independent residual for at least one case by more than the tight/tighter uncertainty. Otherwise preserve a mixed or negative verdict. Two cases are a pilot, not population-level evidence.

No threshold, operator, reference, test ordering or case set is relaxed to obtain a positive result. A failed control may motivate a dated prospective implementation repair or a new numerical-setting amendment; the failed run remains retained. Initial job limit is 45 minutes on one GPU. Lane ceiling is three allocated GPU-hours, with one active job and parent review before submission and before budget reallocation. Stop on environmental invalidity, persistent qualification failure, absence of a resolvable test-space effect, or ceiling. No automatic training or sealed-cohort expansion follows.

## Plain-language glossary

The bank is a frozen set of learned spatial functions; its span allows arbitrary coefficients. A prefix keeps the first ordered functions. A weak test observes a smooth weighted average of the equation residual. The residual measures failure to satisfy the equation. Quadrature approximates a weighted sum or integral; here every grid node is used. A tangent is a field change reachable by varying model variables. Mass normalization measures that change in physical spatial units. A singular value measures sensitivity along a direction; a small value indicates weak detection. Stationarity means the local objective gradient is small and does not certify a global optimum. A reference is a separately checked comparison trajectory. A development case is previously exposed and may guide design; a sealed test remains unopened. A trajectory is the complete time sequence. An iteration is one solver proposal; a rejection does not update the state. A qualification gate is a predeclared criterion limiting which conclusions are supported.
