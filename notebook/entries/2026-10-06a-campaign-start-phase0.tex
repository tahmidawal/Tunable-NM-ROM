\subsection*{Thesis}
A reduced trial basis that is a \emph{continuous, importance-ordered} function of position,
$u(x)=G(x)c$, gives two properties.
\textbf{Ordered:} one trained model offers an accuracy/cost dial at deployment (width $R'$, head on or off).
\textbf{Continuous:} the nonlinear term is an integral, so it can be hyper-reduced by classical quadrature (tensor Gauss, rank-1 lattices) at points that ignore the mesh, instead of quadrature fitted to snapshots on the mesh.
The paper combines the discarded ICLR material (the dial) with Hari's off-mesh quadrature study.
The ICLR paper will not be submitted.

\subsection*{Literature check: outcome}
None of arXiv 2505.14595 ($\Phi$-ROM, a grid-based contrast), 2507.07830 (meshless SPH projection, no hyper-reduction), 2508.21279 (energy-conservative EQP, a fitted method) or the LiCROM cubature (random mesh vertices, equal weights) pre-empts the claims.
No ROM paper found uses weighted classical quadrature for the reduced residual.
Per contribution:
\begin{itemize}
\item ordered tunable bank: \textbf{novel for ROMs}; credit nested dropout (Rippel et al.~2014), slimmable networks, POD truncation, LiCROM;
\item classical quadrature replacing fitted hyper-reduction: \textbf{novel as a combination}; ingredients partly pre-empted (Monte Carlo/uniform points in Simplicits, SNF-ROM, Weder et al.; lattices in PINN training; fitted off-mesh points in CECM);
\item decoupling from the mesh's $O(h)$ error: \textbf{partly pre-empted} (SNF-ROM has the mechanism); Ingimarson--Rebholz--Iliescu (CMAME 2022) argue the opposite position and must be addressed;
\item rule-family behaviour on tensor sine tests: \textbf{novel in the ROM setting}, as known theory with a new consequence;
\item data-free error indicator: \textbf{partly pre-empted in principle}; call it an indicator, never a certificate.
\end{itemize}
Still to read before submission: arXiv 2607.27471, 2607.10965, 2602.01397, 2504.15657.
