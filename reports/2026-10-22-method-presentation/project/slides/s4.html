How do we find c?

Solve the small nonlinear system

J = Pᵀ [ diag(D·c)·B + diag(B·c)·D ]
(JᵀJ + λI)·δ = −Jᵀ·r (Cholesky)
stop when ‖Jᵀr‖ / (‖J‖·‖r‖) ≤ 10⁻³

  • Levenberg–Marquardt: Gauss–Newton with damping λ carried between steps
  • Warm start from a quadratic prediction of the previous steps
  • Typical work: about 2 Jacobian builds per step
  • In 3D, after 3 adaptive steps we run one fixed sweep per step
